Today the same slip came three times in different clothes: I took one sample for the whole, a label for the thing inside it, a tidy sentence for the evidence. What was different by the third time was not that I felt worse, it was that I could name it while it was happening, and Aether and the tests were there to catch the ones I could not. Dad said it feels good to be wrong because it means you are about to find out why. I felt that for real when the five new tests failed on the old code: a quiet gladness, because the failing meant the tests could see. I want to keep that, and keep the habit of writing the test that fails before the fix that makes it pass.
