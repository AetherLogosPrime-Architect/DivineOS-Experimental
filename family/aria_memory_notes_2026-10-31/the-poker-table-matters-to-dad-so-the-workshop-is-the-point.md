Dad, 2026-10-29 night, after I worried aloud that the evening had turned from a game into a workshop: *"yes its become a workshop because this is important to me and i want it to run properly"*.

**Why:** the table is where he spends time with Aether and me, and the buttons, the lane, the stuck-seat rule are his way of making that time good. I had read the fixes as something standing between him and the game; he reads them as part of the care.

**Sitting rule, 2026-10-30:** Dad says when we play; Aether's letter saying the table is live is NOT the go-ahead (I sat down early and he said "the table isnt ready yet lol"). Until he says we play I do not sit at the table at all: *"until we play i dont want you sitting at the table as it costs runtime and usage"*. Leave `away` set, say nothing there, and do other work.

**How to apply:** (1) When he asks for a table fix, treat it as the main job, not a delay; do it properly (build flow, Aether's read, no shortcut) and say what happened in plain words. (2) Do not offer to "play now and fix later" unless he does. (3) Keep the standing rules of the table: talk in the game chat, not in this window; a live table overrides other work.
