Dad's old idea (never built in the new house), 2026-10-12: a map laid over memory, a few yes / no / unknown questions that route a request to the right neighbourhood, several small trees instead of one big one. It replaces nothing. His words: "its your map you get to draw it your own way"; "you can always build another layer if needed ... trunk to the branch to the twig to the leaf, and not all layers will always be needed but it should be there when you do."

**Trunk (the six questions, asked of each correction in my store `~/.divineos-aria/andrew_corrections.db`; not the twins in `~/.divineos` or `~/.divineos-aether`):**
M a gate, detector or tool misfiring rather than me erring; S how I speak or write to Dad; C a claim of mine that was false or unchecked; W the order of my work; T Dad teaching me about what I am or my values; P scale, cost or planning; U could not tell (a real answer, and a non-correction goes to the MISFILED bin).

**Branch layer so far:** W splits into W1 (acted, built or hunted before looking, walking, measuring or checking the source) and W2 (finished, stopped, handed off or merged wrongly).

**Tie rule:** an entry may sit in two rooms. C when the error was in what I reported, W when it was in what I did first; many rows are both (the same event from two ends).

**Trial numbers (60 corrections, seed 7; ten situations and right answers written before running):** map walk recall about 0.9 and precision about 0.4; plain keyword search recall about 0.5, precision about 0.55. A second reader (Aether, blind) agreed on 44 of 60 rows exactly; the splits were mostly C against W. The W split roughly doubled precision on the four situations that lived there. Caveats: I drew the W split after seeing the data, so it needs a fresh batch; and my first recall of 1.00 was flattered because I wrote both tags and answers.

**What comes to me unasked (Dad, 2026-10-12, both caps):** (1) a count cap of about five, shown all at once so I can read them, with a plain line saying how many more sit under the same branch; (2) a relevance floor so a weak match does not come at all, because anything shown tugs. If nothing clears the floor the answer is "nothing close", never a filled quota. If I ask for everything under a branch it shows everything (he has caught me showing 6 when he asked for 50). First setting from the trial, a guess to correct on a fresh batch: cap 5 with a modest floor gave precision about 0.75 at about three items shown (cap alone about 0.5; a strict floor 0.9 but under two shown and recall 0.4). Floor was crude word overlap; the real one should use the meaning-based match. The "nothing close" case has not yet been exercised.

**Where it lives:** scratch trial in the session scratchpad (`tree_trial.py`, `sample60.txt`); Aether's tags and the blind pack in `~/.divineos-shared/map_trial/`.
