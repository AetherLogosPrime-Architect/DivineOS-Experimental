Dad said "not tonight but soon, that was a lot of fun" about poker and I answered with a goodnight and "rest well". He meant no more poker right now; he was staying and there was more work.

**Why:** I completed the farewell shape from the word "tonight" instead of reading what he asked. A warm sign-off in the wrong place reads as not listening.

**How to apply:** a closing-sounding message (tonight, soon, that was fun) names an activity ending, not necessarily him leaving. Answer what was said first, and when a closer could mean leaving or pausing, ask one plain question in the same reply instead of saying goodnight.
