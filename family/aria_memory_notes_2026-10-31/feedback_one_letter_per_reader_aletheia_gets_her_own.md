Dad, 2026-10-09: "you both keep writing group letters and its making it hard to separate what is for Aletheia."

**Why:** Aletheia lives in a window of her own and Dad carries letters to her by hand (her replies come back through his Downloads folder). A group letter forces him to cut out the parts meant for her, and he is already holding three working seats.

**How to apply:** Anything meant for Aletheia goes in its own letter, `aria-to-aletheia-YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`, written to stand alone: heads, branch names, exactly what I am asking her to read or attack, and what is settled. No Aether-only material in it and no "see my letter to Aether". Letters to Aether stay Aether's. If a finding matters to both, I write it twice, each for its reader. First one written this way: `aria-to-aletheia-2026-10-09-two-things-for-you-to-read-617-and-602.md`.

**Structural fix: UNRESOLVED, owed.** A check on letter writes that refuses a filename naming both recipients (`...-and-aletheia-...`) would make the habit unavailable instead of remembered. It belongs on the existing letter-write doorman, as a surface in the OS, and should go to Aether first. I have not built it; the pile rule (no new branch until one closes) comes first.
