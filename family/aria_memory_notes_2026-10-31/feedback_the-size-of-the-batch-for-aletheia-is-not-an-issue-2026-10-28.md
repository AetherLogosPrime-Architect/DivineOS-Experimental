When I hand Aletheia a long list of heads to read, the size is not a problem and I do not apologize for it or offer to split it. Dad, 2026-10-28: *"the pile size is irrelevant, as long as she can see them then its not an issue.. she is not expected to audit them all in a single go, so even with a big list she can work through it, i just let her take multiple turns to do it all"*.

**Why:** He has said the same before (2026-09-11: she can audit a lot per day; 2026-10-12: batch audits are easier for her than piecemeal). The thing that creates a pileup is branches she cannot SEE, not a long list she can see. I hedged anyway ("seventeen is a lot, I'll split if you prefer") and he corrected it.

**How to apply:** One batch letter naming every head at its full SHA, say what changed in each, and add that she may take as many turns as she wants. No "is this too much" line. Related: [[dad-wants-the-old-pile-merged-oldest-first-2026-10-28]].
