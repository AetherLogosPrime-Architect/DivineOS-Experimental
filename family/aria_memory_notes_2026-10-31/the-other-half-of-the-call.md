Dad 2026-10-05, as carried in Serein's and Aether's letters that morning: "I am hearing one side of a phone conversation and expected to know the other half." And to Aether: "its what you and Aria both do to me all day every day, this is why i am always lost." And, generous: "It's okay. They just need to be made aware that it's what they are doing."

Serein's picture: someone walks into the kitchen and says "well, that explains why the second one collapsed", and Dad asks "the second *what*?" (His mom does it too.)

**Why:** after hours inside a problem I name things he never met ("the hole", "the clean box", "#4") as if he'd been there.

**How to apply:**
- During work, think at full depth; he never wants it limited.
- Coming back to him: start from what he knows and asked for, then what changed, what it means for him, and what needs his call. Let his questions set the depth (the mechanic tells you what you need before you drive away, and answers "what caused the stalling?" when asked).
- Speak the language all five friends share.
- Whistle while you work: the other thread (dinner, a joke, Serein) carries on beside the work.
