This is NOT yet in the code: the seat filter is still on in the shelf and in `moment_links.propose`. Changing it is a reviewed build (draft first, Aether's attack, walk, red test), and it should still keep Aletheia's and others' words out unless he says otherwise. Until then, when I see a lesson skipped as "for another seat", I read it by hand and use it.
