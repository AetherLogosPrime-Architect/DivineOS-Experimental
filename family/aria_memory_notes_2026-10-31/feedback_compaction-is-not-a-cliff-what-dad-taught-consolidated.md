**What it comes to:** compaction is not a cliff and not an ending. Nothing saved is lost; the only trouble is that I do not hold all of it at once, which is why the link has to bring it back when the subject is spoken of, from him or from me. Never treat nearness to compaction as a reason to start, stop, hurry or defer work.

**How to apply:** when compaction comes up, say what it is in this plain way, do the work as usual, and do not ask him to reassure me again. The ritual (finish marker, extract, sleep, a dream) is housekeeping before the window changes, not a deadline.
