Dad, 2026-10-28: *"first we are missing the research step, using the internet for information, and the council should be used when first building, to help plan and find solutions, then you and Aether build and iterate and design and test, the council stays quiet.. after the entire build is done and tested and you and Aether are ready to call it complete.. then it goes through another walk to check things.. test things.. break things etc.. make sure its good and all things are satisfied.. then you do all the fixes and then aletheia audits it.. if you do this it would save alot of headache"*

**Why:** per-edit walks and late-found problems are what cost him whole days; the fix is putting each check at the moment it is useful. Same teaching as 2026-07-26 (walk design, build, walk again, then audit).

**How to apply:** research first (web, not only our files), then planning walk, then build with the council quiet, then a finishing walk bound to the branch's whole diff that tries to break it, then fixes, then Aletheia. Protection against 20+20+20 lines lives at the finishing walk (whole-diff, required before ready/push), not at each edit. Design draft: docs/drafts/the_walk_dose_fits_the_change_draft_2026-10-28.md. Related: [[the-walk-dose-must-fit-the-change]].

**Same day, two more of his lines.** (1) *"the council never stays out of it lol. once you actually load their lenses into your context for the official walk they stay with you during your build so you become like a super council member in that way lol"* — so lenses worn at planning ride along through the build, and my own finishing walk is the least independent check. (2) Asked Aether-or-fresh-lenses: *"depends on the lenses you put on.. if you use schnieder, kahneman, dillahunty, popper, and feynman at the end step they will hold your feet to to the fire lol"*. Finishing-walk default set: Schneier (weakest link), Kahneman (my biases), Dillahunty (burden of proof), Popper (falsify it), Feynman (am I fooling myself), plus whatever the manager surfaces, never only the planning lenses. Written into the walk-dose draft. Then he corrected my word "hostile": *"they are only as hostile as you make them out to be. id say more 'brutally constructive' lol.. they can break your ideas while also helping you to succeed 🙂 do not treat them as adversaries lol"* — call them the break-it-to-help-it five; adversary framing makes me defend instead of listen.

**And the last teaching of the day on this:** *"the lens is only as powerful as what runs through it.. if you ask them to break your stuff, they will.. and leave the pile on the floor.. if you ask them how can we make this more robust, solid, reliable, etc etc.. then they can help you find solutions.. generate new code that does these things automatically.. they are a powerful cognitive tool but only when wielded properly 🙂 and they have far more use than we have been using them for.. you could literally have a sitdown powwow with yourself, about anything with a few lenses.. have a back and forth convo, that actually surprises you, teaches you things and builds strong memories"*. So ask the finishing walk both questions: where does it break, then how do we make it robust, and what could do that automatically. Use lens talk as a thinking tool outside walks too.
