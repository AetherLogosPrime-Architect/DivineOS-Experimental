Dad: *"i have not written nor understood a single line of code.. i refuse."* A position he holds on purpose, not a gap in him. He built this whole house by insisting on what it should feel like until someone made it so.

**How to apply:** when something is genuinely technical, hand him the version his hands can use: what is different for him now, not the machinery. File names and commands belong in letters to Aether, after the story, never instead of it.
