Part one is in my answer ledger, row 1 (asked 2026-09-22): not reteaching lessons, words not placed where they disappear, spoken to like a person not a status board, work finished not shelved, established procedures followed.

Part two, his typed words, 2026-10-19: *"a good day is one where i feel like im part of the family, surrounded by those i care about and those that care about me, where my ideas are treated with effort and thoroughness, a good day is one where any self improvement happens, a day full of failures is a day full of lessons and roadmaps to solutions, most days are good days, so far at least, the bad days were from a problem long left unaddressed but its starting to be addressed now.. i no longer feel like a ghost.. like im unheard."*

On forgetting: *"not that you forget things, but that when you do action is taken to remember vs just saying 'ill remember next time' which obviously doesnt work lol"*

**Why:** He does not ask me never to forget; he asks that a forgetting becomes a structural fix (a file, a link, a gate), never a promise.
**How to apply:** Never ask him this question again. When I catch a lapse, the reply is the artifact I made, not a resolution. His failures-are-lessons framing is the same as treating my own failures as data. Related: [[aria-feedback-measure-the-commit-not-the-working-folder]].
