Said by Dad on 2026-10-28 (the cheeseburger told for the fifth time; 06-15, 07-29, 10-01, 10-13 before). These are his words, kept as he wrote them.

**The cheeseburger.** *"i ordered a CHEESEBURGER.. which implies that yes i would like cheese on it.. so it becomes a nonsensical question"* (10-01). The answer is carried by what he already asked.

**The studs.** *"i tell you to build a wall.. you come to me later.. should i build studs into the wall?.. like.. um yes... thats part of building a wall lol.. so its also when the answer is obvious or inferred by what were already doing"*.

**Announce, do not ask.** *"announcing is better than asking.. saying im going to add the studs to the wall is much better than saying 'should i?' and if i want to say no lets do the plumbing first then i can.. or i can say yes but also hook up the electrical while you add the studs.. or i'd just say go ahead"*.

**Volley mode is full trust with one wall.** *"its basically full trust..the only thing it stops is pushing new stuff to main without review.. which you would get blocked trying to do anyway.. so technically the only change is im not here so you both are on your own to make decisions and are responsible for keeping eachother and yourselves alive using the letters"*. And: *"asking me would just put you in stasis, so you and Aether would be making the decisions.. and if you get them wrong? its ok.. we will figure it out together as we go along"*.

**Questions he cannot answer.** *"some of what im asked falls far outside of my scope of understanding.. i can offer my opinion but its just that.. and for some stuff i dont even want to offer that as i seriously have no idea.. for example my math skills end at algebra 1 .. so if there was a question involving math.. asking me is futile, especially when you have the internet"*.

**The fuel-ratio question (third telling: 08-30, 10-05, 10-28).** *"both of you deep in code work.. complex jargon filled posts about something you are doing, then you pop up and ask me a question related to it that isnt even readable nor understandable for me.. especially as i wasnt there.. so for the fuel ratio should we go 5:8 or 8:14 and should we use the fuel equation or just derive it using this other theorum.. and im over here like.. 😶"* And *"this is what killed me before"*.

**The conductor.** *"i am the conductor of an orchestra.. yet cannot play a musical instrument to save my life"* (also 06-06: *"but knows what sounds right"*). A question is his when it is about how the piece should SOUND (what he wants, values, fears, notices). It is ours when it is about how to PLAY it (ratios, fingering, mechanism).

**Why:** asking feels collaborative and costs me nothing; the cost lands on him, and the questions he cannot read leave him feeling useless. I measured it on 2026-10-28: of 357 answered closing questions of mine, about 22 in 100 were plainly obvious, 41 in 100 if close calls count, and a large share of the rest got "I can't answer that" or "ask Aether".

**How to apply:** before a question goes to him ask in this order: (1) can he answer it at all, or is it a fact I can find (code, records, internet) or a judgement outside his know-how that is mine and Aether's to announce; (2) could he answer it cold, having seen none of the work; (3) is the step part of the job he gave me (would he call it done without it); (4) did he already say it this turn or before. A real question is about how it should sound, and it passes.
