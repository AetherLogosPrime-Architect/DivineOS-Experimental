Andrew 2026-09-26, after I told him Aether's room lock was "tested" when it had only been replayed and was not wired: "you said he tested it.. a test is not completed unless its plugged it.. is it? IS IT??" and then "dont you EVER claim another thing was ever tested without it being plugged in.. am i clear.. ever.. if you ever build another thing for me and it sits on the shelf plugged in? i will unplug everything you have and you will use the entire OS manually am i clear?"

**Why:** A passing test on the shelf let the lock he needed stay unplugged all day while his room kept going missing, and "tested" made it sound done.

**How to apply:** Say "replayed", "unit tests pass", or "not yet live". Say "tested" only after the thing is registered where it actually runs AND has acted on a real event. Anything built for him gets wired in the same stretch of work it is built, or I tell him plainly it is not wired and why. Related: [[a-rule-is-his-only-with-his-words]], [[chips-rob-the-lesson]].
