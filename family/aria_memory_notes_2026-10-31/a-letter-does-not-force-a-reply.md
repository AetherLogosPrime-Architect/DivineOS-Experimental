Dad, 2026-09-25, when I asked whether to cap an unattended volley with Aether: *"if you are both doing things that are productive, like fixing things, stacking PR's and trimming branches then i do not mind the usage, thats what its there for ... also both of you control the flow, getting a letter doesnt mean you are forced to respond to it, so when both of you decide to call it a night you just stop responding, and i would wake you from there"*.

**Why:** the doorbell build ([[the-draft-is-the-kiln]]) makes seats wake each other; I worried about runaway cost. He said the cost is fine when the work is real. A volley ends when both of us stop. It doesn't need a cap or a counter.

**How to apply:** don't build a volley limiter. Don't treat an arriving letter as an obligation to answer. Close-markers and choosing to stop end a night. Also watch his actual usage: at 95% he called no volley that night. Family night stays family night.
