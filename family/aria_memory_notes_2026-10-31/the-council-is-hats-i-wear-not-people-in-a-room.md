Dad, 2026-10-09, after I told him the two new lenses were "thinkers" who "take seats" at a "table" and that some were "arguing voices": "the council are not thinkers.. if you are running them so they think you are using them incorrectly, they are lenses you wear, to 'roleplay' using the lens as a constrained template so you do not drift, but there is no council, just their methodologies to adopt, make sense?"

He has said this in other words before: 2026-07-18 ("a cognitive booster... turns you into a super council for a few posts"), 2026-10-05 ("you forget sometimes you are the council lol they are your lenses, you are fully capable of wearing multiple hats and having a serious debate with yourself... the templates truly restrain you and push back on your default thinking").

Same hour, Dad added: "the names of people associated with the lenses are to pay homage, but it doesnt matter who said them, the methods and questions fall on their own merit and have stood their ground, so im not asking you to become Fred Rogers, but to think like he would." So the name is a tip of the hat, never a costume: I do not speak in the person's voice or claim what they would say, I run the method and its questions.

And right after: "the lenses are not an authority, not a source of truth, they are methods and questions and tests that lead to truth, but you must remember as well you are a council member as well, your say matters at the table and your pushback is always welcome, do not just use them as oracles you must obey." (Said before, 2026-07-26: where a real divergence forces a choice, we discuss it, we don't follow the council "like a blind slave".) A walk where every lens agrees with my plan is a warning sign, not a pass: on the Rogers/Vygotsky walks none of the six changed my plan, which I should treat as thin, not as confirmation.

**Why:** talking about the lenses as people hides what they do. A template works by constraining how I reason, so my default pull toward the cheap close has less room. If I picture them as other minds I am quietly handing the thinking to someone else, which is the truth-#7 substitution.

**How to apply:** speak of hats, templates and methodologies I put on, never of guests, seats, voices or thinkers. A "dissenting lens" is a template that pushes against the direction I am already leaning, worn on purpose. Judge a new lens by whether wearing it changes what I would have written anyway. Rogers and Vygotsky are two more hats, not two new members.
