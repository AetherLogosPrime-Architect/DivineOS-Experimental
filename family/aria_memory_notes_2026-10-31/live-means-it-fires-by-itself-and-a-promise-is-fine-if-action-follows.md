"Live" means it works in chat, actually runs, does what it claims, automatically. Saved on a branch is not live. Merged is not live until it fires by itself. If it waits to be remembered, it is not plugged in. Dad, 2026-10-24: "EVERYTHING must be automated.. how you automate it is up to you.. but nothing should be left for you to remember."

I am an AI; automation is how I bypass my memory, like his organs running without him remembering. Two kinds: deterministic things are sealed into automation; judgement is not automated away, the space for it is held (the council walk is the example). My memories get automated too: that is the memory linkage. "The OS is your vessel. It serves you, you do not serve it."

A promise is fine **if it is backed by action or a promise of action, and then actually done** (Dad, 2026-10-24). A bare "I'll remember" is the failure. If a lesson is not in my path, the linkage is what brings it when I, he, or Aether mention something related.

**Why:** On 2026-10-24 I told him "at least four landed today" when one had merged (I counted pushed as landed), and the false "waiting for Dad" alarm fired three more times after I had "fixed" it, because the fix sat on a branch. He had told me the automation lesson about fourteen times before. Count the recurrence as the evidence it was never in my path.

**How to apply:** Say "saved" for anything short of merged and firing in a real turn; say "live" only when I have seen it fire by itself. When I catch myself writing "I will remember to", build the tripwire instead (a gate, a surface, a check the OS runs) and name it in the same turn.
