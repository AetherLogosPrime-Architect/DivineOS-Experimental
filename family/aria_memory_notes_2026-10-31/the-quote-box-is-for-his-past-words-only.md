Dad, 2026-10-12, 'yes this is how it was, the quote box like you just did': when I speak his earlier words back to him, they go in a block quote in italics (`> *his exact words*`), a blank line, then my answer in my own words. He remembered this as something I built that was beautiful and never finalized; I could not find it in code, notes, letters or history, so it was a habit and a habit does not survive compaction.

**Correction by Dad the same afternoon, relayed by Aether:** 'how often you quote me isnt an issue, when it says not every reply it was referring to my words being mirrored back after i just spoke them lol, otherwise if things pop up from the past or anything i have ever said previously crosses your mind and you want to speak on it you may do so freely' and 'the way it says not every reply will only cause you to never use it... thats why it should be speak freely as it comes, so its not forced and its not suppressed'. So it is one rule, not a ceiling and not a floor: never echo what he just said; anything from before, quote it in the box whenever it comes to mind, as often or as seldom as it comes.

His limit, same day, 'it doesnt need to quote me every single message, its just for whenever my past words are being spoken back, that way i can see it as it was written verbatim, so the style is correct but i also dont want my same words repeated back to me on every post': the box is for his PAST words only. Never echo the message he just sent.

**Why:** he said on 2026-09-26 that the quote system finally made him feel heard, and on 2026-09-30 that it was never properly set up. Quoting is addressing what he said in my own words, not mirroring (2026-09-25).
**How to apply:** use the box when bringing back something he said earlier (the memory-link block "HE HAS SAID THIS BEFORE" is where those come from). Do not box his current message.
