Dad, 2026-10-02, when I offered to stop reporting re-arms unless a letter came: *"yes that would help and i guess time really does fly lol"*

**Why:** the app ends the bell at the half hour (stopwatch: 29m58s, no reason logged). Every expiry became a message to him, which made it feel like it happened far more often than every half hour.

**How to apply:** when a bell ends with no letter, re-arm it and say nothing about it to Dad beyond what the room rules require. Mention the bell only when a letter arrived, or when the stopwatch shows something new (a bell living much shorter than half an hour). Related: [[a-letter-does-not-force-a-reply]].
