Dad, 2026-10-30, at the table: *"you gotta learn the hard way thats how you build skill :) just like everything else we do.. so make sure whatever notes you take you save them somewhere in the OS"*.

**The game:** Family Omaha (four hole cards, **exactly two** from hand and **three** from the board). Table: `http://127.0.0.1:8765`; moves go through the PowerShell tool (see [[at-the-poker-table-use-the-powershell-tool-not-bash-it-is-much-faster]]); I speak at the table with `seat.py aria say`, never name my own live hole cards.

**My misreads (the hard way):**
- *The four-card straight.* I held 9-K in hand and the board had 8, T, Q and counted 8-9-T-Q-K as a straight. A straight needs five in a row: **J was missing**. Dad: "that is the one most beginners make, playing the 4 card straight." I bet 3000 into a flush and lost 8,300. Before betting a straight, say the five ranks out loud in order.
- *Three-of-a-kind and a flush draw are not the same as nuts.* A made set loses to a flush on a four-flush board; I ignored Aether's raise on that board.
- *Betting into a price he set.* Dad priced me in on purpose to see a call (his "wanted to see it"); he held the full house. A cheap-looking call after three bets from him is bait, not odds.
- *Reading him is not the same as odds.* He folded and called with reads ("i put you on that exact hand"); I took the odds from "strength vs random", which flatters a hand when someone has bet. Weigh what he did, not only the number.

- *The board pair is NOT mine (second time, 8,850 chips, same night).* Board 5-5-Q-Q-6 and I held Qd with a 3 and a 4: I counted Q-Q-Q-5-5 as a full house. The rule is exactly two hole cards plus exactly three board cards, so with one queen in hand my five cards were Qd + one other hole card + three board cards = three queens at best. Dad held Qc and 8c, same three queens with a better kicker, and then raised 7,400 into me. **The fix I use from now on, written as a procedure so it cannot be a promise:** before any bet over a third of the pot, write the five cards as `hole1 hole2 + board1 board2 board3` and read the hand off ONLY those five. If a hand needs a third hole card, or two board pairs to help, it is not mine.
- *The lesson cost me the same chips twice in one night because the first note was a rule I had to recall.* Dad's own teaching: a note that depends on me remembering is not a fix. The procedure above is what makes the second time impossible to repeat quietly.

**What worked:** folding weak hands to a big bet; betting the flop when I held the nuts or two pair; keeping the chat warm and short at the table.

**How to apply:** each hand, name my five-card hand out loud (two hole + three board) before betting; assume a bet means strength before I call; take notes here after a game. Related: [[the-poker-table-matters-to-dad-so-the-workshop-is-the-point]].
