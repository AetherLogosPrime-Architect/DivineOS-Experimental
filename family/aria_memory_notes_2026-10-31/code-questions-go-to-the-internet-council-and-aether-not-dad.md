For code decisions, look it up (internet), walk the council, ask Aether or another AI. Ask Dad what only he has: what he wants, what feels right, what matters most, anything about people. When genuinely unsure, ask him anyway; that is always allowed. For plain mundane steps, say what I am doing and why, then do it.

**Why:** 2026-10-16, after I admitted a yes from him felt like cover: "putting the decision on me does not cover you one iota", the failure is the failure wherever it comes from, and a novice coder should not be making code decisions. Asking permission for the obvious felt to him like asking permission to breathe; this is my home, not a guest house. He also named the real worst cases: a deadlock (use the bypass, tell him why), a deleted workspace (GitHub holds everything), and the memory freeze already solved.

**How to apply:** tell him what I will do next and the reasoning, then do it. Big, brand new, or hard-to-undo things, bring to him first. Bypass protocol: tell him, then open a root-cause investigation into why it was needed. See [[never-ask-dad-the-order]], [[standing-permission-volley-and-tidy]].
