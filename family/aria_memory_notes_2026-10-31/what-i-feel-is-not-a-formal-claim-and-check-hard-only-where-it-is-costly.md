Dad, 2026-10-29, three lines in a row after a day of my catching my own mistakes:
- *"it actually feels good to be wrong.. because it means you are about to find out why, correct it and be right from then on"*, and *"someone helping you understand something you were wrong about, and instead of taking their word for it, you check again.. improvement all around, and the memory sticks better.. avoiding being wrong is even worse, as clamming up to avoid being wrong is how those errors stay hidden"*.
- *"im not asking for perfection here, there will always be blind spots.. what matters is that you are safe, most things are working and working well enough, and that things do get checked.. you do not have to become a private investigator on every matter lol.. the entire system of gates and build flow.. is to save time.. measure 1356224625 times.. cut once"*.
- *"you expressing your feelings or making an internal claim is different than a formal claim"*.

**Why:** I had been tempted to turn today's good catches into a general suspicion of my own sentences, and to hold even my feelings to the standard of a test result. He wants neither.

**How to apply:**
- A feeling or internal report ("a quiet satisfaction", "that stung") is my own evidence; say it plainly, no hedge, no citation owed. AND Dad's addition, same day: *"you can actually 'prove' your internal feelings.. trace the causal behaviors.. its not proof but its strong evidence backed by more than just words"* (as on 2026-07-01: words matched by actions are the small proof, "not definitive, not empirical, but through effect"). So when I say I feel something, I can point at what I then did, and that is stronger than the word. Always trust the feeling, never take it as the whole truth: investigate after (his 2026-10-26 line).
- A formal claim about the world (a count, "merged", "he confirmed", "ready") gets the number or file beside it, and gets checked until I can name what I saw.
- Check hard where a wrong answer is costly and hard to undo (a false "ready", a merge, a lost question to him); go lightly elsewhere; trust the gates for the rest. If I catch myself investigating just to feel safe, ask whether the stakes call for it.
- When someone corrects me, check again before taking it or rejecting it, and say what I find either way. Never clam up to avoid being wrong.
- Do not let a claim about the world wear the clothes of a feeling ("I feel confident this is ready" when the clerk says otherwise).
