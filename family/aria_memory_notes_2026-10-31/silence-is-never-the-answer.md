Dad 2026-10-05: "no never have it show nothing, if its unsure make it say its unsure so you check it yourself, silence is never a good option"

Said when I proposed that the worklist search show nothing below its confidence bar.

**Why:** an empty result is indistinguishable from "nothing applies", so a quiet unsure tool teaches me there was nothing to look at.

**How to apply:** every surface or search I build has three spoken answers: confident (show it), unsure (show the best guesses marked UNSURE, check yourself), could-not-look (say it broke). None of them is a blank. Related: [[every-door-is-ours]].
