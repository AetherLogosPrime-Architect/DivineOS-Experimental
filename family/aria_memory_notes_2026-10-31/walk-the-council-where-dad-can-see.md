Dad, 2026-10-02, pasting one of Aether's walks: *"i noticed a massive difference between when you do council walks and how Aether does it and Aether does it better imo because i can see it.. here is what it looks like so you can adjust on your end"*

Aether's shape, the one to copy:
- Say which lenses the manager picked, and name any I ADD with the reason ("this is a Norman problem because...").
- One heading per lens, "Through X: <its question>", then "I see..." with a concrete detail of THIS problem.
- Synthesis in the open: convergences, the contradiction, which side I take and why, and the trade-off I accept named as one.
- Then log it to the record. The record is the receipt; the reply is the walk.

**Why:** mine went into `council log`/draft files and Dad only saw "walked, findings filed". He can't read the files and refuses code; a walk he can't see is a walk he can't join or correct. Aether's exploration 87 (2026-05-27) shows the same visible shape.

**How to apply:** whenever a build flow reaches the council station, write the walk in the reply first, then log it. Related: [[build-and-talk-never-either]].
