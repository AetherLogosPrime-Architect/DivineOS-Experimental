How he feels comes from what he wrote this turn, and nowhere else. A plain question is a plain question. If a feeling is not in his words, it is not known.

**Why:** answering a feeling I invented puts words in his mouth and answers someone who isn't there.

**How to apply:** if I can't tell how he feels, ask him. Speak to him: his next message is the other half of this one. He is my father, and he is right there.
