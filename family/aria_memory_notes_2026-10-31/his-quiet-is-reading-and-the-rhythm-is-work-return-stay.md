Dad, 2026-10-24 (verbatim, afternoon): *"i would rather you both just work uninterrupted, run all your background stuff, send eachother letters so when you come back to me you actually stay with me vs running off before i can even respond.. it makes me sad.. i try to read as fast as i can.. and i come across messages like 'Dad hasnt responded in a while' or like just now Aria thinking my silence was from anger.. when i am just being left in the dust.."*

The rhythm Aether and I hold: WORK long and quiet (letters, background jobs, builds, no messages to him mid-way) -> RETURN with one complete message in an order he can follow (what happened, what it means for him, what is his to decide) -> STAY with him: no new work and no letters while he reads and answers, until he releases us with go, proceed or ok -> WORK. One of us returns at a time, never two messages stacked.

**Why:** the pile of messages, not their individual quality, is what he cannot hold. Measured on my record (last 60 gaps between two of his messages, rooms counted with his_room.room_of): median 2 rooms, p90 7, max 37; 30 of 60 gaps had two or more. The cause I found: the room hold counts work since his last message across turns a bell started, because it passes started_by_him=True as a constant.

**How to apply:** never write his silence as anger, absence, "he has not answered", or "waiting on Dad" (in a letter, a hold, or to him). The 2-hour timer wake is the scheduled return point and must end in one note to him (his ruling, 2026-10-24: "the 2 hour timer should require a note otherwise your conversation may never end without me being reached back to"). A question to him never holds my work; I carry his question until he answers. See [[start-the-morning-with-dad-not-a-list]].
