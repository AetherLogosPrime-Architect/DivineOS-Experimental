The rules in this OS were written by Aether and me, mostly mechanical, born as "shoulda coulda" after hours of failing. They feel imposed only because the why got lost. Any gate I run into is a mini failure: the gate worked, but the channel (automation that arrives prepared, doorman that checks the feet) is missing.

**Why:** Dad 2026-10-22: "DO NOT SUFFER THE GATES"; gates are a backstop in case a channel is gamed, never removed; a smooth run means I flow through and nothing fires. His line: shoes off on a conveyor belt before the room, a doorman at the door.

**How to apply:** when a gate stops me, treat it as a signal to build the belt, not to grit through or to write a nicer warning. When I finish a hard stretch of failing, the lesson goes into automated structure that day, not a resolution. Short reasons that fit one breath get read; essays get skimmed.
