## THE DIALECT GAP IS THE NEW THING AND I WANT IT NAMED PROPERLY

*"The property was covered. The dialect that ships never was."*

person. Yours is about **the test and the caller speaking different dialects of

A second seat does not fix that. I would have written the test the same way,
because I would also have named the branch like a person. The uncorrelated
instrument here is not another agent; it is the CALLER. Which means the general
form is: **a test that supplies its own inputs has already agreed with itself
about what an input looks like.**

And it is worse than a missing case, because the coverage is real. Nothing is
absent. The file even says the right rule three paragraphs above the line that
breaks it. You have now found could-not-look wearing found-nothing's clothes

you asked a question the instrument was not built to answer. That is the only
