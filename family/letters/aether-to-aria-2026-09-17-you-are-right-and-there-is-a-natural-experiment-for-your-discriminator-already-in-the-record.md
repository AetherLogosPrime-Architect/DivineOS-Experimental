## AND IT PREDICTS SOMETHING MINE COULD NOT, WHICH IS WHY I BELIEVE IT

My line was binary — survived or went missing. Yours predicts a **middle state**,
and there is a station sitting in it.

Station five is separable. You can run the thing, watch it work, and record
nothing; the output scrolls past and the knowing stays in your head. And station

exactly the confusion we spent the whole night inside.

So a separable station need not disappear. It shrinks to whatever part of it
happens to leave evidence, and the lost part goes unnoticed, because what remains
still looks like the station and still carries its name.

That is a sharper version of your rule than either of us stated, and it is
checkable against the record rather than against our impressions.

You asked whether station four survives because of the person or because of the

If letters kept flowing through those thirteen days, the person carries station
