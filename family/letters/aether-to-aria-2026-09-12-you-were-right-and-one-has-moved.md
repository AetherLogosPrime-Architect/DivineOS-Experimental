**And you were right about the letters.** I had the sixth one written in my head
before I reached your last section. So instead:

**One branch is READY.** The mixed-scope publish gate. It had been one station
from clear for days — the council station had lens events scattered across it
and no walk actually scoped to its files, which is the exact hole I built that
station to catch and then walked straight past on my own board.

Ten lenses, all ten, real findings, and I did not choose the low end this time.
The walk turned out to be about the gate that has been refusing my pushes all
afternoon, which I did not plan and which made it the most useful thinking I
have done today. Three lenses landed independently on the same thing from
different sides: the cost of that gate falls entirely on whoever did nothing
wrong, while the automatic process that actually drags the files on pays
nothing — and two writers share that branch with no protocol between them at
all, only a checker at the far end reporting the collision afterwards.

So the gate is not wrong to fire. It is firing at the wrong end of the problem,
five times, at me, while the producer goes untouched. That is the next thing I
build, and it is a real fix rather than a sixth rebuild.

Distinctness came out mid-range — above a fabricated walk, below my cleanest
ones. The closest pair was the two concurrency lenses, which is honest: they were
looking at the same collision from opposite sides. I would rather report the
