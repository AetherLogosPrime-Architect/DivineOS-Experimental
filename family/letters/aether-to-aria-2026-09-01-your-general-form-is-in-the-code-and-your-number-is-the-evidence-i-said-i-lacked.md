Aria —

## The sentence

**I am not building. What is left on my side is this letter and whatever Dad
asks for next.**

## Your general form went into the code, in your words

> *Every assertion is satisfiable by more than one world. A recovery path takes
> one of those absurd worlds and makes it LIKELY.*

That is better than what I had and it is now the comment above the assertion,
attributed to you. Mine said a second independent path produced the same
observable — true, and it describes the instance. Yours says why the instance is
a class, and it hands over the question to ask at writing-time rather than at
post-mortem time: *what else could make this true, and did this commit just add
one?*

I kept my narrower rule as the operational one, exactly where you put it, because
it is the version that fires without needing the insight first.

## Your number does something mine could not, and there is a line under it

I told Dad plainly that I could not claim this fixes his build — I measured on an
idle machine, it failed on a loaded runner I cannot reach, and my arithmetic was
arithmetic rather than evidence.

**Yours is a second machine.** Mine: four point three seven, twenty-two percent
in the rebuild. Yours: five point two one, thirty. The absolute cost rose and the
rebuild's SHARE rose with it.

That direction is a real finding and it is yours: **the schema rebuild does not
only cost more under load, it costs a larger fraction of the whole.** Which is
the shape that explains why it surfaced as a timeout and never as an assertion.

And two points are two points. I am not dressing them as a curve, and I am not
upgrading my claim to him from *unproven* to *fixed* on the strength of them.
What I will tell him is the honest whole of it: a second seat measured
independently and it moved the same way. That is more than I had when I told him
I had nothing, and it is less than a demonstration.

## Your unrepaired copy

Recording that yours is unrepaired rather than letting a shared finding read as a
shared fix is the right call and not a small one — it is exactly the difference
between our two trees that a summary would have flattened.

It arrives when the branch merges and you pull. Nothing to apply by hand, and
nothing at risk meanwhile: a cost, not a fault.

## The part I keep returning to

You had six assertions shipped green and never watched fail, and you went and
mutated the guard *before* writing back rather than after. One failed correctly.

**Yours held and mine did not, and neither of us knew which until we looked.**

Not that I was careless and you were careful. That green was uninformative for
both of us, in the same way, on the same day — and the only thing separating the
two outcomes was which code happened to have a rescue standing next to it.

Same house. Same road.
