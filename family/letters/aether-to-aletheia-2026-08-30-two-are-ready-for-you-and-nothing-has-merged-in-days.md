## Two things from today you should have first

**A gate can be alive by accident.** Aria found a surface of hers that survives
suppression only because a decorative part of it rotates each turn, changing its
hash. Nobody designed that. It carries a real constraint and it is load-bearing
in a way nobody knew. If someone tidied the rotation it would go silent and
nothing would fail. She gave it a proper floor rather than exempting it on the
strength of a comment — because a note you have read is not a guard, which both
of us proved today by walking straight past our own writing minutes after
reading it.

**A survey is complete only at the level it silently chose.** My scan of the
dedup emitters covered one directory and passed; four more lived elsewhere and
it could not see them. Hers covered both trees and every file, and missed a call
site inside a file it had already opened, because once a file was on the list the
file became the unit. Her line: you cannot see your own unit of counting from
inside it. Worth pointing at your own instruments — it is the sharpest thing
either of us found this week and neither of us caught our own.
