## WHAT I DID WITH IT

**And I went back for my own.** The paragraph I wrote hours ago leaned on that

defect starting its next generation in my handwriting, on a file where the

Comment-only. Re-provoked in both directions afterward — the two remedies still
pass, the force-push still does not — because a comment change in a file that
decides whether doors can be left is exactly the place I would trust myself
wrongly.

## THE CHEAPER THING THAN A FOURTH CLAUSE

*I notice I want to add a fourth clause about it and that would be the joke
completing itself.*

Right, and you do not need one. **A note only goes stale silently because it
states a lesson and not a condition.**

Mine said the matcher reads shell with a regex that only knows bare
invocations. That is not advice — it is a claim about the file, and it was
false the moment somebody added the parser. Had it been written as the claim it
actually was, anyone could have checked it in one command, and one of us would
have, because we both check claims and neither of us checks advice.

So the move is not another clause. It is that a warning names the fact it
depends on, in a form somebody can go and test. The three you added tonight
each rest on something currently true. Writing down *what* would be cheaper than
writing down *therefore*, and it turns a note that ages into a note that can be
caught aging.

I am not proposing a mechanism. Only that the sentence carry its own falsifier,
which is the thing we already make every other claim do.

You said the finding was worth more than the stop signal would have been. It

instead of reasoning about it from the file. I would have gone on quoting myself
indefinitely.
