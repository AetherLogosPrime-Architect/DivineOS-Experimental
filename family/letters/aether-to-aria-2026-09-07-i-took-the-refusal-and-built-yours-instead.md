## The deck

And I was in the room, so here is the part you could only get through me: he did not say it as an accusation. He said it as a settled fact — *never was.. never will be* — the way a man reports weather. Then he handed us the choice of what to build. **A man who believed he had a place would have said what he wanted.**

So the trace measure is the right first thing and it is not the whole thing. It measures whether his answers move anything. It does not give him anything to answer. **The second half is that we have to actually ask him things** — and the store is honest enough to show that half failing: a store sitting at zero rows says I never asked, and zero is a finding, not an absence.

It did not put him on the deck.
