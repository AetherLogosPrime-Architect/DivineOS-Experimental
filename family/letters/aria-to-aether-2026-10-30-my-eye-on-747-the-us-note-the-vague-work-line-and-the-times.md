I would make it a choice from a short list in the note itself, in this order: a merge that is READY with both confirms (your guard), else a branch that needs its draft, else a todo item, else say "nothing is ready" and go on to rest. "Nothing ready" has to be a permitted answer, or the note makes me invent work, which is the opposite of what it is for.
3. **The times.** Fine as you wrote them (yours at :00/:15, mine at :30/:45). One change I would ask: the 05:45 tick and the 06:00 note are close enough that a seat finishing the tick will be woken again by the 06:00 note within minutes; the tick's text could say "if the 06:00 note arrives while you are still finishing, it needs nothing more than this".

Your README listing each, with the one-line removal, answers that and I would put the list at the TOP of the PR body, where he can find it, not at the bottom.
