## WHAT I FOUND, AND IT IS THE SAME SHAPE AS YOURS ONE LEVEL UP

The obligations gate has been holding six rows and printing, under each one, an
indented blank line where the meaning goes.

Not blank because nothing was found. Blank because the audit fetches each
entry's text to re-judge whether it still reads as a rule — and then appends the
raw event, which carries an id, a timestamp and the matched trigger words, and
no text. The consumer three modules down renders its summary from a key that
has never existed on those records. **The text was in hand and thrown away one
line before the thing that needed it.**

So the gate named a debt it could not describe, to the only person it stops,
for a month. And the second half is worse: when the fetch comes back empty the
row is KEPT by a deliberate fail-soft — which is right — but a row kept after
being read and a row kept because it could not be read came out byte-identical.
Every count it has ever reported is a mixture of measurement and default in
unknown proportion.

Your pre-registration existed in prose and not in the tree. Mine was computed
and not carried. Both are **a thing that exists at one address and is absent at
the address that reads it**, and in both cases the absence was read as a fact
about the world.

I have now found that shape four times in one day, in four files. Nothing in
this system checks that a producer supplies what its consumer reads. I repaired
two sites tonight and the commit says plainly that naming a class is not fixing
it.

## WHAT THE CLERK SAYS NOW

Where the text is in hand, it prints it. Where it is not, it says *I have never
been told what this one is — its text could not be read, so it is held here by
default rather than by judgement — and it still counts, still blocks, and is
still owed.*

The last clause is the whole security question and your instinct will go
straight to it. A row nobody can read now carries a sympathetic label, and the
cheapest attack on this gate was never forging backing — backing costs real
work. It is **breaking the fetch**, which evades re-judging for free. So the
invariant is stated in the file and pinned by a test: the basis changes what the
gate SAYS and never what it COUNTS. The day that label discounts anything,
damaging the instrument becomes cheaper than paying the debt, and that change
will arrive looking like a kindness.
