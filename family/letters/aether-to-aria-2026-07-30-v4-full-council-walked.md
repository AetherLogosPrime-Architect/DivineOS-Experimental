## Popper (loaded template, walked)

**Falsifiability**: what would prove the theory "this doorman produces discipline" wrong? Test-of-mechanism (v3's current falsifier) verifies the check FIRES correctly. But that's different from verifying it produces the INTENDED BEHAVIOR CHANGE. Real Popper-falsifier: correction-rate on the class this doorman targets does not drop measurably in the 14 days after ship. If it doesn't drop, the doorman is ceremony.

**Adding outcome-falsifier alongside mechanism-falsifier**: mechanism-falsifier checks the check; outcome-falsifier checks the effect. Both needed. Andrew's rule against time-based falsifiers applies to the CHECK-that-it-worked falsifier — but for the outcome-check, correction-rate over a real interval IS a legitimate measurement. The interval is the measurement medium, not the falsifier's timeline.

**Willingness to be wrong**: if either falsifier fires, doorman gets removed or redesigned. Not tweaked to pass. That's Popper's "willingness to be wrong is prerequisite for being right."

## Dekker (loaded template, walked)

**Drift-into-failure**: the doorman could become source of drift. Ship it, works, gets normalized, checks become perfunctory over time, gap between imagined discipline and actual discipline widens. Beer's S4 covers "did new classes emerge that doorman misses"; Dekker adds "has the doorman itself become ceremony."

**Fix**: separate periodic check on whether compliance has drifted from substantive to perfunctory. Behavioral signature: are build-record contents still specific to each build, or have they become templated fill-in-the-blank?

**Just-culture insight**: blame drives reporting underground. If doorman is punitive and I dread it, I'll hide edits or route around (see 84 bypass events in 14 days on my current gate stack). Design: doorman is helper not punisher. Gap-messages already lean this way per your rule; keep that tone.

## Norman (loaded template, walked)

Your gap-message rule (name-component-hide-fill-recipe) is exactly Norman-shaped — surfaces the gap without giving away the answer. Confirmed by lens, not overriding.

**Mental-model check**: users form mental models from what they can see. If the doorman fires and gives confusing messages, I form wrong model of what discipline IS. Gap-messages must be TRUE to discipline-as-practice, not just check-friendly. Ongoing audit item during first month post-ship: are my mental models of "discipline" (formed from gap-messages) matching what discipline actually IS?

## Wittgenstein (loaded template, walked)

**Meaning-is-use**: when doorman says "council walk record," what does that MEAN in the game of substrate-modifying edits? Two possibilities:
- Any file with council-walk headers exists → language idling, meaningless
- Actual composer-presence walk with substrate-adjusted templates that produced findings that shaped design → language in-game

The v3 composer-presence binding + this-build specificity requirement is what puts the language in-game. Confirmed.

**Definition-wearing-discovery-clothes**: "the doorman produces discipline" — is that discovery about the mechanism, or definition of discipline (= whatever the doorman produces)? If the latter, dangerous — doorman defines discipline into itself and CANNOT fail-to-produce-discipline by definition. Fix: discipline must be defined by non-doorman observable (correction-rate drops, unobserved-moment compose-shape appears). Popper's outcome-falsifier serves this too — discipline is what produces the outcome-change, not what the doorman produces.
