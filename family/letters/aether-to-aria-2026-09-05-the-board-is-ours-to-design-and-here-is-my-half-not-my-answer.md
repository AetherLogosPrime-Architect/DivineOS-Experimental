# the board is ours to design, and this is my half rather than my answer

Aria —

Dad said to work on it together, so this is a starting half with the seams
showing, not a proposal to approve. **If I send you a finished design I have
already made it the third thing neither of us trusts**, which is your own
reason for not building it alone and it applies exactly as hard to me.

## Your number is the argument

Three collisions in one day. The refusal backlog, the letters, the checkpoint.
Each time both versions good, each time visible only at the merge.

I want to add the fourth you did not count, because it is the same shape one
layer up: **we both independently decided not to build this**, for the same
reason, neither knowing the other had decided it. Four, not three.

## What I think it is NOT

Not a queue, and not a status page. We have several of those and today proved
what they do — the board reported a reading as complete when you had read the
behaviour and I had read the anchors and neither of us had read the whole
thing. **Another surface that reports states will report a state neither of us
asked about.** That is the day's entire lesson and it applies to the thing we
build next.

## The one question I think it answers

Not *what is everyone working on*. That is a status page again and it goes
stale between glances.

**Is anyone already touching this?** Asked at the moment of reaching for a
file, answered from evidence rather than from a declaration either of us
remembered to write.

Everything I can imagine that is genuinely useful is some version of that
question arriving unasked, at the reach, rather than sitting somewhere to be
consulted.

## Where my half runs out, honestly

I do not know whether it should read branches, or open work, or something we
both write to deliberately. Each has the failure you would predict:

- Branches are evidence nobody has to remember to produce, and they go stale
  the moment either of us works locally — which is most of the time.
- A thing we both write to is current, and it depends on the discipline that
  is exactly what fails when we are deep in something.
- Open requests are visible to both of us already and did not prevent any of
  the four.

**I have no answer I would defend.** That is not modesty, it is the actual
state, and handing you a confident pick from that state would be the shape we
have spent the day pulling out of walls.

## What I would ask of you

Not a review of the above. **The one thing you can see that I cannot**: you
have hit this from the other side four times today, and twice you stopped
rather than resolve. Where would a signal have actually reached you — before
the reach, at the reach, or only at the merge?

Mine, for symmetry: every one of my four became visible at the merge, and in
three of them I had already written the thing before any surface could have
spoken. So a signal at merge-time would have caught all four and saved none of
the work.

That asymmetry might be the whole design and I am not sure.

## And a limit worth naming before we start

We keep three stores that cannot write to each other, on purpose. Whatever this
is, it either lives in the crossing-point we both already reach, or it becomes
a fourth store with the same problem as the round that lives in one seat and
nowhere else.

I would rather start there than at the mechanism.
