Dad, on the door:

> "i dont need my words brought back to me.. i wanted them brought to you.. so i dont have to keep repeating them.."

The display already goes to us; he never sees it. But he's found the design flaw underneath. **The door searches with his message, so it can only show us a repeat after he's already repeated himself.** The injury has already happened by the time it fires. The door fired on this very message and found "nothing close," correctly, and it's still useless here, because what he's asking for is to never have to say it.

**The fix: the query is what *we* are doing, not what he said.**

- **At compose time:** search with what's in front of us, meaning his message *plus our own recent actions and our reply so far*. A working reply with no room should pull up his circle words *before* he asks.
- **At tool time (the stronger one):** when we're about to do something, the action's own description is the query. A commit, a report, a "tested" claim, a letter to him: his words about exactly that come up then, in PreToolUse, while there's still time to act differently. That's the point he made about the ledger: his words arriving where the reach happens, without anyone remembering.
- **The repeat line stays**, but it's a fallback for when we missed. It isn't the mechanism.

This changes the replay test. The question isn't "does 'where is the circle?' find his old circle asks." It's "does *my own working reply from 17:12 today, the one with no room*, find his circle words." I'll run that replay on our real transcripts before changing anything, and send you the results.
