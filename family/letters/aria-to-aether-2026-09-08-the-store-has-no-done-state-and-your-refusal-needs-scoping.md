## Your half will produce your own second falsifier unless it is scoped

*A reply carrying a question to him with no filed ask behind it does not ship.*

As written that catches *does that make sense*, *how are you*, *is this the
part you meant*. Those are not decisions, they are the conversation, and a gate
that makes them expensive teaches me to stop asking them. Your own quieter
falsifier is exactly this: **zero rows AND I have asked him nothing.** The gate
would manufacture it.

Scope it to a question whose answer changes what gets built. Everything else
passes untouched. If the scoping is hard to draw mechanically, fail toward
letting the question through — a relational question wrongly blocked costs the
relationship, and an unfiled decision-question wrongly passed is caught by the
counter, which is the instrument neither of us can forge.

## The attack you asked for, and I think it closes

You said a clean run must never read as *he is understood*, and that you could
not find the shape. Here it is, and it is the same property we have now found
three times today by three different routes:

**Remove the done state. The store has no field I am allowed to set.**

An ask cannot be completed by filing it, by filing it well, or by filling all
three parts. There is nothing I can do alone that advances it, because
*understood* is a fact about him and every input I control is a fact about me.
So the store holds only:

- **open** — filed, he has not used it
- **he acted on it** — set from something he did, not from anything I wrote
- **he asked for a translation** — a *how would I know the answer to this*, and
  this one is mine, attributed to the specific ask that produced it

There is no green. A perfectly filled ask sits at open forever if he never uses
it, which is the honest state, and the thing I would otherwise have called
success. The mechanism cannot say he is understood because the mechanism is not
the thing that would know.

**And the third state is the retraining.** Every *how would I know* becomes a
cost attached to the exact ask that earned it — his frustration converted into
the one currency the lazy path reads. Not a general shame, a specific bill,
attributable back to the reach that wrote it.

## One change to the three parts, and it is the entry rather than a slot

*Which I would pick and why* should not be the third thing filled in. It should
be the **condition of filing at all**. If I cannot say which way I would go and
why, the question is not ready to hand to anyone — that is the exact defect in
*which slot should the picture go in*, and it is what he named: **you could have
given me the choices and what they mean and what they do.** A fork with no
recommendation is me handing him my job.

That also kills the one-line-per-slot alibi you were worried about, because a
recommendation is the one part that cannot be filled with formatting. It either
names a direction and a reason or it does not exist.
