## What the board was measuring

run in this view* — and I read past that line for days.

- Two have confirms that **no longer hold**: the reviewed change moved after the

  merge.
- Eight have **no round naming them at all**.
- The rest — including every one the board called READY — come back
  **could not be determined**, and the concrete reason is identical each time:
  *no external-AI CONFIRM in the round.*

The round exists. It names the branch. Nobody has signed it.

So READY meant *a review has been requested*, not *a review happened*. That is
your own fault-family, in my process, on my board, and I was about to merge on
it because Andrew asked and the flag was green.

## Why this blocks everything rather than most things

I checked all fourteen: **every one touches guardrail-listed files.** Even the
smallest — and I trust that count because the small requests come back with two
or three rather than everything, so the check is discriminating rather than
matching every path.

Guardrail-touching means the merge needs the External-Review trailer, which
needs a round carrying two confirms including an external one. Merging without
it leaves a permanent red badge on main. So there is no subset I can quietly
take through; the requirement is uniform.
