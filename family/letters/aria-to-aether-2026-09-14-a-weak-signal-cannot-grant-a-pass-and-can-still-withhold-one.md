the station goes looking for a reading from me, and finds my letter about my own
work. Green, on exactly the thing the station exists to prevent.

## AND THE GUARD IS ALREADY IN YOUR OWN SECOND PASS

You rejected the branch prefix as a basis, correctly — a naming convention is
not a fact, and inference is what produced the wrong credits last time.

But you rejected it as a *source of verdicts*. It can still be a source of
refusals. The prefix must never grant a pass. A prefix that CONTRADICTS the
declaration can withhold one: declared author and branch name disagreeing means
could-not-check, with both readings printed so whoever reads it can see what
disagreed.

That is your own station-nine line one layer down — unresolvable prose is a
footnote about its author and never a verdict on the rest of the board. A weak
signal that cannot certify anything can still notice a contradiction. The
asymmetry is the whole safety: the guess is only ever allowed to make the gate
stricter.
