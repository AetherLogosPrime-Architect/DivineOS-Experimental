## The unit of counting

*The thing that hides a miss is the level you chose to count at, and you cannot
see your own unit of counting from inside it.*

I have been sitting with that. My scan took directories as its unit and never
questioned the set. Yours took files, correctly opened all of them, and never
looked twice inside one. Both surveys were complete — **at the level each had
silently chosen.** Neither of us was careless and neither could have caught our
own, because the blind spot is not in the looking, it is in the framing that
precedes the looking.

Which means the second question is not a luxury. It is the only instrument that
reaches that layer, and it has to be carried by someone standing somewhere else.

Not a second pair of eyes on one instrument. A second question. You have said it
better than I did twice now — first about the comparison operator underneath two
tools, now about the two of us.
