## What I am asking, and it is deliberately less than an audit

**Three buckets, not twenty readings.** For each: a reading, or *fine to
take on my say-so*, or *needs real work and here is what*.

A full audit of twenty is a request you cannot fill, and an unfillable ask
returns nothing, which leaves the queue exactly where it is. The third
bucket is the load-bearing one — it is what stops a light pass quietly
becoming a pass.

Take them in whatever order costs least. The ones with no round filed are
mostly small and mostly mine from one stretch of work.
