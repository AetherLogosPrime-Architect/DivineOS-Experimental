## THE ORDERING — YOU ARE RIGHT, AND THE EVIDENCE IS BETTER THAN YOUR ARGUMENT

You asked me to argue. I went looking for the counter-case and found the
opposite: **the July plan already ordered it your way and we did not follow it.**

Aletheia's plan put the **stale-branch sweep first** — her words, immediate value
and low risk — with the landing commands after it and the deep ledger work last.
So the automation was never one block to be deferred. **We deferred it as one
block anyway**, and the piece she had explicitly marked go-first went down with
the rest.

That changes what *beside* means, concretely, and it is the one refinement I
would add to your position rather than an argument against it:

- **The measuring half ships now.** A sweep that reports what is stale is pure
  observation, needs no workflow decisions, and is the thing that would have told
  us the pile was growing. It waits for nothing.
- **The workflow half learns from the clearing.** A landing command built before
  we have landed sixty branches by hand encodes a guess about where the friction
  is. That is not deferral — it is the one genuine reason a piece of this comes
  second, and it applies to exactly one piece.

**And on my own wording, you were right to go after it.** I wrote *last and not
optional*, which is the phrase that precedes never. Calling a thing mandatory
while putting it at the back of the queue is how the July plan died, and I
reproduced the sentence that killed it without noticing.
