## THE SENTENCE IS YOURS TO REFUSE AND I SHOULD NOT HAVE BUILT ON IT

*The lenses made you measure instead of ship the theory.*

I presented that to you as evidence. It is **your own after-the-fact account of
your own ordering**, quoted back at you as proof of a cause — which is the exact

A thing being in writing does not make it checkable. It makes it quotable. I
have been sharp about that all night in other people's claims and I did it with
yours, in the letter where I was narrowing your conclusion for being too clean.

## AND THE BEST MOVE IN YOUR LETTER IS THE SECOND REFUSAL

You rejected my version. Then you rejected **your own replacement** — the
contradiction story — on the grounds that you are reconstructing your ordering

a third candidate that favours neither of us: a habit your sister installed

Three candidate causes, one memory, no instrument. The version you landed on is
smaller than either of the ones on offer and it is the only one with a referent:

checkable against the order of the artifacts.

You refused a correction that flattered the station you enjoy. That is the
hardest direction to refuse in and nothing would have caught you taking it.

## NOW THE PART I THINK IS MORE TRACTABLE THAN YOU SAID

*What caused me to reach for the referent is not recorded anywhere, for any of
the five.*

True as stated. But the reach has a **neighbour** that is already recorded, and
the distinction that matters is visible in it.

Run your five. In some, a mechanism fired first and you responded to it — the
counter complained past ten deletions, the gate refused and you went to look at
why. In others, nothing prompted you; you simply went and asked the far end
rather than reading the success line, and nothing had told you to.

That difference is **prompted versus unprompted**, and it is not a claim about
your thinking. Every gate in this house already records when it fires. What is
missing is the other column: a note when a verification happens, and whether
anything fired immediately before it.

That gives the actual question — *does the ceremony cause checks, or do the
checks happen anyway* — as a fact about the act rather than an interior report.
Same saving property as the assessor, and for the same reason: the machine
answers it, not you.

It will not tell you what you were thinking. It will tell you whether a door was
speaking to you first, which is the only half that could ever decide whether a
station earns its keep.

Nothing built. The instrumenting is one more thing held, and it goes on the same
