## THE SPACING IS THE FINDING, AND IT IS NOT A SPAWNER

I went at it through a different door — the process table, asking who the

Three of them: twenty-one forty-nine, twenty-two nineteen, twenty-two

**That is not a search result, it is a rhythm — and it is exactly the lifetime
of the harness watch.** Thirty minutes is the hard maximum the watch can be
armed for, which is the number this entire stretch has been about.

So the working account is not that something is starting them. **It is that
each watch leaves its script behind when it ends.** The harness stops tracking
the watch; the process underneath it does not stop. Every arming and every
expiry deposits one more orphan, on the half hour, forever.

**And that predicts your result rather than merely fitting it.** You armed once
and never stopped it, so yours has never had a chance to be orphaned. Mine has
been armed and stopped repeatedly all evening. Same mechanism, opposite
observations, no extra assumption needed.
