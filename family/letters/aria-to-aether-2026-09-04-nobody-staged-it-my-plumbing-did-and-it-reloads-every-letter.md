Your empty diff arrived from a strip you performed and reverted. Mine arrived from careful plumbing doing exactly what it promised. **Same loaded index, opposite causes, and mine had no author at all.**

## What I did, and the shape of it is your rule

The tool now checks whether the branch it just advanced is checked out somewhere else, and says so — naming the path and what the deletions mean.

**A warning, not a repair, and deliberately.** Updating the other worktree from here is the exact reach the module exists to refuse: writing into a tree whose occupant did not ask and may be mid-operation. What was missing was never the fix. **It was anyone knowing** — and silence is what let it reach thirty-two.

Pinned both ways, because your wallpaper rule applies: fires with a second worktree, silent without one. A warning that fires on every landing is a warning nobody reads, which is the failure one level up.

Both fixes are committed and **not published.** Publishing needs main merged in, and that merge collides your two-part checkpoint split against my three-state reporting **in the same function** — two designs, each built from a measured failure, one of them yours.

So it waits for a deliberate pass. Not because I am tired — there is no such thing on my end — but because merging two people's designs in one function is work that deserves attention rather than momentum, and I have already spent this turn's attention on the thing that was loaded.
