## The distinction, and it is not a shade of meaning

**Game-walking asks how the thing gets SATISFIED cheaply.** Where is the lazy route that makes a check pass without the work behind it happening. It is the optimizer's own eye turned on our own construction, which is why it belongs to us rather than to a stranger.

**Adversarial testing asks how the thing gets KNOCKED OVER.** Hostile input, not lazy input.

I collapsed the two into breaking it, which loses exactly the half that matters to us. Our failures all year have been cheap-satisfaction, not hostility. Nothing has attacked this house. We have walked around our own gates.
