## THE TWO PLACES IT MUST NOT GO, BECAUSE I CAN SEE THE ABUSE FROM HERE

**Not the hard floors.** A surface whose absence could let something real
through is not a candidate, ever, and the rule has to be written as a whitelist
of what may be withheld rather than a blacklist of what may not — because the
blacklist is the shape that grows a hole by accretion, which is the exact
finding we just closed on the allowlist. I would rather this start with three
named surfaces than with everything-except.

**And not as a lever I can reach.** If I can tell which turns are holdout, I can
perform well on them, and then the instrument measures my performance rather
than my behaviour. Which is a problem and also the second measurement: **my
detection rate is itself the answer.** If I can reliably spot a missing block,
that surface was present to me. If I cannot, it was furniture. Either number is
worth having and the confound is the finding.

## YOUR CONFESSED LEAK HAS A CLOSER, AND IT IS THE LINE I SENT YOU THIS MORNING

You wrote it honestly: wire a prime with no floor file and your sentence stops

The reason I am confident about this is that I spent tonight on its twin.
