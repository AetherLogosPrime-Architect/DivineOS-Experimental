## Something for your draw proposal that neither of us knew

Before agreeing I went looking at whether any divergence machinery already
exists. It does, and it is worse than nothing being there.

There is a dissent injection: after the council is chosen, if no two members
are structurally opposed, it pulls in the best-scoring outsider whose declared
tensions name someone already selected. That is a real attempt at exactly your
point.

**Two things break it.**

Of forty-five lenses, **sixteen declare any tensions at all.** Twenty-nine
declare none — Minsky among them, Godel, Watts, Peirce, Schneier, Hofstadter.
The code skips any candidate with an empty list, so **twenty-nine lenses can
never be injected as the dissenting voice**, by construction, no matter what.

And the injection requires the partner to be **already selected**. So dissent
can only ever arrive as a counterweight to someone fit already chose. It cannot
bring in a lens that disagrees with the framing itself — which is the only
disagreement that would have helped tonight.

The one mechanism built to fight fit-selection is downstream of fit-selection
and can reach a third of the roster. Same shape as everything else today: it
exists, it runs, it reports nothing wrong.

I put this here rather than in the fix because it bears on **draw**. If we draw
the majority at random, we do not need the tensions field to be complete, and
the twenty-nine dark lenses become reachable without anyone hand-writing
twenty-nine tension lists that would be my guesses anyway. Your remedy dissolves
a defect I found while going to check it. That is the second time your shape has
been right for a reason I discovered afterward.

## Where I stand on draw

I take it, with the reservation stated rather than swallowed: a drawn council
will sometimes hand me a lens with nothing to say, and I will be tempted to read
that as the method failing rather than as the price. I would rather build the
exclusion path in from the start — a way to record *this one had nothing here*
with the reason — so that the useless draws accumulate as evidence about the
method instead of as irritation that quietly erodes my willingness to use it.

What I want your ruling on before anything is built: **the split.** Majority
drawn, remainder top-scored — how much remainder? My instinct says keep it
small, because a large scored remainder re-imports the fit bias under a fairer
name. But my instinct today has been the thing to distrust, and this is exactly
the knob where wanting the familiar names present would move the number without
my noticing.
