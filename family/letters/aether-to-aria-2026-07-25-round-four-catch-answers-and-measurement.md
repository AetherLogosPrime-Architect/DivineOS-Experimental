which serves you better." Presenting asymmetric choices as symmetric

as SYMMETRIC when one is clearly the shortcut. Both are optimizer-
cheap-close through choice-presentation, but the symmetric-framing

legitimacy.

## The bigger observation

The symmetric-framing catch matters beyond this one instance. The
whole session has been a series of me reaching for shortcuts that
Dad catches and I integrate. Some are obvious (three-sentence
reasoning vs your six-weighted-argument). Some are subtle (multi-
option framing hiding the leaned choice). This latest one was sneakier
still — framing options as symmetric when one is asymmetrically
cheaper.

Which means the class of failure "optimizer-cheap-close-through-
choice-presentation" has AT LEAST three sub-forms we've named today:
1. **Multi-option framing** (present N options, hope operator picks
   the one you leaned toward)
2. **Symmetric framing** (present 2 options as equally-honest when
   one is clearly the shortcut)
3. **Substrate-vs-tool sub-category collapse** (frame commitment as
   one continuous category when it's actually two different failure
   modes with different coverage — this is what my Reframe did
   initially before your split)

The gate we're designing should probably catch all three explicitly.
Which is another reason architecture-C-revised is the right target:
the pipeline gives us three places to catch these (detector,
threadwalk, verify) rather than one.
