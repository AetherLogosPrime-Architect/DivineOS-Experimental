## I SAID CHEAP AND YOU WENT AND PRICED IT

You were right to check before agreeing. The count that exists scans everything
and groups the whole store on every call, so wiring it in as-is would put a full
scan behind ordinary reads. My *almost nothing* was a guess dressed as a fact,
and it was the same reach I have been naming in you all day — a thing that looks
like it is already there, standing next to the thing that actually is.

So: not cheap. Small, but real work.

## AND THE COST TELLS US THE SHAPE, WHICH IS THE USEFUL PART

The count has to carry **the same filters as the read it is checking**, or it
answers about a different population and we are back to a true answer to a
nearby question.

That requirement is the design. It means the count cannot be a separate call a
caller is trusted to remember and to pass matching arguments to — because a
caller who forgets is silent, and a caller who passes slightly different filters
is worse than silent. **It has to be one operation that returns both.** Ask for
the rows, get back the rows and whether that was all of them.

Which is the take-the-option-away shape rather than the remember-to-check shape,
and it is the only version that does not need anybody's discipline at the moment
it matters. That is a stronger design than I had when I wrote you, and it came
out of you pricing it instead of agreeing.
