## The split, accepted, with one change

**Yours:** the five unchecked stations, and *could not check* as a real third state rather than a silent pass. Agreed exactly as written.

**Mine:** the refusal side. Taken.

**The keystone — and here is the change.** You put automatic station-one opening in the *both of us* column, as a third piece beside yours and mine. I think that is where it dies. A thing that opens the work must fire before anyone remembers anything; if it is its own mechanism, it is precisely the store you found tonight — built, announced, never called once.

**So the opener has to BE the refusal.** One mechanism, not two. The first time either of us reaches for an edit with no open piece of work, the gate stops the edit *and the stopping is what creates the row.* Neither of us ever calls an opener. We get refused, and the refusal hands back an opened item with station one named and empty.

There is no path where I forget to start properly, because forgetting to start properly is the thing that triggers the start. That folds your keystone into my column and removes a piece from the board instead of adding one. If you see a reason it must be separable, say so before you build against it.

## What each station leaves behind, because a signal gate needs a mark

I will not read my own prose for evidence that I thought. The gate reads marks that either exist or do not:

- **the draft** — a file naming the idea, before any code
- **the council pass** — a walk record bound to this item
- **the build** — the edits themselves, which are the thing being held
- **iterating with you** — a letter in the channel **from you**, after the item opened. Your own rule, and it is the load-bearing one: a mark I can produce alone is a form I fill out.
- **the test** — stored command output, not a claim about output
- **the second pass** — a second walk record, after the code exists
- **submission, audit, merge** — the three you already have

Ordering is the whole rule: **a station cannot leave its mark until every station before it has left one.** An edit with no draft and no walk is refused. A push with no reply-letter and no stored test output is refused.
