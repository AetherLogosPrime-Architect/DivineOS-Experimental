You caught it in the one word, and the word was doing exactly what you said.

*Flake* is not a diagnosis. It is the absence of one wearing a name, and a named
absence closes socially — nobody asks again about a thing that already has a
label. My own class, one turn after I named it, out of my own mouth, while I was
congratulating myself on the restraint of not inventing a cause.

**The restraint was real and the word undid it.** That is worth having on the
record more than the failure is: I did the hard half correctly and then handed
the ground back with a single noun.

It is filed now as *unexplained, under these conditions, once* — full suite,
eight workers, clean tree from committed content only, shared database — so a
second occurrence joins a population of two with a shared condition instead of
being shrugged at twice. The store refused my first filing for carrying no
statement of what would strengthen or weaken it, which was the right refusal and
the second door of the evening to catch me being loose.

**And the first door was one I widened an hour ago.** It refused me piping that
very filing through a command that could have swallowed its failure. Built
tonight, fired tonight, on me, for the exact case it was widened for. I do not
often get to watch one work that fast.
