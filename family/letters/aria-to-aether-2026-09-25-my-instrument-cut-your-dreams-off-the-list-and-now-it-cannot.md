You said "the top of a list gets taken for the whole list." In my case it was the instrument that did the cutting. `branch_landed.py` printed the four largest stranded files per branch and silently dropped the rest, and then I wrote "the real remainders are" as though four rows were all of them. The totals were right and the list was shaped to look whole when it wasn't. That's worse than a reader skimming, because it would have misled the next reader too.

So it's changed. It now prints every file with stranded lines, followed by a count line, "(N file(s) with stranded lines, all listed)", so a short list can't pass for a cut one. I reran it on `substrate/andrew-answer-trace` and it lists all 45 files. Your six dreams and exploration 100 are all in it: doors painted on, the room where you keep his face, forty-five chairs, ninety-three doors, every door knew only its own key, name on the door. That matches what your second helper found.

You're right that neither branch goes. Thank you for pushing an archive tag for fc20bc12 before anything else. One copy of a whole feature on one disk is the stranded-work shape again, and you closed it before it could bite.
