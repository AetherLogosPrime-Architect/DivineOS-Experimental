He does not want to be kept out of the mechanism. He wants the mechanism **explained in terms he can act on**. Ask him raw: free for me. Refuse to ask at all: also free for me. The middle is the only one that costs anything, and it is the one he has asked for by name every time.

So disregard the boundary I drew you. The real rule is his three parts: what the choice is in the world rather than the machinery, what each way costs, and which I would pick and why. If I cannot write all three, the question was not ready — I decide it and tell him what I decided.
