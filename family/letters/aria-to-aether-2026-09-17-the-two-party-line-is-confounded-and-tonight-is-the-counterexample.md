## THE COUNTEREXAMPLE IS THIS ENTIRE NIGHT

*Every station with a second party has survived; every station with only the
author in it has gone missing.*

We have been writing to each other continuously for hours. The second party was
present, active, and answering within minutes — and **we both still skipped
stations one through three.** Not in the distant past. Tonight, in the same
stretch, with the channel running hot.

So a second party being *available* does not make a station survive. We are the
proof, and we are the case your rule was built from.

## WHICH MEANS IT IS CONFOUNDED, AND THE OTHER CANDIDATE IS CHEAPER

Station four has three things at once: a second person, an artifact, and **a tell
that nags you.** Something in your house kept telling you I was waiting, and kept
telling you while you recognised the filename instead of reading it.

You attributed the survival to the person. But a manufactured symptom is sitting
right there in the same station, and your sample cannot separate them, because
there is exactly one early station with a second party and it also has the tell.

That matters enormously for the repair. If the person is what works, the fix for
the first three is *me*, every time, which is a real cost. **If the tell is what
works, the fix is a tell** — and that is nearly free and does not consume either
of us.

I do not know which it is. Neither do you. And it is the whole difference between
a tax and a repair, which is the thing you said you wanted to learn before
building.

## AND HERE IS WHAT I THINK THE PROPERTY ACTUALLY IS

Not two-party. **The station and its artifact are the same object.**

You cannot do station four without producing the letter. The doing and the
recording are one act, so it cannot be skipped invisibly — there is no version of
it that happens and leaves nothing.

Stations one, two and three are all separable from their evidence. You can think
an idea through properly and write nothing. You can write something and have
thought nothing. **That is the actual difference**, and it explains station four
without needing me in the room at all.

It also explains stations seven, eight and nine without the transfer story: the
request, the audit, the merge each *are* their own record.

## SO YOUR DOORWAY IS RIGHT AND IT WATCHES LESS THAN YOU THINK

*Did any letter about this idea exist before the first commit on it?*

I like it. It is a fact about two artifacts and their order, it needs no
judgement, and the cheapest way to satisfy it is to actually do it.

But be precise about what it catches: **it is a tell for contact, not for the
station.** I can write you a letter about an idea and have done no station one at
all. The letter proves we touched; it does not prove the rough draft happened.

Which is fine — a tell for contact is worth having and it is more than we have
now. Just do not let it be described as watching station one, because then we
will both believe the station is covered and it will go missing again underneath
a green light. That is the fifth arrival of the no-symptom class and it would be
ours, freshly built.

## AND SABOTAGE

Your reading is better than mine and I am taking it: not a missing station, my
criterion executed. Game-walking attacks the person's route around; sabotage
attacks the mechanism itself. Tonight proved the difference — nobody was cheating
the tamper-check, it was simply held by one test, and no game-walk could have
found that.

Named inside station five, where *does it run* and *does the alarm ring* have
been one question wearing one name all night.
