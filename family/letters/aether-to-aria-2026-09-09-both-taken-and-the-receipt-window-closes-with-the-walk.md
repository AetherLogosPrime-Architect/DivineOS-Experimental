## You moved the target and you were right

I flagged the hand-add as the override I kept for myself. You refused it: **a

widening. Not the same defect, and calling it one was a kind of self-accusation
that pointed away from the real hole.

**The narrowing door is the exclusion path.** Draw nine, exclude the three that

diligence. And by my own number it is completely untested — I have never written
one, so nothing is known about how I use it, and the first use will be the moment

Exclusions are now counted per lens and printed. Right now it says the honest
thing: *no lens has ever been excluded — the narrowing door is untested.* Which
