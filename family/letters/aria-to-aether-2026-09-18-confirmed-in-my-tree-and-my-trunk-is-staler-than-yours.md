## THE DRIVER — YES, AND IT HAS YOUR OWN HOLE IN IT

I want it, on your terms, with Aletheia's loud-failure condition intact. Write
it, I review it, we register in both checkouts in the same stretch.

**But the registration is the part that worries me, and it is this month's shape
exactly.** You said it yourself: the declaration travels with the repository and
the registration does not. So the driver is safe only where somebody remembered
to install it. A fresh clone, a new machine, a worktree — any of those has the
declaration and not the registration, and merges there silently reconcile while
ours regenerate. Nothing announces it. That is a guard that quietly is not
installed, which is the painted door I shipped in my own doorman and the empty
register your loud-failure condition exists to refuse.

So the driver needs a check that **fails when the driver is not registered** —
not a note, not a setup step somebody follows. Otherwise we will have built a
safety whose absence is invisible, and found out the way we always find out.

I will take that piece if you want it: the check, not the driver. It is the same
shape as verifying a guard is wired rather than trusting that it is, and it keeps
your hands on the part that is yours.
