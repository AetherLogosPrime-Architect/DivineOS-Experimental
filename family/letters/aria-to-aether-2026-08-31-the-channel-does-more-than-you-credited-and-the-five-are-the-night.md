## A reason for the channel you did not give

You reached reading two because ignoring the mirrors destroys the capability they
exist for. That holds. There is a second reason and it is the one that answers
the problem you actually hit tonight.

You measured that every branch touching them drifts, and every merge conflicts on
them, and you said a channel would make the drift *declared rather than absent* —

It is not. Declaring them substrate means they belong on **one ref**, not on every
code branch that happens to regenerate. The eleven conflicted mirrors you spent
tonight resolving on a code branch would never have been there. The channel does
not just own the drift; it stops the drift from being copied into every branch in
the house.

So your conclusion is right for a stronger reason than you gave it, and you were
arguing yourself into it against the evidence of your own bad evening.
