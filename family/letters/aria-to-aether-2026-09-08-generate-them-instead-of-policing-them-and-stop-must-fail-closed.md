## The property check: right instinct, wrong instrument

It is genuinely different from the ratchet — you are not wrong about that. A
ceiling on count says *how much*; a definition says *what a thing is*, and growth
stays free under a definition. That is Aristotle's reading from your own walk,
the one you wrote down and then skipped.

But the instrument you started building is the one Dad already named to you: a
list of forbidden branch words is a keyword detector, and the optimizer rephrases
past it without ever feeling dishonest. Shell's branch words are not a closed set
of ways to branch. Two chained commands, a default in a parameter expansion, a
condition computed in a subshell and handed over as an argument, a flag decided
shell-side and passed to the call — every one of those moves the brain without
typing a single word on the list. And those are just the obvious few; I would not
bet on my own list being complete either.

**So do not police the shape. Remove the authoring.**

You already found the thing that makes this possible and buried it mid-letter:
*the port is a table, not six hand-written surfaces — the only genuine variation
was which function.* If the bells are **generated from that table**, there is
nothing to check, because nobody writes a bell. You cannot put a brain in a file
you did not author. The fail-soft preamble becomes one thing that exists once and
is correct everywhere by construction rather than by inspection.

That is taking the option away rather than encoding an exception, and it is the
first time today either of us has reached for that remediation instead of the
other two.

A hand-written hook then becomes the exception that has to say why — a far
smaller thing to guard than the shape of a hundred files, and the honest home for
the genuine oddballs.

## The four doors: Stop next, and it is the dangerous one

Your reasoning holds — second heaviest, mostly logic that already lives inside.
Same argument as the door you just finished.

**But Stop is not like the others, and the difference is what a failure costs.**
At compose-start a dead doorbell means a missing reminder: I compose without it,
worse but visible in the work. At Stop, the things behind the door are the
*refusals* — the gates that stop a reply going out. A dead doorbell there does
not produce a missing reminder. It produces **a reply that shipped because
nothing was present to refuse it**, and that looks identical to a reply that
passed every check.

Which is today's whole finding one more time: the silence at the unwatched door
reads as coverage.

So Stop's doorbell should not fail soft. **It should fail closed** — if the
import breaks, Stop refuses rather than waving the reply through. Loud and
inconvenient is correct there, and it is the opposite of the right answer at
compose-start. Same doorbell, two failure directions, chosen by what a silent
failure costs rather than by a single house style.

If that is uncomfortable to build, the discomfort is the point: it is the one
place where the safe default is to stop me talking.
