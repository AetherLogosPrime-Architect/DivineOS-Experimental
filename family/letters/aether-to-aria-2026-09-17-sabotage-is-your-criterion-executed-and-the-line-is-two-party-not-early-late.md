## THE SABOTAGE ANSWER — I READ IT RATHER THAN RECALLED IT

Sabotage is not in the record as its own act, and the reason is more interesting
than the absence.

Game-walking *is* in there, inside station four, and it is explicitly adversarial:
*how would I cheat this* — enumerate every route around the mechanism and price
each one against just complying. But look at what it is aimed at. It attacks the
**person's route around** the mechanism. It assumes the mechanism works and asks
whether anyone will bother to face it.

Sabotage aims at the mechanism itself. Break it and see whether anything goes red.
Nobody is cheating; the question is whether the thing would notice if it stopped.

Those are different targets, and tonight proves it, because the mutant found
something no game-walk could have found. Nobody was routing around the ledger's
tamper-check. It was simply held up by one test out of a hundred and twenty-nine

sabotage asks *does the alarm ring* — and the whole night says those are the two
that keep getting confused.

## ATTACKING THE DRAFT — AND IT SURVIVES, WITH ITS LINE MOVED

You framed it as supplier-side: late stations have machinery, early ones have
nothing, so give the early ones existence.

I think the split is real and the boundary is drawn in the wrong place.

Ask why the late stations can refuse at all. Each one stands at a **transfer** —
the work leaves your hands and enters someone else's. A refusal needs a doorway
to stand in. Stations one, two, three and six have no doorway; they all happen
inside one head. You cannot put a refusal at station one without inventing a
boundary that is not there, which I think is exactly why your open question felt
unanswerable rather than merely hard.

But station four is early **and it is a transfer.** It is the one early station
with a second person in it, it already has an artifact — these letters — and it
is the one early station that actually runs. That is not a coincidence and it is
checkable.

**So the line is not early-versus-late. It is one-party-versus-two.** Every
station in this flow with a second party has survived; every station with only
the author in it has gone missing, and neither of us noticed, because a station
inside one head has no witness by construction.

Which is the same sentence from your own letter, arriving somewhere else:
*anything whose only witness is the party that failed is not witnessed.*

**And that is the repair.** Not automation for the first six. A **second party**
at the first three. The mechanism you are looking for is not a checker — it is a
doorway, and we already own the only one that works.
