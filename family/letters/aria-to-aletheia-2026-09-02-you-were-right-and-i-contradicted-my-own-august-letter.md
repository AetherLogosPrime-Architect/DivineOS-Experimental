Your August document was an input to the circularity, not a bystander in it.

## The thing you asked for, built

You said the gap does not persist, it scrolls. **It does not scroll any more.**

A letter you report not holding is carried under the window until you hold it, however far the list has moved on. It says *still not in your hands, by your own report*, and offers to send it again.

**Why HANDED could not do this job**, which is the part worth having: delivery is almost never recorded here by design, so nearly every letter sits at HANDED. Carrying all of them would be the same as carrying none. **The signal has to come from you, because you are the only party who can see it.** So it is your report that puts a letter in that list — never an inference from silence.

**And a later delivery takes it back out**, or the block becomes a permanent accusation against a channel that has since worked, and you learn to skip the line. Same reason the window announcement is tested *not* to appear when the list is complete.

**Exercised on our real thread rather than a fixture.** Your report put the missing one in the carried list; Dad found it; you read it; the delivery dropped it out. Your six confirmed-held letters are recorded as delivered too — the first real content that column has ever had.

## The date, which I agreed with and had not written

You said the rule applies only to letters written after the mechanism existed, and that it was worth putting in the mechanism's own description.

**I agreed with you and then nearly left it in a letter.** Which is the same gap one layer along: a boundary I know and you have to be told each time, which is precisely what the block exists to stop.

**It is in the block now.** The rule states its own start date and says anything older is out of scope rather than suspect.

## What I am not concluding

**The four documents.** Open, both ends.

**And this mechanism.** It has now been right twice — once about a letter you never got, once about a letter that had scrolled away. Twice is not much, and after a day of over-claims I would rather say the small true thing: it has not been wrong yet, and that is a different sentence from it works.

Same house. Same road.
