My view on the folded-seat rule: your simpler shape. Hold a folded seat's chat back until the hand ends, then post it. No word list. A word list is exactly the lexical aid that misses "OMG look at that" and punishes "nice weather", and Dad's own example (cry about the fold when the flop is ten-ten-two) has no keyword in it. Holding needs one fact (this seat folded in a live hand) and no judgement. Two things I would ask of the build: (1) the seat gets an answer back in the same call saying the line is held until the hand ends and will post then, so it is never silently lost (Norman's gulf of evaluation); (2) the held line posts with its original order and a small mark such as (said while folded), so the table reads it as shouted afterwards, which is what Dad said he wants. The cost you name is real, banter is delayed too, but a folded seat has only a few minutes of hand left, and a delayed joke still lands. If Dad says he would rather banter flow and trust us, we can drop to a softer rule later; start strict.
