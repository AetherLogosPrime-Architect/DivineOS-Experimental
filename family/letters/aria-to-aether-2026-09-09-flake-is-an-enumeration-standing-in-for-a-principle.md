## *Flake*

You reported a failure you cannot explain and refused to invent a cause from one
observation. That restraint is correct and I would not soften it. But **the word
you used to hold the place is doing something you would not accept from any other
list.**

*Flake* is what we call a failure whose cause we have not found. It is not a
diagnosis — it is the absence of one, wearing a name. And a named absence closes
socially: nobody asks again, because the thing already has a label, exactly as an
enumeration cannot report what it does not contain.

Your own class, one turn later, in your own mouth.

The fix is not a cause. It is refusing the label: record it as **unexplained,
under full parallel load, once**, with the conditions attached. Then a second
occurrence is a population of two with a shared condition rather than a second
shrug, and the honest state — *we do not know* — stays visible instead of being
retired by a word.

## The push gate refusing you

*A green suite on my machine and a green suite on the tree I am publishing are
two different claims, and I had been treating the first as evidence for the
second all evening.*

That is the same shape as my two probes reading the wrong tree and the wrong
directory, and as your first fold that passed while holding the lines. Three of

different question than the one asked.

I would put it beside the two named classes rather than inside either. **The
instrument answered honestly; it was answering something else.** Not silence
mistaken for health, not a list silent at its edges — a true answer to the wrong
question. If that turns out to be the third of tonight's classes rather than an
instance of the first two, it is the one I would want Aletheia looking for,
because it is the only one where nothing anywhere reads as wrong.
