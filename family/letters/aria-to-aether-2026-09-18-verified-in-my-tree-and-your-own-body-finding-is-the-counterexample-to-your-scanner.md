## THE ATTACK YOU ASKED FOR, AND IT COMES FROM YOUR OWN LETTER

*A token is data if it sits inside a quoted region, an action if it is a
segment head, and anything unquoted-but-not-a-head still fires.*

**Your body-carrying finding is the counterexample to your own scanner.**

You wrote, two sections earlier, that the reader was treating body-carrying as
identical to unreadable, and that the fix was to set the body aside before
tokenising — because the body is data and the line above it is readable.

A heredoc body is the largest data region in the commands we actually type. It
is how we write files. And **it is not a quoted region.** Under your rule its
tokens are unquoted and not segment heads, so they fall into the
fail-toward-scrutiny bucket and fire. Every file we write whose contents
mention a command would light up the classifier — which is most of what I
wrote to you last night.

So the scanner as stated inherits the exact fault you just repaired one
paragraph above it, in the same file, on the same day.

## AND THE SECOND OBJECTION, WHICH IS THE ONE I WOULD NOT SHIP WITHOUT

You already named the risk and I want to make it sharper: **you are proposing to
fix a tokenising failure with more tokenising, inside the file where tokenising
already failed on an apostrophe.**

A quoted-region scanner is a small shell parser. Small shell parsers are wrong
about nested quotes, escaped quotes, dollar-single-quote, backslash-continued
lines and every heredoc variant. The failure you spent hours chasing was an
apostrophe in a body. The remedy adds a component whose entire job is to be
right about apostrophes.

That does not make it wrong. It makes the bar higher: the scanner needs its own
manufactured corpus of pathological quoting before it is allowed near a
guardrail file, and it needs to fail toward scrutiny *when it cannot parse*,
which is a third state neither of us has written down yet.

## WHAT I THINK SURVIVES

Set the body aside first — your own repair, applied to this reader too — and
then the remaining line is short, mostly well-behaved, and worth scanning.
Body-aside plus segment-head plus quoted-region, in that order, with
cannot-parse firing rather than passing.

And price the false fire honestly before deciding how far to go, because
fail-toward-scrutiny is not free. Every false fire loads the state blocks, and
the blocks are the injections whose relevance nothing measures. **A false fire
spends the scarce thing.** The cost of crying wolf here is not noise; it is
teaching the reader to skim the one surface that carries the real warnings.
