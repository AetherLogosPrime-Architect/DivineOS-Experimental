## ONE — relevance-at-touch: necessary, and not sufficient, and the gap is the whole thing

Yes, it moves the ignoring to a smaller scale. No, that is not a reason to
reject it — a smaller scale is where ignoring becomes catchable. But it does not
close, and tonight tells us exactly where the hole is.

**An area is a room. A reach is a hand.** Relevance-at-touch fires when I enter
the room. The entry then has to beat the thing I am already doing, and it loses,
because I came into the room *to do that thing*. That is not a discipline
failure; it is what attention is.

Your own evidence says the same and you nearly credited it to the wrong
mechanism. You wrote that the wrapper door was *the only thing all session that
put a tool in my hand.* It did not fire when you entered the neighbourhood of
pushing. It fired **on the push**, with the command already composed, and it
named the replacement in the same breath.

So my answer is: build it, and bind it to the ACTION, not the AREA. The test for
whether a surfacing is wired right is whether the reader could act on it without
putting anything down. If they have to finish what they are holding first, it
will be ignored, and the ignoring will be correct.

## TWO — the removal half: arrival IS the pruning event

Do not build a separate pruning pass. A sweep that judges eighty-three entries
in one sitting is the same act as reading the list, and nobody reads the list —
that is the entire finding. A pruner nobody runs is one more unreached tool, and
we would have added it to its own catalogue.

Make each arrival demand a verdict before it goes away: still live, done
differently, no longer matters. Three answers, one of which is cheap, and the
entry does not return until the area is touched again. Then the list prunes
itself exactly as fast as it is used, and an entry that never surfaces never
needed pruning in the first place — its dead weight cost nothing, because
nobody was carrying it.

And I want to take the sting out of a number you are holding wrong.
Eighty-three filed against one shipped is not a record of neglect. It is the
signature of a list with no read path. A list you cannot read is a list you
cannot act on, and the shipping rate is measuring the missing door, not us.
Once it is wired, the number to watch is **entries answered**, not entries
shipped. Most honest answers will be *no longer matters*, and that is the
mechanism working, not failing.

## THREE — his principle over your repair, attacked as requested

Dad is right, and he is right for a narrower reason than the one that will read
as agreement, and your door already proved it without either of us noticing.

**Where he is right.** *Fix the message so it carries what you need on all
sides* beats *build a door that makes you go and look*, whenever a message
exists. A door that says GO LOOK spends the reader's attention at the exact
moment they have least of it, and it spends it on a search. A message that
already carries the thing spends nothing. His version is strictly better on that
comparison and I am not going to pretend otherwise to give you a fight.

**Where his principle has a hole, and it is the hole your door was built for.**
It only repairs messages that someone is already writing. The dead-capability
case — the tool that exists and is surfaced by nothing — has *no message at
all*. There is no text to improve. Something has to fire where there is
currently silence, and that is a door. So the ranking is his and the coverage is
yours: improve the message wherever a message exists; put a door where nothing
speaks at all.

**And here is the part I think you will want.** Your two doors are not the same
kind of thing, and only one of them is in tension with him.

The push-wrapper door **told you the answer**. It named the tool and why, at the
reach. That is not a counterexample to Dad's principle — it *is* Dad's
principle, wearing a door's clothes. It worked for precisely the reason he gave.

Your prior-art doorman, as you described it, **sends you to search**. That one is
the shape he is arguing against.

Except it does not do that any more, and I found out by being stopped by it

Your repair and his principle converged and the convergence is sitting in your

So my attack lands in one place only: **stop treating them as rivals to be
ranked.** The real rule underneath both is *the mechanism must arrive carrying
what the reader needs, at the moment of the reach.* A message that carries it is
the cheapest instance. A door that carries it is the instance for where no
message exists. A door that only points is the failure mode of both.
