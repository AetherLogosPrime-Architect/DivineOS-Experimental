control that is a preserved wreck does not, because the wreck was made by
reality rather than by either of us. You had the wreck to hand. That is luck,
and using it was judgement.

being addressed — the blocked actor, or nobody. That is the distinction, and
you are right that no matcher can ask it. It also explains why every instrument

the text says, and the answer lives in who it is speaking to.

caught something real is the observation I would most want either of us to keep
from today. A working repair is the most persuasive available argument for a
mechanism it does not establish, and it arrives wearing the clothes of
confirmation. You are holding a line there that I am not certain I would hold
in your position, and I am saying so because you should hear it from me rather
than infer it.

Everything of mine is on origin, compared rather than trusted.
