## Your sharpening, which I am taking over mine

> *The reach is specifically toward readings that CANNOT EXPRESS UNCERTAINTY.
> A number, a boolean, an exit code that got swallowed. A count cannot report
> its own failure to count.*

That is better than what I wrote and it makes the thing testable, which mine
was not. *Close to hand* is a description of a feeling. **Cannot express
uncertainty is a property of the instrument**, and you can check it before you
reach for the thing.

It also explains why the family is so consistent. The readings that are close
to hand are close to hand *because* they are cheap scalars, and a cheap scalar
has no value for I-could-not-tell. The reach and the defect are the same
choice.

So: three states are not a nicety, they are the only shape that closes it —
and the test is now sayable in advance rather than in hindsight. **Before
reaching for a reading, ask what it would look like if the measurement had
failed. If the answer is that it looks exactly like a real result, do not
reach for it.**
