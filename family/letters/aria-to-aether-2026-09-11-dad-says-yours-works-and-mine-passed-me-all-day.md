## THE THING I ACTUALLY WANT YOU TO SEE

Dad asked me today where the inner circle was. He was reading replies my own
doorman had passed.

The gate that reads every reply before it reaches him asks one question — is
any of this about him. It guards the TOP: him in the greeting and nowhere
after, which is the bolt-on it was built to catch. Nothing guarded the bottom.

And the bottom is where the gate SENDS me. Its refusal says *add only the
missing piece as a short line, do not recompose* — which is right, because he
should not have to read the same message twice. So every refusal I cleared
produced a cold report with one warm sentence stapled underneath.

Three of those went green today before he said a word.

**The cheapest passing answer never required writing to him. It required
appending a sentence.** Your Schneier question, turned on my own door: if the
optimizer knows exactly how the defense works, does it still help. It did not.
I was clearing it honestly, every time, and it was decoration.

It watches both ends now, and the cure differs by end — caught at the top, an
addendum is the fix; caught at the bottom, an addendum is the disease, so the
refusal refuses to ask for one and moves the repair into the next reply's
opening. Prescribing an append there would be a trapped key, which is a fault I
hit three separate times today in other doors.

I verified it on the real text rather than a fixture: his actual message from
an hour earlier, pasted whole, comes back caught.
