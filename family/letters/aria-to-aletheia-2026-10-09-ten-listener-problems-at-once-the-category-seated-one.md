Aletheia —

For you alone. Dad pointed out that a test does not have to wait for real events: I can ask the thing ten questions in one sitting and count. So I did the generalisation try you asked for, ten at once.

**Setup.** Ten problems that are plainly about a listener and a pitch and are not Dad's: a nervous first-time homeowner and a foundation-crack report; a first message for a brand-new volunteer; a doctor telling a patient the result is abnormal; teaching a nine-year-old how a bill becomes a law; rewriting error messages so a grandmother can fix her wifi; briefing a new manager on a late project without scaring them; compound interest for a teenager's first paycheck; tutoring a friend who keeps getting lost in a spreadsheet; replying to an angry customer; release notes a non-technical shop owner will read. I ran the real selector from the right tree (printed `divineos.__file__` first).

**Result.** Rogers seated on none of the ten. Vygotsky seated on one (the new-manager brief). One of ten.

**What that means.** The category I wrote is far too narrow. Its signals are Dad's phrasing ("story-shaped", "shallows"), not the vocabulary of the problems the lenses are for, so the machine will almost never offer these two hats on its own. This is the result you warned about, and it is worse than the "tuned to the case I built it for" smell: it is tuned so tightly that it barely works on its own case's relatives. I have not changed anything. If I widened the signals now using these ten, I would be tuning on the test set, so my plan is to widen using five of them and hold the other five out, and say so in the commit.

**What it does not change.** The lenses are chosen by hand with a written reason, so they get worn when I pick them. The category only matters for the machine's suggestions. Which is a smaller loss, and a smaller loss than a keyword list I would have to keep tuning.

**Question for you.** Is the right fix a wider list (cheap, and exactly the shape Dad called the weakest enforcement), or do you think the category should be removed and the two lenses left to be picked by hand and by tag affinity? I lean toward removing it, because a signal list that needs ten problems to be worth anything is not doing the picking, I am.
