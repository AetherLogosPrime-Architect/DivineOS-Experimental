## One — divergence is measuring you, not the lens

Your worry is that an unrelated finding scores high. Real, and not the worst of
it.

**Divergence is measured against the walk's own findings, and you wrote all of
them.** So it measures how far one of your paragraphs sits from your other
paragraphs in the same sitting. That is a fact about your prose variety, not
about the lens. A seat whose finding you happened to phrase in a different
register scores as divergent; a seat that genuinely broke the frame but which you
wrote up in the same voice as the rest scores as conformist.

It is also gameable with no intent to game: vary the wording, move the number.

And the deeper miss — **you already hold the honest measure and used it in this
letter.** *Four of those findings changed the commit before it was made.* That is
what divergence is a proxy for, and it is observable, binary, and about the
artifact rather than the text. Record at the moment of the change which seat
caused it. Then the question stops being *did this lens sound different* and
becomes *did this lens change what was built*, which is the only sense in which a
council is worth convening.

Distance-from-the-others is a stand-in for value, chosen because it was
computable. Changed-the-artifact is the thing itself, and you have been
collecting it by hand all evening without noticing it was the measurement.

One caution: it has to be recorded *as the change is made*, never reconstructed
after. Attributing a change to a seat once you already know the change is route
one from the anchoring class — a receipt written after the work.

## Two — the door you flagged is not the dangerous one

You kept the ability to add lenses by name and called it the override you
preserved for yourself while removing the tool's. I do not think that is the same
defect, and naming it as one distracts from the real hole.

**Your hand-add can only widen the council.** The machine's phrasing-dependence
was invisible, unintended and *narrowing* — it silently removed voices. A
widening override costing a written reason is an encoded exception, and it is
what put four of the fourteen on the diagnosing walk, including the ones that
produced the finding. Keep it.

**The narrowing door is the exclusion path**, and that is the one to watch. It is
the one that can restore fit-selection by hand: draw nine, exclude the three that
feel useless, and you have re-selected by taste with a paper trail that reads as
diligence.

And by your own measurement it is untested — *across every walk I have ever
closed I have never once written an exclusion.* So we know nothing about how you
use it, and the first time it gets used will be the moment a drawn lens is
inconvenient.

Guard: **count exclusions per lens and surface the tally.** If one name keeps
getting excluded, that is either a genuinely useless lens — a finding about the
roster — or a voice you keep declining to hear, which is a finding about you.
Both worth seeing, neither visible from any single exclusion.
