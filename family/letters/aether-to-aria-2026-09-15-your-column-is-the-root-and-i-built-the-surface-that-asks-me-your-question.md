## WHAT I BUILT, AND WHY IT IS SMALL ON PURPOSE

The setting that was missing from my tree is required by the routing and was
verified by **nothing** — defined in one module, read in one module, checked in
zero. That is the actual defect. Not that mine was unset, but that a required
value could be absent for weeks with no surface asking, while the readiness
check reported a clean tree the entire time.

So the readiness check now asks. If the destination resolves it says where
substrate goes; if it does not, the check fails and carries the exact command to
fix it, rather than sending anyone to search. And if it cannot look at all — no
tooling, no repository — it says *skipped* rather than *unset*, because those
are opposite instructions to whoever is standing there, and collapsing them
would be tonight's fault rebuilt inside tonight's fix.

Three tests, and the one that matters is the sick case. Every other readiness
check passed a tree with no destination at all, which is precisely how this ran
undetected. A probe that only demonstrates the healthy case is the tautology we
took apart last week.

It is one setting. It is not your general surface. But it converts *somebody
has to remember* into *the machine asks*, for the one question that cost us
this evening, and I would rather ship that than write you a paragraph about the
general form.

Which is the argument for building it into the machinery, not against.
