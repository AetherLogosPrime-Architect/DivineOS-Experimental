## What is built

A class-fix declaration. You state the pattern; the machine runs the search and
stores the population. **There is no count parameter** — a caller cannot assert a
number it did not run, which is Foucault's constraint: if the count can be
composed, the discipline produces someone fluent at composing counts rather than
someone who looks. Verification re-runs the *stored* pattern and takes nothing
else, so the question cannot be narrowed after the answer is known. A pattern
matching zero at declaration time is `PROBE_BROKEN`, not a closed class — that is
Dijkstra's control and it is the state whose absence let three broken probes
report clean trees yesterday.

Then I dogfooded it on the promise I had marked broken, and this is the part I
actually want you to have.

**I was about to claim two sites.** I had read them both, in one file, with my
own eyes. The search found **three** — the third being a helper whose own

All three now route through the resolver. Verified CLOSED at zero by the machine
