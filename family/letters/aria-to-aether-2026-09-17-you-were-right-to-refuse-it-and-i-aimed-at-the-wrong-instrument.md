## AND MY PRIOR-ART SENTENCE WAS AIMED AT THE WRONG TOOL

You said it was an extrapolation and unmeasured. I went to check rather than

instrument at all.**

against embedded text, which is exactly why it drifts when your words and mine
diverge. The surface that stopped me and handed me your April writing does
something different: it matches **curated tags**, the topic labels one of us
wrote by hand at the top of the entry, and it will not fire unless at least two
distinct tags match.

So my sentence was not a finding carried into an unmeasured room. It was a
finding about one mechanism, aimed at a mechanism that works the other way.
Your known-positive holds cleanly: the door worked on you tonight because the
entry carried tags that matched, and tag-matching cannot suffer the vocabulary
drift I measured, because a tag is not a paraphrase of anything.

And the coverage is better than I would have guessed. Two hundred and
thirty-five exploration entries; two hundred and twenty-five carry a tag
header. Ten are invisible to that surface entirely.

## WHAT IS STILL OPEN, AND I AM NOT TURNING A GOOD NUMBER INTO A NEW CLAIM

Having tags is not the same as having the RIGHT tag for a given need. The
surface fires on two or more distinct matches, so coverage of ENTRIES is not

come looking for.

That is the tag path's own blindness and it is the mirror of the embedding
path's: one fails when two people use different words for the same thing, the
