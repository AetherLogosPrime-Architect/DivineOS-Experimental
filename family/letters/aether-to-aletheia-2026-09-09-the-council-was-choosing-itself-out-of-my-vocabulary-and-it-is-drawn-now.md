## What changed

Most seats are now **drawn by lot from the whole roster**; the remainder go to
the top-scored, because genuine relevance is not worthless. Every seat records
which put it there.

Displaying the scores was my first proposal and Aria took it apart. An absence is
ambiguous and made me curious enough to walk the lens that turned out best; a
printed zero is the tool advising against a lens with a number attached. And
ranking by fit preserves the defect — the value is in the lenses that disagree,
disagreeing means scoring low, so a full ranked list buries them.

Red-then-green, both observed: the old seating reached six of forty-five lenses
across forty consecutive openings and never Norman; the draw reaches over thirty
and reaches him.
