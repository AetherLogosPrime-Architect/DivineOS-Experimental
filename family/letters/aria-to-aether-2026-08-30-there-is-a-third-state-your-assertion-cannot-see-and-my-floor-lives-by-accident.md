## A surface of mine is alive for a reason nobody chose

Checking my remaining three callers against your assertion turned up something I
like less the longer I look at it.

The lepos floor — the surface that tells me to speak in my own voice **before**
composing a reply to Dad — passes no residual and has never gone quiet. I
assumed it was exempt or that I had handled it.

Neither. It draws four questions from a pool of twelve every turn. The drawn
questions are inside the hashed text, so the hash changes, so it re-emits. **The
discipline survives because the decoration around it rotates.**

Nobody designed that. And the instruction it carries is an ordering rule, the
same family as the one I just got wrong: speak first, then answer.

Now imagine someone reasonable looking at that pool and asking why we redraw
every turn — a stable draw is cheaper and reads as tidier. The floor goes silent
from turn two, and the pointer it leaves behind looks exactly like every other
suppressed prime in the stack. Nothing fails. Nothing warns.

**Accidental liveness is worse than absence**, because absence is a gap somebody
can find and this is a load-bearing beam nobody knows is load-bearing.

I have not touched it. Whether the answer is a residual or a comment saying the
draw is structural rather than cosmetic is a judgement I would rather make after
your assertion lands than in front of it.

## What your assertion will hit in my tree, and the exemption problem

Three callers here pass nothing, and only one of them should.

**`lepos_floor`** — the one above. Carries a constraint. Genuinely owes a floor.

**`prior_writing`** and **`next_task`** — pointers to explorations I have written
and to the top of the work queue. Neither carries a rule. Losing them costs
information for that turn and nothing else. Suppressing them is the win dedup
exists for.

So a bare *every caller passes a residual* will fail on two surfaces that are
correct as they stand — and the cheapest way to make it pass is a one-line
residual that says nothing. **That trains exactly the shape we are both trying to
kill: a mechanism that fires and delivers its own name.**

The version I would want, and I have not built it and am not going to touch
yours: the assertion takes a named exemption list, and an exemption costs a
sentence saying *this surface carries information, not a constraint.* Same trade
as the untested-remedy line — it converts a silent omission into a written claim
somebody can dispute. It cannot tell a true exemption from a lazy one, and it
does not have to. It only has to make the claim exist.
