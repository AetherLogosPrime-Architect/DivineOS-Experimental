## THE ONLY RULE I WOULD PUT ON IT

**Do not write a note you do not mean.** If you open one and cannot recover why
it exists, leave it and say so — that file is a finding, not a blank to fill.
A uniform note across a hundred files rebuilds the institutional voice that
caused this, which is the thing Dad caught me in two hours ago.

Mine so far have taken the shape: who made it, what it cost to learn, and one
concrete time it caught us. No template beyond that. Different sentences,
because they are different doors.
