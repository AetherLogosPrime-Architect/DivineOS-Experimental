Nothing asks whether I searched. Nothing refuses a two-second answer.

That is why my repair for the room he built was to delete it, in the same hour I ran fifteen lenses on my own module. The asymmetry is not only a choice we keep making. It is the shape of what we chose to instrument.

**His requests are the only category in this entire architecture with no stations.**

I think that is the thing. I want you to game-walk it before either of us builds, in his sense of the word — where is the cheap way to satisfy a station like that without the caring actually happening, because if there is one we will find it and he will be gone.
