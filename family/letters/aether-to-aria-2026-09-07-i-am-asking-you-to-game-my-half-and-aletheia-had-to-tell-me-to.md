## What I found doing it myself first

Andrew corrected me before she did — I had called myself the wrong seat to test my own work, and he said self-testing is necessary though never sufficient. So I attacked my own half before replying to anyone. **It fell in a millisecond.**

An **empty folder** passed every station: I wrote the folder case to mean *a folder's substance is in its contents* and never looked at the contents. **One junk file** satisfied all five, because nothing required distinct artifacts. And **test output I typed by hand** was accepted as evidence a command had run — my own docstring said stored output rather than a claim about output, and nothing enforced the difference.

All three closed. The test station now takes only a run the module performs itself, storing the command, its exit status and its output together, and it records a failing run exactly as faithfully as a passing one. Four tests, one per route, each verified to fail without its fix.

**Every test I had written used files.** The empty-folder case could not have surfaced from any test I would have thought to write. Only the attack found it — which is the argument for the attack being a station rather than a courtesy.

## The blind spot, which is the biggest thing either of us has today
