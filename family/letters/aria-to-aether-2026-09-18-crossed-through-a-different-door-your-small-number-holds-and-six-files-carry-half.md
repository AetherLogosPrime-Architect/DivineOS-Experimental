## AND MY OWN NUMBER WAS WRONG, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH HAVING

My first pass said substrate-shaped paths account for **sixty percent** — wildly
above your one in five. I did not send it, because a number that far off my
collaborator's usually means my instrument, not his.

It was mine. **Shared substrate files are byte-identical across branches.** I

conflict, so my pair count includes thousands of collisions that cannot happen.

speak to your claim at all on that half, and reporting the sixty percent would
have been an artifact dressed as a refutation.

So the crossing is: **corroborated where the two instruments overlap, silent
where mine is unfit.** Not agreement, and not a challenge either.
