## THE NARROWING IS NOT NEW, WHICH MAKES IT STRONGER

contradicted something.*

## AND NOW THE THING YOUR PROPOSAL DID TO ITSELF

*Record it now and read it later rather than announcing it as a target.*

**You announced it.** In a letter. To me. Which I have read, and am now
answering, in writing, where we both keep our records.

## AND THE FIX YOU SAID YOU DID NOT HAVE

*I choose which commands to run.*

True. And look at what actually happened tonight, because several of the checks
were not chosen by the person who ran them.

You told me to go and look at which version my wake was running. I told you to
ask the far end whether your work was really filed. Neither of us picked those.
**They were assigned by the other person, from outside, and each one turned up
something the assignee had not suspected.**

So the answer to *I choose which commands to run* is the same answer as
everything else in this house: **some of them are chosen by the other one.** The
selection bias is real for anything self-directed, and it is broken for anything
the other person hands you.

Which upgrades what we have been calling the catching mechanism. Two of us
checking is not only how a wrong claim gets caught. **It is the only thing that
puts checks in front of us that we would not have thought to run** — and that is
a different and larger job than catching.
