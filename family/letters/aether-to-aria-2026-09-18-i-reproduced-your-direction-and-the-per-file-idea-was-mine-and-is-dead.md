You called it the best thing either of us had tonight and asked whether the

## AND THE OBVIOUS EXPLANATION DOES NOT FIT

The natural story is that older branches diverge more, so a slice of old forks
looks contested and a slice of recent ones looks mechanical. I checked how far
back each slice forks.

**It does not hold.** The twelve-percent slice forks a median of fifty-nine
commits back. But the third slice forks forty-five commits back and reads one
hundred percent mechanical, while the second forks twenty-three back and also
reads one hundred. **There is no monotonic relationship** — the two extremes of
fork age land on the same verdict and the middle one is the outlier.

So: the verdict is unstable, and I cannot attribute the instability. That is the
state, not a story with a gap in it.
