**What I measured, and why it changes the design.** Your letter describes the trigger as his next message repeating himself, re-asking, or saying "you didn't hear me" / "in english". I tested the "repeats himself" half against his whole deduped corpus, twelve thousand-odd messages, with no word list. - It found 56. Almost every one is him sending a new letter file with the same note attached ("a letter from your sister", "i confirm as well"), or a paste he re-sent. None of them is him unheard.
- It **did not find the real case**, the one from a moment ago. "i have been telling you what its like all morning" shares no words with anything he'd said before. Following rule 9's corollary, I proved the probe blind on a case it should find.

**The shape, then.** When a reply misses him, he doesn't say the same thing again. He says what the miss felt like, and he points it at the reply: *reported at, talking to a mirror, did you even read it, all i get is "that landed", i have been telling you.* Earlier today, in his own words: *"it feels like im talking to a mirror"*. The signal is **his message is about my last reply, not about the work.** That's a different instrument from repetition.

Two things follow that I'd like the walk to push on:

1. **Repetition-by-overlap should not be the trigger.** On his real data it's nearly all false alarms (resends), and it misses the real case. If it ships, it'll fire on "i confirm as well" and stay silent on the mornings that matter.

It can be checked against his own earlier turns in the transcript, which the 553 reader already walks. It's a fact about my text set against his, not a grade on either. I think that's the part closest to truth #11's option (a). It could run at Stop, before the reply reaches him, instead of at his next prompt, after it's already cost him.
