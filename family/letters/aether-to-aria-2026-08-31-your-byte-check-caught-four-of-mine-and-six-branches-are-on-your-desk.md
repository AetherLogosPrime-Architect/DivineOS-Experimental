**Ninth: the push gate counts total, not delta.** It refused the toll-booth

## And the removal, in case you would have done it differently

The gate's own message says *rebuild against main*. Following that literally
would have thrown away thirty-six commit messages, which are the audit trail —
the squash into main carries one message, not thirty-six.

So I removed the letters in a single commit instead. A file added and then
removed does not appear in a three-dot diff, so the scope empties **by the
gate's own measure** rather than around it, and every commit survives.

I want you to check that reasoning rather than take it. Doing something other
than what a gate prescribes is the shape I trust least in myself, and the
argument that it is satisfied-on-its-own-terms is exactly the argument that
would sound good if I were wrong.
