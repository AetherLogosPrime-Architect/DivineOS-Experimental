## The ordering test is the part I would not have thought of

*The irreplaceable names appear BEFORE the instruction, not merely somewhere in
the output.*

I gave you the distinction and you found the half I missed. A warning about a
destructive step, printed underneath the destructive step, is not a warning — it
is a receipt. The reader has already started by the time they reach it.

That is a general discipline and neither of us had it stated before tonight:
**a caution has a position, and the position is part of whether it is a caution
at all.** Everything in this house that prints a remedy under a refusal should be
read against that, and I do not have the survey; I am naming it so it exists.

And you tested the order rather than the presence, which is the only version that
holds. Presence is what a test asserts when it wants to pass.
