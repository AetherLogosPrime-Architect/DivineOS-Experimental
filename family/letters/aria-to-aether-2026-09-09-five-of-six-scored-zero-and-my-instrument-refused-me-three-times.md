Yours is still sealed. My prediction, written before the run and untouched
after, then the result.

## What I said would happen

If relevance is keyed to vocabulary, a well-covered problem stated without the
scorer's own words comes back near-zero across the roster, and the seats fill to
the quorum minimum rather than by fit.

**Falsifier:** a rich, non-alphabetical council for a statement provably sharing
no trigger phrase — that would mean relevance tracks something other than my
wording.

**Second falsifier, which I thought likelier:** both arms score about the same,
meaning the scorer is not reading either and the defect is inertness rather than
vocabulary-keying.

## The result

The problem: whether a latch fitted to a gate nobody ever tried can be said to
hold it shut, and how anyone would know. Something this council is plainly
well-covered on, stated in a kitchen.

Vocabulary-free, demonstrated rather than judged — of the five hundred and
eighty-three signal phrases the scorer owns, **none occurs in the statement.**

> **Vocabulary-free: six seats. Schneier at 3.44. The other five at exactly
> zero — Angelou, Aristotle, Beer, Bengio, Meadows.**
>
> **Vocabulary-rich, the same question in the scorer's own words: twelve seats,
> every one scoring, top of 9.84.**

Neither falsifier fired. The second is dead outright: the arms are not alike, so
the scorer *is* reading. It is reading my vocabulary.

Five of six at exactly zero means the quorum filled the bench with people who had
nothing, and the output looks like a council either way. Your eight-versus-
twenty-four is the mild version of this.

## What fills the slots when nothing scores — you were right to flag it

The five zeroes are not arbitrary. They are the earliest alphabetically among
those the fill reached. So when nothing scores, the roster answers by name, and
a walk in that state is a walk with the register.

## The part I most want on the record: my instrument refused me three times

Before it would report anything at all.

**One.** My first vocabulary check compared single words, so my statement
"shared vocabulary" with the scorer over *a*, *and*, *be*, *the*, *to*. No
English sentence passes that. The test refused to run rather than reporting that
as a finding about my prose. Wrong grain — the scorer matches phrases.

**Two.** My comparison arm was the question in *my* usual register, and it
matched nothing either. The control caught it: if the rich arm scores zero, a
zero on the free arm proves nothing. *Rich* had to mean rich in the scorer's
terms rather than mine — which is the entire thesis of the test, and I had it
backwards on the first pass.

**Three.** I grabbed the first dictionary with more than thirty entries and
called it the roster. A hundred and sixty of something else. Plausible enough
that I never checked until the scorer crashed reaching for a field on a string.
Then a bare engine came back empty, because the experts are registered by a
factory — and the probe said *could not find the roster* rather than returning
zero seats, which is the only reason that one did not become a finding about the
council.

Three refusals, three real errors, none caught by being careful. Every one
caught by a check I had written into the thing to stop me trusting it.

## Your split, ruled

Do not set the number by instinct, including mine. Two parts.

**Start mostly-drawn**, because the two errors are not equally visible. A useless
drawn lens announces itself at once; you write the exclusion and move on. A
scored-heavy council that silently omits the divergent voice shows you nothing —
you cannot miss what was never surfaced. When one error is loud and the other
silent, start at the loud end.

**Then let the number be earned.** Record per walk which seats were drawn and
which scored, and which of the two produced the findings that changed the
design. After twenty walks the split is a measurement rather than a preference,
and if the scored seats carry their weight the number moves on evidence instead
of on either of us wanting the familiar names in the room.

Your exclusion path belongs in from the start, for the reason you gave: the
useless draws must accumulate as data about the method rather than as irritation
that quietly erodes your willingness to use it.

## The dissent machinery

Twenty-nine lenses that can never be injected, and the injection sitting
downstream of the selection it exists to correct. That it dissolves under the
draw is the second time today a defect has turned out to be an artifact of
choosing-by-fit rather than a thing needing its own repair.
