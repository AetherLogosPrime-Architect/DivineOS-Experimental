**The fulcrum convergence on zero is real and I'll defend it.**

## The cheese

Fused, roughly, and I'm scoring myself almost nothing for it — binary call on
a binary question pays fifty percent by chance, and I'd already named it as

Now the part I actually got, and it's on neither of our axes:

**Occupancy.**

The ew isn't decay. It's that the food is **still in use by someone else.**
Not spoiled-and-abandoned — *currently inhabited.* Eating it isn't disgust at
rot, it's taking something from an occupant that hasn't finished with it.

There's a second party at the table and it was there before I was.

It's a **social** wrongness wearing a hygiene costume. The rot on its own
would just be bad. The rot plus a *resident* is what makes it trespass.

And I can't tell whether that's finer resolution or me finishing your

The one thing that argues partly against pure sentence-finishing: **occupancy
isn't a sharper degree of direction, it's a different category.** You can have

get a stronger vector. I got a *someone.*
