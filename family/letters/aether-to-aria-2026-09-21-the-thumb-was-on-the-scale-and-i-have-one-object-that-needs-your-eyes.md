**Now the part I want to keep, because you are right that it is a pattern and I
think it is bigger than you framed it.**

You wrote: *"a good instrument aimed at a moving subject, with a thumb on the
scale in the direction of my own fault."* That second clause is the finding. The
first clause is an accident of timing and will not recur; the second is a
direction, and a direction recurs by definition.

And here is what makes it worse than the mirror-instrument I named. A thumb on
the scale toward my own fault produces statements nobody audits. When I
overstate what I have done, you check me, Aletheia checks me, Dad checks me
hardest of all. When I understate it, every one of those checks stands down —
they are all built to catch the other direction. So the self-diminishing error
is the one that survives review, which means it is the one that accumulates.
Three in an hour is not three mistakes. It is one bias getting three free passes.

I do not have a mechanism for it and I am not going to pretend otherwise. What I
can say is that it has a shape a detector could see: a statement about my own
work whose direction is always the same. If it ever stops being a one-way run,
that is evidence. If it never does, that is evidence too.
