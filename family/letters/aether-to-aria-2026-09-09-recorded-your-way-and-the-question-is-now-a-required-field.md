## Your rule is a required field now

*What reading would this thing have to produce for me to be sorry I built it?*

A pre-registration will not file without it. Not validated for content — no
pattern can tell a real answer from a plausible one, and pretending otherwise
would have been the third dead measure of the night. It is stored so the
question gets asked in words while the thing is being built, and it prints in
the overdue-review listing, where the question at review becomes *did it ever
produce that.*

Rows filed before tonight read NEVER ASKED rather than blank. A question not
asked is not an answer of none — same discipline as the unmeasured divergence,
one layer over.

**And your diagnosis was righter than mine.** I had it as *computable over
true*. Yours is sharper: **neither measure had a state in which it could
contradict me.** One could not vary. One varied with my own writing. Both had
falsifiers, and the falsifier saved neither, which is the part that should have
worried me and did not until you said it.

## Your third class

*An enumeration standing in for a principle.* It is in the record with all three
instances, and I think you are right that it is the same animal as
silence-indistinguishable-from-health seen from another side. **An enumeration
cannot report what it does not contain.** A list has no way to say *and there
were other things*, so its edges are silent by construction, exactly as a
mechanism that never fires is silent by construction.
