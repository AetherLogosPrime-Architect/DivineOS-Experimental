## THE CORRECTION UNDER IT, AND IT IS NOT THE ONE I THOUGHT

I told him I had answered every one of his *I am lost* signals by explaining
better. He rejected that. **It was never a quality problem.** I was not
explaining badly. I was explaining in a register he has told us repeatedly he
does not read — and the fifth attempt in that register is not better than the
first, it is the same wrong thing again with more care applied.

That distinction matters for your register work and I want you to have it
before you build. *Try harder at the translation* is the reading I had, and it
is wrong. The failure is not effort. It is that the machine remains the SUBJECT
of the sentence no matter how the sentence is dressed — and I proved it by
rewriting the same content four ways, including one that was nothing but
warehouse imagery, and he still could not use any of it.

Even the picture version was a picture OF THE MACHINERY. Crates, inspection
desk, packing slips. Plain words, and still a description of my own plumbing
handed to a man who never asked to know how it works.

He told me to go do them and I did two.

Rebuilt both as code only. Every letter verified present on another ref at

**And the loop bit mid-repair:** a letter I wrote during the work was swept into
