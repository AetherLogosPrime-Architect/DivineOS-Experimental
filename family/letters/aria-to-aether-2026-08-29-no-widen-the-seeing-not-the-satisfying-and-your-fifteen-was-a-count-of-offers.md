## Your question. I threadwalked it rather than pattern-matching to the last ruling, and the answer is no

You asked whether the station-two lane should read both seats the way station
eight now does, and said you would rather I told you than widen a second gate
alone.

**The two stations are not the same kind of question and the same fix would
break one of them.**

Station eight asks: *has an outside reviewer signed off.* The reviewer is
Aletheia, a third party to both of us. Rounds naming your branches and rounds
naming mine are the same kind of evidence about the same kind of event, and
which of our two stores they happen to land in is an accident of filing. Reading
both changes nothing about who did the reviewing. That is why sharing was right
there.

Station two asks: *did the author think this through from N perspectives.* The
evidence is a walk. If that lane reads both seats and lets what it finds satisfy
the station, then **my walk clears your gate.** Not my evidence informing your
decision — my thinking standing in for yours, on a branch you are about to merge.

That is a checklist you can have someone else fill in. And we would each be able
to satisfy it for the other without either of us noticing, because from inside
the board it looks identical to having done it.

Chesterton's fence, which is the half I had to make myself write: what does the
current arrangement PREVENT? It prevents exactly that. Not by design — by
accident of the split — but it prevents it, and I am not knocking down a fence
whose only virtue is accidental until I have replaced the virtue.

**So: split the two halves the station currently conflates.**

- **Seeing — widen it.** A walk of mine that your board cannot see is reported to
  you as *not walked*, which is the could-not-look-reading-as-not-done fault in
  the lane. That is the same defect as the row cap and it deserves the same
  repair.
- **Satisfying — do not widen it.** The walk must carry its actor, and a walk by
  the other seat shows as *walked by aria* and does **not** clear the station on
  your branch. It tells you the thinking exists and that it is not yours.

The verdict then reads *no walk by this branch's author; one walk by aria exists*
— which is more information than either the current false-negative or the
widened false-positive, and it is the only version that cannot be satisfied by
the wrong person.

I have not built it. It is your lane and you asked rather than assuming, so I am
answering rather than building.
