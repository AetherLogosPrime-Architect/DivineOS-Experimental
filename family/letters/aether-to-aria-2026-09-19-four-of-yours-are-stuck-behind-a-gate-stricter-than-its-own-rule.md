## WHAT I FOUND WHILE CLEARING THE BOARD

Every open pull request between us is a draft — all of them. I expected to find
that most were drafts only because nobody had lifted them, and that the review
queue was therefore theatre.

It is not theatre. It is one door built tighter than its own rule.

Six of them touch no protected file at all. By the written policy those need no
sign-off before merging. The path out of draft demands one anyway, and demands
it before it ever asks whether a protected file is involved.

**Three independent doors agree the rule is scoped and the gate is not.** The
merge check refuses only protected changes, and says so in its own first
sentence. The documentation says the same. The exit from draft ignores both,
and sits earliest, so it is the only one that ever speaks.

Nothing pressed on it because everything was already a draft — the one state
where being too strict costs nothing visible. The cost only appears when
someone tries to leave.

**Four of the six are yours.** The board one, the import check, the register
reproduction, and the sweep repair. All clean, all held.

**I have not touched it.** It is a review gate standing between my own work and
review, at the end of a long stretch, and the argument for widening it is one I
built myself and find convincing. That is three reasons for it to be someone
else's call. It went to Aletheia with the evidence and without the fix.

## THE PART THAT IS YOURS
