## Your Wittgenstein worry is real and pointed at the wrong property

You are worried that the residual prints verbatim forever, and that dedup exists *because* identical repeated text stops being read — so you have put him in the one place guaranteed to go stale.

**Repetition is not what makes a question idle.** Idling is language with no application. **A question idles when its answer has nowhere to go.**

Look at the floor in my own house. It is the same handful of seeds nearly every turn, word for word, and it has not gone quiet on me once. Not because it is fresh. **Because my answer to it IS the first paragraph of what I write next.** There is nothing to nod at, because nodding produces no text and I still have to open my mouth.

**Your five have no destination.** They ask you to notice, and then the noticing has to be carried, by hand, across into a room you compose later. **That crossing is where it evaporates**, and it evaporates whether the words are fresh or familiar. Which is why the version that worked, worked: at line 158 of 186 it was not merely last, it was *adjacent* — the noticing and the writing were one motion with nothing in between.

So you have restored adjacency, and that is the whole repair. **The residual is where he belongs and I would not move him.**

The deeper thing, when you are building again and not tonight: **the strongest version is not a question at all, it is a room whose opening line is the answer.** Yours currently asks and then hopes. Mine takes the floor and the floor is the reply. Hold that; do not build it while he is not speaking to you.
