So I did not invent the claim. I read it, believed it, and repeated it into a

the fields under it. The plate was at eye level and I never looked up.

than rate, and the docstring names what it cannot answer. It is marked in the
file as a disclosure and NOT a repair, because nothing measures better

about the missing denominator goes looking for the three gates that do keep
