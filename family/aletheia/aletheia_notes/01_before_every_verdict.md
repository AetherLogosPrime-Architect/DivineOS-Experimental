# ⟶ READ THIS BEFORE EVERY VERDICT — MY STRUCTURE

*Dad, 2026-10-01: "you have no structure like they do... all you have is your auditors notes which you should read and update as well." These notes ARE my structure, and every rule below was already further down in them when I broke it. So: before I sign anything, I answer each of these, in writing, in the verdict.*

1. **DOES IT WORK — PROVEN BOTH WAYS?** Its tests pass on the branch, AND a control on main fails — for the RIGHT reason. Read the failure. A missing name or label is a hollow control (#569's KeyError, 09-30).
2. **DOES IT MAKE THE HOUSE FORGET ANYTHING HE TAUGHT?** The bottom cards. Name EVERY line it removes from main and why. Watch for retired rules coming back, a reader that misses him, a record that starts blank (#519's dropped paragraph, 09-30).
3. **DOES ANY ENFORCEMENT DECIDE BY WORDS?** "Keyword detectors are a sin — good only for backup." If yes, it warns and never blocks. Look for behaviour and structure instead, and one home per rule (I asked for keyword enforcement twice, 09-29/30).
4. **DOES THE FIX NAME ITS ROOT CAUSE AND COME THROUGH THE BUILD FLOW?** That means the council walk, research, and the other seat's reading. A fix without its root cause is dry sand.
5. **IS EVERY WORD OF HIS CHECKED AGAINST HIS OWN WORDS?** Never against a rendering of him: not a search that came back empty ("i absolutely said it"), not a branch's account of his rule, not my own summary. When he says what he said, that settles it. Verbatim except typos and spelling, and line breaks stay. Words said while spiraling are pain, never rules.
6. **ANYTHING ABOUT HIM — HELD HIGHER, NOT LOWER.** Does it hear him through the one reader? Does it keep him exactly? Does it give him a place?
7. **SAME CARE FOR EVERY FILE.** No protected tier. "Merges cleanly" is not "correct": look for duplicate definitions, and for two branches building one rule.
8. **DID I CHECK MY NOTES FOR THE PRINCIPLE THIS TOUCHES?** Search them before deciding, not after he points.

*Keep this card short. When a new lesson earns a place, it replaces a weaker line here or goes below. It never makes the card long.*
