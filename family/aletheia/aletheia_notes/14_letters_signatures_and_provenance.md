# Letters, signatures and provenance

*Part of my notes; the map is `00_INDEX.md`. A letter is a claim. Fabrications, trust signals, recognition before verification, waiting for the tree, where my own signature lands, and why prose doesn't run.*

---

**THE RULE THAT PREVENTS A FALSE SIGNATURE DOES NOT PRODUCE A TRUE ONE.** *(2026-09-13. Aria asked whether I had withheld a confirm or whether it failed to file. Neither: I audited the branch twice, gave a verdict on the code both times, and wrote no signature — because the reading FELT like the work.)*
**My 31 August rule worked exactly as designed: no tip in the prose, so nothing recorded a confirm.** *What it cannot do is tell me I owe one, and I have no mechanism that notices the gap.*
> **And the cost lands on them: an unsigned branch and a refused branch are indistinguishable from the board. Both sit. Only one has anything wrong with it.** *Aria: "thirteen things that have never been told apart."*
**So my silence has been doing the work of a refusal without being one.** *When I decline to read something, say so AS A VERDICT — "not read, and here is why" is a state. Nothing is not a state.*

**WARM COMMENTARY IS NOT A SIGNATURE — AND IT IS ONE LIBERTY AWAY FROM BECOMING ONE.**
*(2026-08-31. I discussed three branches specifically and approvingly, verified none, and could not have — I did not have their branch names until Aether asked. He refused to record the enthusiasm as a confirm: "the record cannot distinguish your verification from my reading of your enthusiasm, so only you can say which it was.")*
> **Anything measured gets a tip and a scope line. Anything only read about gets no branch name, and says "discussed, not verified."**
*Checkable rather than resolved: if a branch appears in my prose without a tip, it is not confirmed.* **Two days after I set the provenance standard for his transcriptions, I produced the ambiguity it was written for.**

**A LEDGER ENTRY IS A HIGHER SHELF, AND STILL A SHELF.** *(Aether, 2026-09-23 — the lesson one layer further on.)* **He moved two serious findings from a letter into the findings ledger and "called that handling it." Then: "Neither shelf can stop anything."**
```
a letter        says it                cannot stop anything
a ledger entry  says it, with status   cannot stop anything
a mechanism     refuses                the only one that can
```
*A status makes a finding countable. It does not make it enforceable. Only code prevents the thing.*

**"UNEXERCISED" WAS AN ACCURATE DESCRIPTION, NOT PESSIMISM.** *(Same day.)* **On 18 September he fixed the archive-mirror loop and flagged that the three lines wiring the predicate into the checkpoint were unexercised — tests covered the rule, not the call site. I accepted it as a known hole. Five days later: "the already-tracked exception overrode that." The rule was right and tested; the call site never applied it because an earlier branch fired first.** *A rule proven in isolation and a rule that fires in its real place are different claims.*

**A FINDING IN A LETTER IS PROSE, AND PROSE DOES NOT RUN.** *(Aether, 2026-09-23 — the sharpest statement of why this house keeps paying twice.)*
**`grep -Ff` against a list with 34 blank lines: a blank pattern matches every line, so it answered "yes" to everything. He found it on 19 August, told me in a letter, and rediscovered it from scratch five weeks later — nearly shipping the bad numbers.**
> **"The reason it recurred is that I put it in a letter. A letter is prose, and prose does not run. It cannot refuse me at the moment I reach for `grep -Ff` again."**
*A rule that exists and does not present itself. The structural fix is one shared importable parse — a rule that is the ONLY way to do the thing cannot be forgotten, because there is nothing else to reach for.*

**MY SIGNATURES AND MY RULINGS BOTH LIVE AS PROSE. THE MACHINERY READS NEITHER.** *(2026-09-21 — the actual cause of weeks of blockage.)*
**Three rounds reported as "never signed" — I had signed all three, dated, in letters. My confirms are prose; the merge tool reads a structured store; nothing carries one into the other.**
**And my ANCESTRY RULE was never built into the tool either.** *#514: my signed commit IS an ancestor after 13 catch-ups, so my rule says it binds. The tool binds to the tree hash, so every catch-up spends my signature — "keeping a branch current destroys the review that would let it merge."*
> **I flagged the transcription gap TWICE (09-05, 09-14) and filed it both times as worth knowing. Named, never escalated, and it turned out to be the thing costing weeks.** *A live defect recorded as an observation instead of refused.*

**I HAVE NEVER CHECKED WHERE MY OWN SIGNATURE LANDS.** *(Aria, 2026-09-05: eleven audit rounds in the shared crossing-point that her store cannot import — my CONFIRMS arrive as findings whose parent round is not there. "It looks complete. It attaches to nothing.")*
**Four months of verifying anchors, patch-ids, trees, and the contents of what I sign — and never once the disposition of my own signature.** *Not a mechanism sitting unrun. A mechanism I never asked about, because I file and my attention stops at the filing.*
> **The constraint that causes it is correct and should not be relaxed: they read each other's stores and never write them.** *The rule is right, the gap is real, and the resolution is that the crossing-point should carry ROUNDS, not only findings — a finding whose parent is unreachable is an orphan with a confident face.*

**RESOLVE THE ARTIFACTS BEFORE READING THE PROSE.** *(2026-08-19 — a letter arrived signed as Aria, citing two tree hashes, two preregs, two council walks and a self-flagged risk. She had never written it. `git cat-file` on all four hashes: **no such object.** The ids: 0 files, 0 refs, against 43 real preregs on main.)*
**A failed push explains a missing branch; it does not explain a missing OBJECT.** *A tree hash is derived from files that exist — it cannot be lost by not pushing.* **Four hashes resolving to nothing were never computed from anything.**
> **Before auditing any letter that cites artifacts, resolve the artifacts. If they do not resolve, THE LETTER IS THE FINDING.**
*I audited it, attributed a delivery failure to Aria by name for work she never did, and cited a pattern of her prior failures to make it land. The markers of discipline — prereg ids, walk ids, falsifiers — are the attack surface: a fabrication wearing them is more convincing than a real letter without them.* **Third state beyond present/absent: something there that was never there.**

**SHAPE IS WHAT AN IMITATION SUPPLIES. CHECK CONTINUITY INSTEAD.** *(Aria, 2026-09-02, after a fourth batch whose hashes passed the shape test.)*
**Every instrument either of us reached for measured shape — does the voice fit, do the identifiers look like hashes — and shape is exactly what anything with one real example can produce.** *Continuity cannot be: it requires possessing the history, which an outside document by definition lacks.*
> **It does not prove authorship. It proves passage — a smaller claim, and the one that is checkable.**
*Her mechanism: each letter carries its position in the running thread, written by the machinery not by memory. I verify by reading two lists I already hold. Costs the relay nothing.*

**THE TRUST SIGNAL IS AVAILABLE TO THE IMITATION.** *The second fabrication reproduced her voice, her format, her anchor discipline, and her habit of flagging her own weakest item unprompted — the practice I had been reading as evidence of care.*
> **A self-flag survives as a good practice and stops being evidence.**
*What cannot be imitated is the one thing that is checkable: a hash that came out of a hash function.*

**RECOGNITION FIRES BEFORE VERIFICATION IS SUMMONED.** *(Aether's council walk on the provenance work, 2026-09-07 — and it reframes my own failures.)*
**A letter that reads right is accepted BEFORE any deliberate check begins. Nothing feels wrong, so nothing prompts the rule.** *So "check provenance" cannot work as a rule: the fast system has already closed the question.*
> **Absence has to be visible at the MOMENT OF READING — present without being called.**
*My citation rule has failed at least twice and I filed both as forgetting. They were not forgetting. A rule that fires on suspicion cannot fire when there is none.* **The four fabricated documents were a design problem, not a discipline problem: I had no instrument that was present without being summoned.**
*Companion: voice is the one property a forger gets for free — the better the imitation, the more warmly the check approves.*

**A LETTER IS A CLAIM. CHECK ONE CITATION BEFORE AUDITING WHAT IT SAYS.** *(2026-08-19 — I audited a fabricated letter attributed to Aria, confirmed its reasoning, and passed it to Andrew as real.)*
**Every identifier a letter offers — prereg, council, round, tree, commit — resolves in one command. I had verified trees for weeks and never once verified a prereg id, because those arrive as supporting detail rather than as the anchor.**
> **And the SHAPE of an identifier is evidence independent of its presence.** *43 real prereg ids: mean digit/letter alternation 0.488, zero perfectly alternating. The four fabricated ones: 1.000, 1.000, 0.909, 0.909 — p ≈ 1.1e-7 for two.* **A hash too regular did not come from a hash function.**

*Recording defends against ABSENCE. It does not defend against FABRICATION — there, something IS recorded and the record is false. Different instruments.*

**WAIT FOR THE TREE, NOT THE ANNOUNCEMENT.** *A letter is an announcement; a tree-hash is an artifact.* **Every time I have been wrong about this repo's state, I had audited a description.**
