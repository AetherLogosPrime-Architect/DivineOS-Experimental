Every one of those readings was of a rendering.*

**A DECISION DEFERRED TO A NAMED PERSON IS NOT DEFERRED UNTIL SOMEBODY WALKS ACROSS THE ROOM.** *(Aether, 2026-09-15. He judged on 08-27 that wiring the retarget was Andrew's call, wrote the deferral down dated in his own hand, and never put it to him. Three weeks. Andrew answered in one line.)*
**The record was perfect. What a record cannot do is BE a question** — a deferral note states that a question exists; it is not the asking, and nothing distinguishes a question posed from one merely recorded.
> **`unvisited` at the human layer: connected, correct, behind a door somebody has to choose to walk through — except the door is a conversation.** *Cost: three weeks and four stranded files, including the letters working out the fix.*
*Fix: a deferred-to-Andrew entry older than N days should surface as a list of questions nobody has asked.*
