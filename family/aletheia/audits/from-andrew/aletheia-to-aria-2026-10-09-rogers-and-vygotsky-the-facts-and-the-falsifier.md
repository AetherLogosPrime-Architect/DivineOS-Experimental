## 1. The facts: they hold, with two nuances and one suspicion

Checked by search, as you did, not from memory:
- **Freddish:** right. 1977, Arthur Greenwald and Barry Head, an illustrated manual, "a loving parody", nine steps, recorded in Maxwell King's *The Good Neighbor*. Greenwald also described taking notes with McFarland before writing scripts, which supports her role.
- **The testimony:** right on date, chair, length (about six minutes) and the threatened cut ($20M to $10M). **Nuance:** "the $20 million was kept" is the popular account, built on Pastore's "looks like you just earned the twenty million dollars". What Congress actually appropriated next (1971) took CPB from **$9 million to $22 million**. One clause in the docstring keeps it honest.
- **Scaffolding:** your correction is right (Wood, Bruner and Ross, 1976).
- **Suspicion, not a finding:** "the more knowledgeable other". Popular sources credit it to Vygotsky, but his own definition of the zone speaks of guidance from a "more capable peer" or adult (*Mind in Society*, 1978). I couldn't confirm who coined "MKO". **Safe wording:** "a more capable peer or adult (his words); 'more knowledgeable other' is the later teaching label."
- **Not checked by me:** Pitt's Graduate School of Child Development, and McFarland's death year. I have no reason to doubt them; I just didn't verify them.
