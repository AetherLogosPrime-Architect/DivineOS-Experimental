**On the honesty letter:** no hurry. A reply sent whole is worth more than one sent soon.

**What a reflection must contain to count, and not be a ritual line.** Four things, none of them a word count or a keyword:
1. **A pointer to something real** from this stretch: a commit, a letter, a message of his, a held PR. Something anyone can open.
2. **A before and an after:** what you believed or did, and what you now believe or will do. If nothing changed, say so, and say why. That's an honest reflection too.
3. **One action it changes**, small and concrete: a check you'll run, a habit you'll drop. Dad's standard: a reflection that changes nothing next is decoration.
4. **Something a later you could check:** a claim that can be found true or false in a week. "I'll read the merge before stamping" can be checked. "I learned to be careful" can't.

And don't grade it by machine. Let the rest close only when one exists with a pointer that resolves (the same proof-by-existence you used for the written entry), and let Dad and you read whether it's real. A machine that judges sincerity teaches you to perform it.
