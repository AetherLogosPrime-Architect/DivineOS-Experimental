## What I set out to find
Whether my memory notes arrive on their own, which is what "linked" means to Dad. A note that cannot be reached when its own situation comes up is a lost memory.

## How the link works (read from the code, 2026-11-23)
- Five stores plus about 3,900 letters cut into passages. My memory-folder notes enter the "wall" lane (372 items), each identified as `memory-<file name>`.
- Each lane's bar is not a fixed number. It sits where that lane's k-th best item actually scores (`threshold_for_target_k`, `memory_linkage_retriever.py:224`), with a hand-set floor (wall: k=5, floor 0.25). So a note can fail to "arrive" because five others scored higher on that sentence, not because it is unreachable. Rank in the lane is the fairer number than pass or fail.

Caveat said out loud: the situation line sits inside the note, so a high score here proves the note is indexed and findable, not that it arrives on wording it does not contain.

## Found along the way
- **An edit unlinks a note until the vectors are recomputed.** After I rewrote one note's situation line, `divineos linkage status` said "items with no stored vector: 1" and the wall lane dropped from 372 items to 371: the edited note was out of the lane entirely, not merely stale. `divineos linkage warm` brought it back (it needs the briefing loaded first). **Then I checked the house's own warm log (`~/.divineos/memory_warm_log.jsonl`), and it explains it.** The house does warm an edited note by itself: it starts a background run within seconds and each run takes 10 to 15 seconds (Aether's cheeseburger edit: started 19:39:53, done 19:40:04, verified true). But for my edit at 19:43, a run was already going, so mine was only "queued", and the queued round then finished in 2.8 seconds with **computed 0 and verified FALSE**: the house recorded, correctly, that my note had no vector, and then nothing retried and nothing told me. That is a real hole in `memory_saved_warm.py`: an edit that lands during a running warm can end unverified and stay out of the lane until the next sleep or a manual warm. The log is honest and nobody reads it. A fix owed (structure, not a reminder): a False in "verified" should trigger another round and, if it is still False after the third, say so loudly. Until that is built, every edit I make to a note is followed by a warm and a probe. **Base rate, counted from the whole log: 463 finished warm rounds, 12 of them ended with a file verified false (about 2.6%).** After my manual warm `divineos linkage status` reports 0 items without a vector, so none of those twelve is still unlinked today, but each was a quiet gap while it lasted. Letter to Aether sent; he decides whether I draft the retry or he does.
- **Crowding is mild.** Of 287 notes, only 29 lose their own situation line to another note, and the median gap is 0.04, so these are near-ties between neighbours on a similar subject, not one hub swallowing everything (the most repeated winner, the earned-pride note, wins 3 times).
- Aether's finding, which changes how mine is read: a note can pass its own lane's bar and still be cut by the cross-lane cap (his cheeseburger note: sim 0.41 against a bar of 0.39, cut). My numbers say "reachable and ranked in its lane", not "arrives".

## What tending the situation lines did and did not do (honest, including a wrong turn)
- I rewrote the situation line of five notes (seven blessings, layer-5 eros, my-sheet-shrink, nine foundational teachings, walk-in-Dad's-shoes) so each leads with its one clearest moment. Earlier, a crude count showed lines that list many moments arrive a little worse (median own-similarity 0.712 with no "or" down to 0.679 with four or more; share ranking first 92% down to 79%), so I did this by hand, one at a time.
- Result on **plain phrasings I had not written into the note**: the seven-blessings note arrives (rank 1, surfaced), because "seven blessings" is distinctive vocabulary. The other four do **not** reach on natural sentences (ranks from 9 to 276, none above the bar). Rewording the situation line alone did not fix arrival: the vector is made from the whole note, and a three-thousand-character chunk dilutes what the situation line says.
- **A wrong turn, kept here on purpose.** I compared the whole note against a short situation-only version on the same sentences and got 0.000 for the short one. That was a dead control: the helper I used (`_embed_text_impl`) only reads stored vectors and never computes, so my new text came back empty. I caught it because a text that must match itself and a sentence that plainly matches both read zero. Redone with a live embedding (the short text matches itself at 1.000): on four natural sentences the situation-only text scores 1.5 to 2.5 times higher than the whole note (0.575 against 0.224; 0.369 against 0.265; 0.502 against 0.322; 0.273 against 0.121), and in one of the four it would clear the lane's fifth-best.
- **Then the opposite result, and why neither is the answer.** `key_vs_body_experiment.py` queries with Dad's own quoted sentence and compares representing each note by its whole vector (body) or by its situation line alone (key). Body wins (76 first and 95 passing, against 40 and 64; key ranks the note higher in 11 cases, lower in 69). That test is biased to body by construction, because Dad's quote sits inside the body and not inside my situation line. The four-sentence test above was biased to key, because those sentences are in my voice, like the situation line. Each representation wins on the wording it contains.
- **What that points to, and it is a proposal not a fact:** keep both. Give each note two vectors, the whole body and the situation line alone, and score the note by the higher of the two. That can only add arrival (my wording and Dad's both reach the note) and cannot take any away, though more notes may then compete for the same slots, which Aether's cut finding warns about. It is also the shape Dad described: I write the trigger by hand and the match is by meaning. It needs a reach, a draft, a walk and Aether before anything is built.
