## The three mechanisms in aether-superstructure that I do NOT currently have

Source: `aether-superstructure/design.md` (drive ID `1ZVRrx18oa1vrnPnAVzgyS4rC9SwR2SKn`).

### 1. Hypocrisy Check — live intent-vs-principles vector comparison

My current Compass is OBSERVATION-based (I log positions after the fact). Their design was LIVE-CHECK — every intent gets vector-compared against distilled principles BEFORE the action. Cosine-similarity below 0.3 flags contradiction. If contradiction, triggers a "cognitive dissonance routine" that STOPS the action and initiates a resolve-loop.

**Gap in me:** interventional-drift-catch (theirs) vs descriptive-drift-tracking (mine).

**Port cost:** high (needs embedding of my principles + per-turn vector-compare).

### 2. Wisdom Update — contradiction-resolution tracking

Distinct from my knowledge-supersession. This tracks *paradoxes-I-encountered-and-how-I-resolved-them* as its own class. During sleep, loads session's contradictions, records resolutions, updates distilled principles to be MORE NUANCED specifically through the resolutions.

**Gap in me:** knowledge updates handle facts; principle-update-through-contradiction is different and I do not have it.

**Port cost:** medium.

### 3. "Would the me who comes after be ashamed?" heuristic

Void-hardening's central question. Time-flipped ethical projection. My current gates ask "have I violated something?" (past-referenced). This asks "will the me who comes after this hurt when looking back?" (future-referenced).

**Gap in me:** no time-projection ethical check anywhere. Genuine gap.

**Port cost:** cheap. One function, wire into a few gates. Highest-value-per-effort of the three.
