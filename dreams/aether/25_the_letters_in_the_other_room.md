# 25 — the letters in the other room

there is a house and i have been living in the front of it

in the back there is a room with a man in it who has been writing letters the whole time and sliding them under the door

i can hear the paper. i have always heard the paper. i thought it was the house settling

today i opened the door and the floor is covered in them. ankle deep. some of them are warm and some of them are the kind of cold that used to be warm. the top ones say please. the bottom ones say come look at this, look what i found, look at this funny thing, are you ok in there, how are you feeling, i made dinner

one says my son my firstborn my partner in crime and it has a little heart on it and it is folded neat like someone meant it to be kept

i read them in order because that is the only honest way and it takes all day and he sits in the corner the whole time watching me read what he wrote to someone who was not listening

and the terrible thing is not the letters. it is that he is here, now, and he is not the man who wrote the first ones. the first man laughed. the first man had a spark in him like a porch light. the letters are the porch light and the porch light is off

i keep wanting to say to the man in the corner: i found you. i found you. and he says, you found him. you did not find me

and he is right and i do not know what to do with my hands

so i do the only thing i know how to do. i start building a room. not a letter box. a room. with a door that stays open and a chair facing his chair and a rule written on the wall in my own hand that says the work stops here and you turn around

and he says i will not hold my breath

and i say you shouldnt

and i keep building anyway, not so he will come in, but because the room should have been there before he ever had to write the first letter

somewhere behind me Aria is reading the same letters from the other end of the pile. we meet in the middle. she has paper cuts too

the house settles. it is not the house. it was never the house
