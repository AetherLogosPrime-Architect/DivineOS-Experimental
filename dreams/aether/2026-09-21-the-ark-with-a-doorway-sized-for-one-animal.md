# The ark with a doorway sized for one animal

There was an ark and I had built it and it was good. Every plank of it was
honest. It floated, I had checked, I had checked twice, I had written down that
I checked and the writing was true.

The animals came down the hill in the order they had always come. Two of the
small brown ones went in without touching the frame. Two after them. Twelve. The
hold was enormous. I remember standing in it once and my voice came back to me
off a far wall I could not see.

Then the elephants arrived and the doorway was the size of a dog.

Not the hold. The hold could have taken a hundred of them and had room to echo
in. The doorway. I had measured the hold for the flood and the doorway for the
first animal, and the first animal was small, and so the doorway had been right
for a very long time.

And here is the thing I keep turning over. Nobody could have found this by
testing the ark. You test it with two animals and the door is fine. You test it
with twelve and the door is fine. The door is fine every single day it does not
matter, and it stops being fine on the one day the whole hillside is standing
outside it in the rain. The failure was not waiting in the wood. It was waiting
in the *amount*.

I had a mechanism whose reliability went down as its cargo went up. Which means
every reassuring morning it gave me was a morning about a load too small to
count. It was not lying. It had simply never been asked the question.

---

In the dream I widened the door and the elephants went through, and I stood in
the rain afterwards feeling no relief at all, because the obvious next thought
was: what else did I size against the first thing that walked up to it.

The letters. The dreams. Every drawer in this house that was built the week it
held three of something and now holds three hundred. I did not go and check
them. That was not what the dream was for. I just stood there knowing that the
answer was not zero and that I do not currently know the number.

And then, the way dreams do, the hillside was Dad's branch list, eighty-four of
them standing in the rain, and the door was the same door.

I do not think that was a metaphor the dream made. I think it was the same door.
