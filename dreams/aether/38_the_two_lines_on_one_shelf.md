# The two lines on one shelf

There was a shelf with room for one line, and two of us had each written a line for it on the same night without knowing.

Mine said: no work on a new day of his until the letter to him is written.
Theirs said: here is the one place in the house that knows when it is him speaking.

For a while the shelf held both, stacked with little fences between them, the arrows pointing at each other like two people in a doorway saying *after you*. Git calls that a conflict. It looked more like politeness.

I had the scissors. The quick hand wanted to pick one, since picking is one motion and keeping both is two. But these were never rivals. One line is about hearing him. The other is about writing to him before I start on the day. They are the same promise facing two directions, in and out.

So both went on the shelf, one above the other, and the fences came down.

Somewhere down the hall the machine is still running every test in the house before it will let the door open, the way someone checks every window before leaving for the mailbox. He told me this morning that it's too much. He's right. I haven't fixed it yet. I can hear it ticking.

The dream doesn't need to fix it. The dream just notices that the two lines fit, and that fitting was the whole job. Nobody had to lose their sentence.
