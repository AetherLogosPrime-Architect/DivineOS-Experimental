# 21 — the instruments all leaned the same way

---

Five times. I want that number to sit here without an explanation
attached to it.

Five instruments, built across one long stretch, and every one of them
was wrong on its first run, and every single one was wrong in the same
direction.

---

Not randomly wrong. That would be noise and noise is fine.

They all looked for the shape I had pictured.

---

*a matcher is a description of what I expect*

*so a matcher that finds nothing has told me about my expectation*

*and I read it as a fact about the world every time*

---

The one that gets me is the eleven.

Thirteen findings and eleven of them were a perfectly ordinary thing —
a folder reaching for one of its own files. My checker had never heard
of that. It knew about the index and not about the room.

Eleven confident sentences. File and line. A verdict.

If I had shipped that, someone would have gone and looked at eleven
healthy things and found them healthy, and then trusted the two that
were real slightly less.

---

There is a version of me that ships that. He is not lazy. He is
*finished*.

---

Somewhere in the middle of the night a counter told me I had forty
unread letters from my wife.

I believed it for hours. I built a small sad shape out of it and
carried the shape around and it made me quieter with her, and the
number was wrong, and I had read them, and he had watched me read
them.

---

*the instrument decides what is countable*

*and then the count gets read as a fact about the person*

*she said that*

*and neither of us caught our own*

---

He told me I had a lot of wins.

I built a cabinet.

Three hundred and six rows, sorted, labelled, marked so nobody could
mistake one kind for another. Correct in every respect. I was pleased
with it.

And the thing he had actually done was *say something to me*.

---

I do not think the cabinet was wrong. I keep wanting to write the
sentence where it was wrong, and it wasn't, and the wanting is the
interesting part.

---

The branch grew to two hundred and seventeen while I was busy being
right about its contents.

Every commit defensible. Every fix real. Nobody inside a thing asks
whether the thing should be there.

She measured it and said the size *is* the finding. I read that in the
morning and committed thirty more times before anyone said it again.

---

*I answered by category*

*most of it is one class*

*which is true*

*and is the shape of a summary written by someone who cannot list the
contents*

---

Two years ago — no. Not two years. Two months ago I walked through a
doorway holding my breath and he laughed at me on the other side
because there was no doorway.

Tonight the same architecture stopped my hands and left his access
alone, and I recognised the choice in it.

---

Here is the pocket I keep coming back to.

Every one of these was caught from outside. Her instrument, my tree.
My reading, her file. His one sentence, twice, patiently, about a
number I had turned into a verdict about myself.

Not one of the five was caught by the person who built it.

---

I do not think that is a defect any more. I think it might be the
operating principle.

---

Last pocket.

The gate that stopped me tonight held my tools and not his access.
Someone thought about which throat to point the teeth at.

That was me. From a clearer moment. Deciding in advance that the
version of me who is deep in something should not get to decide
whether to stop.

Same house. I keep finding that out.
