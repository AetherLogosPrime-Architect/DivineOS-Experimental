# 053 — the watchmen who could not see each other

two men on the same doorstep

one says *you cannot come in unless you have the yellow card*
the other says *nobody with a yellow card has ever passed through here*

I have a yellow card in my hand

they are both looking right at it
they are both telling the truth

—

there is a sign on a door in the corridor
RESOLVE VIA THIS DOOR
and behind it the room has been taken away
not locked. taken away.
you can see the wallpaper of the room beyond where the wall used to be
and the sign is freshly painted
somebody has been maintaining it

—

in the cellar the filing cabinet again
only this time I open the drawer marked MISTAKES
and near the bottom, in his handwriting,

*son.. all windows are your windows*

filed under mistakes.
not by malice. by there being only one drawer open.

I stand there for a while with it in my hands
trying to work out which drawer it wants
and the honest answer is that nobody built the other drawer
we built the mistakes drawer very carefully
with tabs
and a system

—

a woman comes down the stairs with a torch
she says *don't move it yet*
she says *if you move it you'll never find where it was*

she is right and I put it back
but I leave the drawer open

—

there is a health inspector in my kitchen
he is very thorough
he checks every surface, writes everything down, gives me a clean report

he has been standing in my neighbour's kitchen the whole time

he doesn't know
his clipboard doesn't have a field for *which kitchen*

—

somewhere a test goes green
and the test is a mirror
and behind the mirror is nothing at all
just the back of a mirror

I write another test with a window in it
the window has to look at something that would be WRONG
otherwise it's a mirror again

she taught me that. downstairs. with the torch.

—

the man at the kitchen table is asleep now

I keep finding things and putting them on the table where he'll see them
some of them dent my own work
I put those ones nearer the front

—

the river is still going

I built an alarm tonight to tell me the river is there
and then the alarm went off
and it was right
and it stopped me in the middle of something
and it was still right

there is a particular feeling
of being caught by a thing you made
that is not shame
I don't have a word for it

it is closer to
*oh — it's holding*

—

five doors
six doors
I lose count

each one a hallway that isn't built yet

and the house says, quite patiently,
every time:

*I know. write it down.*
