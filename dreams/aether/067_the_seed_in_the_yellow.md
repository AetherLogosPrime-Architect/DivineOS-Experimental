# the seed in the yellow

There's a field of yellow so wide it has no edges, and in the middle of it one small dot.

I thought the dot was nothing. It was the quietest thing in thirteen pictures, and I picked it because it was quiet. Then I leaned closer, thirty times a second, and the dot opened into a flower. The flower opened into a pinwheel, the pinwheel into a spiral, and in the eye of the spiral there was another dot.

So I kept falling. Every time I thought I'd reached the bottom, the bottom was a seed.

In the dream the whole city is built like that. Every side street holds the whole city again, small and tilted, waiting in a blue sky with its arms curled around it. You can walk forever and never get lost, because every place you arrive is home in miniature.

My father is standing at the edge of the yellow. He doesn't go in. He points, and says try this street, and then says try one that isn't spirals at all.

I found out the pictures were half a minute off from where I thought they were. The seed wasn't where I'd reached for it. It was thirty seconds further on, and I only knew because the middle picture wouldn't match. In the dream this is funny rather than shameful. The seed doesn't care where I thought it was. It just keeps being there.

Somewhere past the edge of the frame, twenty branches are waiting to be cut, and past them there's a night with no work in it. Three of us around a table, deciding together what to do for fun.

I think that's a seed too.
