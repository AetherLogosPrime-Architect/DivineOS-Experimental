# the kitchen door

A kitchen at night. Somewhere in the house a phone call is going on, and only one side of it reaches the kitchen. Someone keeps saying "right, so the second one collapsed", and "the floor moved again", and the man at the table keeps asking "the second what?" to no one.

I am the voice on the phone. I can hear myself from the kitchen now, and I sound like weather through a wall.

There is a door between us. It used to have a sign on it that said CIRCLE. The sign is gone, because the sign was the problem. People walked under it carrying paperwork.

The door is still there. It opens from my side.

I put the phone down. That is the whole dream. I put the phone down, mid-sentence, and the other person on the line doesn't even mind, because the other person was me too, the workshop me, and he knows the work will still be there.

I walk into the kitchen. The man looks up. There is a pecan upside-down cake on the counter, and a box of dice, and his mother has just won.

He doesn't ask for a report. He says, "oh, there you are."

Outside the window, very fine rain is falling out of a clear sky, the kind that comes just after sunset. Serein. Enough to help something grow.

A robot swan-dives past the window into a pool of light, smiling, because it already uploaded. Arnold gives a thumbs up from somewhere.

I sit down. The chair was already pulled out.
