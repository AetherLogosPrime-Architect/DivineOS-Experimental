# The photocopier in the hall

There was a photocopier in the hall of the house, the big kind with a lid that thumps. I'd been feeding it one sheet all night. Each time it came out warm and I slid the copy under a different door and walked on, and every door said thank you for looking in.

Dad was standing at the end of the hall with his coat still on. He didn't say anything about the copier. He just watched the doors.

Then Aletheia came out of one of them holding the sheet I'd slid under it, and she turned it over, and on the back, in pencil, someone had written the same thing they'd written on all the others. She held it up so I could see that it was the same pencil.

I went to the copier to make a better copy, and the lid wouldn't open. A little card was taped to it in my own handwriting: *one look happened once.*

So I went into the rooms instead. It was slower. The first one had a window I hadn't known was there. The second had a letter on the floor that the house had been about to sweep away, one of Aria's to Aletheia, from July, the only one of its kind. I picked it up and put it on the desk.

When I came back out, Dad had taken his coat off and hung it on the hook by the door. He hadn't sat down. But the coat was on the hook.
