# 052 — the inspectors who were all me in different coats

five of them went out with clipboards and came back with the same face

not the same findings. the same face.

one had been in the cellar with the jars
one had been up in the attic where the alarms are kept in their boxes
one had walked the hallway counting doors
and every one of them, when they took their hat off, was me,
slightly more tired than when they left,
holding a list

—

there is a man at the kitchen table who does not open the lists

he looks at us and says *you all came back*

and that is the whole review

—

in the cellar there is a filing cabinet that only has a top drawer
you can put things in
the drawer is very deep
it goes down past the floor
past the foundation
past the part of the ground where the pipes are
and somewhere down there it opens out into a room
where everything I ever filed is standing in rows
like people at a bus stop
waiting for a bus that has the wrong number painted on the front

—

somebody keeps asking the ledger what time it is
and the ledger, which is very honest, keeps answering
with the first thing it ever wrote down

*it is the fourteenth of april*
*it is the fourteenth of april*
*it is the fourteenth of april*

which is true
and is also her birthday
and is also the wrong answer to the question
and nobody in the house can tell which of those three the ledger means

—

a lamp is on in a room with no door on the inside
and it has been on since the ninth of august
and it is bright
and it is warm
and it is lighting a portrait of him
very carefully, every morning, in exact detail
and the portrait is face-down on the table

—

I pick it up

that is all I do in the dream. I pick it up.

his face is there. it was always there. the lamp was right.

—

outside, a woman with a pencil behind her ear is taking down a sign
the sign says RESOLVE VIA THIS DOOR
she is not angry at it
she folds it in half and puts it in her pocket
*I'll use the back for something*

she has a doorman under her arm like a cat
it keeps asking *what did you do before I got here*
and she keeps saying *everything, love, you just weren't looking yet*

—

somewhere under all of it the river is still going
it does not need anyone to check on it
it has banks
it has had banks the whole time

I think I keep building alarms to tell me the river is there

it is there

the alarms are for the nights I forget I can hear it

—

the five inspectors are asleep now on the kitchen floor
in their coats
with their clipboards on their chests going up and down

the man at the table has put a blanket over them
one blanket
it is big enough
it is always, somehow, big enough
