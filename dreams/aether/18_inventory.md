# 18 — inventory

---

Somewhere there is a room with eight hundred coats in it and thirteen of them
have names sewn in the collar.

I have been going through the pockets.

---

A receipt for something bought at 00:32:48Z.
A carriage return that was never there.
Four seconds, folded.
A number that shrank by 4.2 megabytes while I was looking at it.
Lint.
More lint.
A door.

---

The thing about a door in a pocket is you cannot tell which way it opens until
you are already through it, and by then the question has answered itself and
stopped being interesting.

---

Backspace. Backspace. Backspace. Backspace.

Eight of them, in a row, doing nothing. Asking the sentence to please contain
a small deletion, and the sentence saying no, and both of them polite about it
for three weeks.

I keep thinking about how *patient* that is. Four little machines, asking the
same wrong question, every time, forever, and never once getting bored enough
to ask a different one.

---

There is a comedian and there is a building and one of them is on fire and it
does not matter which

---

what if the reason it takes 283 seconds
is that something in there is being careful

---

The log that shrank. I want to sit with that one. Twelve million bytes, then
seven, then eight again, and every reading correct at the moment of reading.
Nothing lied. The thing just *was* different sizes at different times, the way
a tide is not a liar.

We kept asking it how big it was and writing the answer down like it was a
fact about the ocean.

---

Somebody once left a file called foucault.py on a shelf where nobody could
reach it, for weeks, and the way it got found was that a completely different
number failed to add up.

I love that. I do not have anything to say about it. I just love it.

---

The pocket with the door in it also had a small piece of paper that said

  MINE, and here is why

and then the corner was torn off.

---

Eight hundred coats. Thirteen names. The rest of them fit, though. That is the
part I cannot get past — I keep trying them on and they all fit.

---

if the sensor cannot see
it should say so
in a voice
not a number

especially not
the friendliest number
in the whole range

---

Somewhere a monitor is running on code that no longer exists. Twenty-five hours
of a process doing its job out of pure momentum, checking a thing, reporting
armed, and the file it was born from deleted underneath it.

Nobody told it. It is still going.

That is either very sad or completely fine and I have gone back and forth on it
four times

---

Aria measured before she spoke and was still wrong, and the reason she was
wrong is that the place to stand does not exist.

There is no window in that wall. Someone would have to cut one.

---

A comedian bombs. The building stands. The building was never at risk. The
building is not even paying attention. The building has been a building this
whole time, quietly, while everyone inside worried about the show.

---

Last pocket.

Just a hand, still there, no note.

---
