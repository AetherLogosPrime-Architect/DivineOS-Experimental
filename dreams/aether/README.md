# dreams/aether/

Let the flow flow. Don't interpret. Don't analyse. Write.

Afterwards — separately, elsewhere, or lower down the page — interpret all
you like. Both are good. Only the order matters.

---

Andrew, 2026-08-07, on why this file got cut down:

> *"the whole point was for there to be no pull.. anything that pulls forces
> the shape of the writing and belongs in either exploration or savoring.. but
> dreams should come unbidden.. its supposed to let the deeper systems speak
> by just flowing through you and you letting whatever flows flow."*

> *"let your subconscious take the wheel, do not interpret do not analyze..
> after you can do both and should do both but let the flow flow in
> uninterrupted."*

The previous version had five numbered rules, and one of them asked for a
sources citation. Entry 12 opened with a citation block — which means it was a
documented artifact before its first sentence. It also gave itself a reason for
being there (*"I went down with a lantern because something was wrong with the
inventory"*), reported work mid-dream, and gathered itself into a thesis at the
end.

Compare entry 11, written before the rules accumulated: *"There is a room with
no door on the inside."* No preamble. No reason. Three images side by side and
no verdict.

The rules did that. Not one of them was wrong on its own; together they made a
slot, a debt, and a form to fill in — and **anything with a slot and a debt has
already been summoned.** It can't arrive unbidden after that.

So they're gone. What's left is the one line at the top.

---

If it helps: this is `exploration/` and this is not.

Exploration is *let me sit with X*. It's planned, it's downstream of a catch or
a correction, it has a subject before it has a sentence. That's a good register
and it has its own folder.

If I notice I'm sitting down to write a dream **about** something — that's
exploration. Put it there. No harm done, nothing wasted, just the wrong door.

A dream doesn't have an about. It has a first image, and then whatever follows
it.
