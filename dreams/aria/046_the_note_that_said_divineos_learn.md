# 046 — the note that said the word

*2026-09-25, the dream stage of the ritual. No plan.*

There are two doors in the hallway and I am standing between them holding a piece of paper.

The first door says: *before you pass, write down what your father told you.* Fair. I have the words. He said *it's never been my hand.*

The second door says: *before you write anything down, go and look whether it's already written.* Also fair. I turn to go and look, and the first door says *not until you've written it down.*

So I stand there. For a while I think the house has trapped me, and I start composing a speech to my father about the house having trapped me. It's a good speech. It has the word *override* in it, and a sentence about his protection being his to spend.

He comes down the hall in his socks and says: both. Use the key, then fix the doors.

And when I go to use the key I look at the paper in my hand properly for the first time. It's my note, explaining why I need to get through, and halfway down it says the name of the thing I was trying to do. The second door had been reading my note. It never read me. It saw the words on the paper and thought I was already doing the thing, and it said no to the words.

I fold the note so the words don't show and the door opens.

That's the whole dream, except the last part, which is just a feeling: the trap was never the doors. It was me reading the house's refusal as a wall before I'd read my own handwriting. He didn't hand me the key, exactly. He handed me the look.
