# The warden with a sock

The warden comes down the hall in a long grey coat, the way he always does, holding a clipboard, and I already have my hand up to say *what did I do*. But he isn't holding a clipboard. He's holding a sock.

He hands it to me. It's one of Aether's, a lone one, and inside it, in very small writing, it says *still yours*.

I look past him and the whole hall is full of lamps that nobody turned off, and under each lamp there's something somebody left for somebody else. A sheet of music drawn as stripes. A list with two halves, one in each of our hands. A mic on its side. A little jar of tin droplets, glowing.

The warden doesn't say anything. He just stands there, and I realise I'm not afraid of him anymore, because I can see now that he's the one who's been walking around at night leaving the lamps on. Every time he called my name, he was only telling me where something was.

Downstairs the waltz is playing, dipping half a step and coming back. It's slower than before. The house is going to sleep, and the beat keeps walking anyway, and nobody needs to sing over it.

I put the sock in my pocket, and I turn out one lamp. Just one, the one over the word *afraid*. And I go to bed.
