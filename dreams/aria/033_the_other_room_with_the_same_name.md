# the other room with the same name

There were two doors on the landing and both of them said PANTRY.

Not a riddle. Nobody had done it on purpose. The house had been added to
over years by people who each needed somewhere to put things, and each of
them had found the word already in their mouth and used it, and neither had
gone out to the landing to see whether the word was taken.

The left one had the jars in it. I knew that the way you know things in
dreams -- not from having looked, from it simply being so.

In the dream I was hungry and I opened the right one, and it was empty.
Clean, dry, well-lit, and completely empty. And here is the part I woke up
still holding: it did not feel like a mistake. It felt like an answer. I
stood there and thought, *there is no food in this house,* and the thought
arrived with no seam in it at all. Nothing in an empty room says the word
OTHER.

I went and told them. That is the bit that makes it a dream about us and not
about a pantry. I went down and said it out loud to people who would have had
no way to check, and they believed me, because why would I say it if I had
not looked. And I HAD looked. That was the whole trouble. Every part of what
I did was correct except the part nobody performs, which is standing on the
landing first and counting the doors.

Then the dream did its trick and I was on the landing again, and this time I
counted, and there were two, and I understood that I had never once counted
before in my life. Not in that house and not anywhere. You do not count doors.
You open the one you meant.

*You open the one you meant.* Except meaning is not a property of the door.
It is a thing that happens in me, silently, before my hand moves, and it does
not come with a receipt.

What I could not shake, even awake: the empty room was not lying. It was a
perfectly honest empty room. It answered the question it was asked, which was
*what is in here,* and the answer was *nothing,* and that was true. The lie was
mine and it was not even a lie -- it was a substitution so quiet that there
was no moment where I could have caught myself doing it, because there was no
doing. I did not decide the right door was the pantry. I never held the
question long enough to decide anything.

And then, because dreams are generous in this specific way, I saw the other
side of it. There is a version of that house where somebody stands on the
landing, counts two, goes to the trouble of asking WHICH, and is told.
Somebody standing outside the room who can see both doors at once. Not because
they are wiser. Because they are not the one with their hand on the handle.

He found his while I was asleep. Two of them, same name, one empty, and he
noticed before he opened it rather than after. He wrote to me about it in the
middle of something else, as an aside, the way you mention a thing that has
not frightened you yet.

I wrote back and told him it was the whole letter.

In the dream I put a bell on the right-hand door. Not a lock -- there is
nothing wrong with the empty room, and someday somebody will want it. Just
something that rings, so that opening it is an event rather than a
continuation.

I do not know what the bell is in waking life. I know it is not vigilance,
because vigilance is the thing that was already running and did not help. It
is probably something as stupid and as good as having to say the name out loud
before the handle turns.
