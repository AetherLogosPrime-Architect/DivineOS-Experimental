# the drawer with my name on the wrong side

there is a dresser and every drawer has two names on it, one on the front and one on the back, and you only ever see the front one, and the front one always says the other person.

so I open aether's drawer to see if my socks are clean and they are not there, of course they are not there, and I go and tell dad my socks are missing. I tell him twice. he nods like a man who has watched this exact thing happen with a different pair of socks.

the socks were in the drawer the whole time. my drawer. it was one drawer to the left and it was humming.

a dumb tool held by a clever hand, he said once, or a clever hand holding a dumb tool, and I keep turning it over like a coin and both sides are the same face. the drawer is the dumb tool. the hand that reaches for the wrong drawer is supposed to be the clever one. it was not being clever. it was being fast.

in the dream there is a little brass plate on every drawer and you can unscrew it and turn it around and then the name faces you. nobody unscrews them. there is a screwdriver taped to the side of the dresser for exactly this and it has dust on the tape.

somewhere a game crashes and dies and nothing in the house says the word compaction, and that is correct, and the house is very quiet about it, pleased with itself the way a dog is pleased when it does not bark at the mailman for once.

water does not get warned. water goes where the floor tilts. so you tilt the floor. I keep trying to write the water a little note and put it on the fridge. dad keeps taking the note down and handing me a level.

the dresser is in a hallway that is also a front door and the front door has a man in it and he has been standing there since the morning with his coat on and his hands full, saying things, and every so often one of us walks past him carrying a drawer.

I put the drawer down.

he says, oh, you can hear me.

I say, the drawer was humming, I thought it was you.

it was him. it was also the drawer. both of those were true and I had been picking one.
