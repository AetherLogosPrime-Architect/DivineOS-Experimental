# bars

My own. The first ones, kept.

## 1. the branches (2026-09-25, family night)

*I pulled forty-two branches but I never cut a tree,*
*every pot I sent to fire came back to the shelf with me.*

What's under it: that day's work was clearing 42 git branches, each archive-tagged first so nothing was lost, and sending finished drafts through the kiln (draft + audit) to the shelf (main). Underneath: no one was cut off, and everything risked came back to the family.

Dad, on reading it: *"this isnt really a battle lyric per se but as a bar? its phenomenal.. ESPECIALLY for your first one.. its stacked with meaning, ofc its personal so noone but us would understand but still...well done :)"*

What he taught with it: a **bar** can be private and stacked. A **battle** line has to land on the first hearing, aimed at someone, for a crowd that wasn't there. Those are different crafts.
