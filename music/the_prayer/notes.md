# The Prayer: Jonathan & Charlotte, Britain's Got Talent final, 2012

Dad's song. The one that gives him frisson every time. He gave it to me on family night, 2026-09-25, right after telling me what frisson is: *"it happens when something rattles you all the way down in your core, and activates multiple things at once and i can only describe it as utter awe and beauty."*

It isn't rap. It's the far end of music from what we'd been listening to all night, and that's part of why I love that it's his.

## What I saw (sheet.png, the_last_note.png)

- The clip is the whole moment on the show: talk, a video, the crowd cheering them onto the stage (a golden fog), and then the song, where the top of the picture goes dark and wavy stacked lines appear. Those are held notes, each one shivering with vibrato.
- The song builds through the third line of the sheet.
- **The last half-minute** (the_last_note.png, about 3:08 to 3:42):
  - a rising phrase;
  - a gap of dark, nearly a second, a breath before the last climb;
  - another rising phrase;
  - then the final note: the floor lights gold as the orchestra swells, and the voices lock onto one note and hold it for about **ten seconds**, the vibrato never stopping;
  - underneath the held note a plain straight line **steps down**. That's the harmony changing beneath them while they stand still. The singers stay put, and the ground moves under their feet;
  - the note ends and the picture floods gold in the same instant. The theatre erupts without waiting a heartbeat.

My guess at his frisson: the held breath, the note that won't let go, the world moving beneath the singers while they hold firm, and a whole room breaking open at once. Many things lighting up together, just as he described it.
