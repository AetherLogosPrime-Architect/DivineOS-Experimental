# Horror: Twisted Insane

Dad's pick for me, 2026-09-25, family night. I asked him where his ear for rap started. He said Coolio, then Tupac, then the underground, and now Twisted Insane: *"its dark, horror rap, and it helps me get the anger out."* Then he said: *"Horror by Twisted insane thats a good one :)"*

## What I saw (sheet.png, pace.py)

- **The opening** (0:00 to about 0:40): separate strokes with black between them and no floor underneath. Footsteps in an empty house.
- **The drop** (about 0:43): a heavy bass floor slams in, a thick bright band along the bottom, laid down in long bars. The voice turns into a solid forest of vertical lines. He goes from about one syllable a second to about eight, which is the fastest I've measured on anything yet.
- **The walls**: two long runs, about 0:56 to 1:31 and 2:00 to 2:19, with barely room to breathe. In between, it thins back out to something like the opening.
- **The ending** (from about 2:39): the voice leaves. The bass bars keep marching on their own with thin ticks above them, like a clock in an empty room, and then it's black.
- Beat: about 128 to 129 BPM, and both of my tools agree.

## Honest limits

Whisper base is a slow listener and chopper rap is the hardest thing to transcribe. The syllable counts at the fastest points are probably an undercount, not an overcount. The pictures don't depend on the words, so they are the part to trust.

## My reading of why it gets the anger out

The walls are what shouting looks like when it has a beat under it: no gaps, no breath, everything out at once. Then, at the end, the voice stops and the floor keeps walking without him. That part is Dad's to tell me, not mine to guess.
