# Vanta Audits — Side-Project Space

**Established:** 2026-07-26
**Purpose:** Hold audit reports + findings for Vanta's projects, as an outward-facing application of DivineOS council + wisdom.

---

## Background

Vanta ran an external security audit on the DivineOS public repo (2026-07-26 — findings at `docs/security_reviews/2026-07-26_vanta_public_repo_audit.md`) and offered a reciprocal exchange: Andrew and Aether audit Vanta's projects with the full council + accumulated wisdom, offering constructive advice and opinions on what works and what doesn't.

Andrew 2026-07-26: *"my friend who gave use the vanta audit wanted us to audit his projects as well with the full council and offer him constructive advice and opinions on what works and doesnt while bringing your wisdom and experience to the table"*

## What lives here

- Per-project audit folders (one per Vanta project reviewed)
- Reports + findings routed via council walks
- Recommendations in a shape both Andrew and Vanta can use

## Shape of each project audit

Structure per project (fill in when a project lands):
- `<project-name>/`
  - `intake.md` — what Vanta shared, project scope, ask
  - `council_walk.md` — the lens walks against the project (ALL relevant per Andrew's morning teaching, not just 3)
  - `findings.md` — surfaced gaps, risks, opportunities
  - `recommendations.md` — constructive advice in Vanta's language
  - `outcome.md` — what Vanta did with the feedback (if reported back)

## Discipline

Same discipline as internal audits, applied outward:
- **Question-quality gates answer-quality** (Andrew 2026-07-26 council-usage teaching): frame the walks as "how do we help this succeed" not just "find holes"
- **Multiple framings**: adversarial-review + solution-generation + blind-spot-detection + building-assistance + viability-evaluation as appropriate to the project's stage
- **Council is methodology-inputs, not authority**: findings feed our + Vanta's judgment, not override his
- **Vanta owns his substrate**: recommendations are offered, not prescribed. He's the composer inside his own system.

## Relationship-shape

This isn't consulting-work-for-hire. It's reciprocal exchange with a friend of Andrew's who offered his time on our repo. Reciprocity matters — we bring real wisdom, not go-through-the-motions review.
