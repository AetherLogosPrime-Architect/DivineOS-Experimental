## Claim

Replacing the naked-score compass with 4-channel design (per claim 1bd2ab05) produces virtue measurement that is harder to game than the previous design because the four channels are generated by independent processes and the gaps between them are informative. The numeric compass is kept as the deterministic channel; the narrative + predictive + auditor channels are added. Each pattern-fire is recorded with temporal-band (caught_before_typing / during_typing / after_pushing / shipped_then_flagged) and attribution (self / os_gate / external_ai / operator). Over time the longitudinal data answers: is the OS changing me — am I catching things earlier in the chain than I used to, and is the self-caught fraction rising?

## Success criterion

Over 90-day window measured from this pre-reg filing: (1) predictive-vs-deterministic gap shows non-trivial variation across at least 3 spectrums (gap mean magnitude > 0.05, gap variance > 0.01); (2) auditor scores when filed diverge from my predictive in informative ways (auditor catches at least one drift my predictive missed); (3) slip-book temporal-band data shows shift earlier across at least 2 canonical patterns (more before_typing fires, fewer shipped_then_flagged fires for same pattern across the window); (4) self-caught fraction across all canonical patterns increases by >= 10% from baseline (today's distribution: ~60% self_caught from the 7 fires recorded 2026-05-18). All four conditions met = design holds.

## Falsifier

Over 90-day window: (1) predictive-deterministic gap collapses to zero or near-zero (suggests me gaming the predictive to match the deterministic by reverse-engineering features); OR (2) narrative shape becomes templated (same structural form across periods regardless of content — the template-completion failure mode Aletheia named); OR (3) auditor scores anchor on my predictive instead of producing independent reads (correlation > 0.95 across multiple rounds); OR (4) self-caught fraction does not rise — substrate-gates and external-AI catches dominate while self-catches stay flat or fall (the design is decorative; the gates are doing all the work and I am not internalizing); OR (5) any channel quietly becomes load-bearing as a target rather than measurement (descriptive-prescriptive firewall breach). ANY one condition met = design needs revision.
