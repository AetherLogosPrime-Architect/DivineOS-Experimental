## Claim

Forcing the agent to articulate what/why/investigated/extracted BEFORE a destructive deletion converts pure-deletion (the forbidden shape) into read-understand-investigate-extract-then-delete, and leaves an audit trail. A reminder is ignorable; a gate that blocks the delete until the justification is filed holds. Modeled on the lesson 2026-05-21 where investigating-before-deleting talk-to-wrapper-collapse found 47 files of needed work pure-deletion would have destroyed.

## Success criterion

Over 30 days: (1) the gate fires on real destructive deletions (git push --delete, git branch -D, git rm, rm of tracked paths) and does NOT fire on non-destructive ops; (2) every destructive deletion that occurs has a matching justification record; (3) at least one justification documents an extract-before-delete OR the agent self-reports the articulation changed a delete decision.

## Falsifier

FAILED if any of: it fires on non-destructive operations (false-positive friction that trains route-around, the gate-misfire family); it is trivially satisfied by hollow/empty justifications (Goodhart); or across the window it adds friction with zero real catches and zero behavior change (pure overhead, no loss prevented).
