**Andrew 2026-09-09, standing order, verbatim:**

> then you will attach it to reflection.. after every work flow you will enter
> reflection and look for any failures you encountered along the way and you
> will do a root cause diagnostic and fix for all of them, this is mandatory,

## What it is for, in his words from earlier the same hour

> the no shame system was so you actually look at your failures during
> reflection and devise solutions or plans to address them, plans being a
> proper build flow using the council and research if you cannot find a
> solution

Reflection was ALWAYS meant to be where failures get worked. I read *no shame
in failures* as *no urgency about failures* and turned the room into a place to
describe them. This order restores the room to what it was built for.

## The hole this closes, measured today

The correction store already refuses a filing carrying no root cause and no
structural fix. It refused me twice today and was right both times.

But that door only stands where a filing arrives. It cannot see the case where
I notice a failure, SAY SO OUT LOUD, and keep walking.

That happened in front of him. I said a check I had built an hour earlier had
just failed to catch me, in plain words, then moved to the next paragraph.
Nothing anywhere registered that a failure had been named and abandoned. He
caught it: *"no root cause investigation of why your check that you just built
failed.. just sweep it under the rug and add it to the pile."*

**So the enforcement catches me when I am being diligent and misses me when I
am not** — the exact inversion of what a guard is for.

## What the order requires, stated as a build

At the end of a workflow, reflection must:

1. **Collect** every failure encountered during the work, not only the ones I
   chose to file. The failure noticed mid-work and stepped over is the target
   case; it is the entire reason the order exists.
2. **Refuse to close** while any collected failure has no root cause and no
   fix, or an honest no-structure-possible with a named reason — which the
   correction store already knows how to demand and can be reused rather than
   rebuilt.
3. **Not depend on my remembering.** He said no exceptions, and my memory is
   the thing that has failed every time. Whatever collects must read the action
   stream and my own text, never my recall.
