# The stall check lets a stance stand (draft, 2026-11-03)

The idea: the check that holds a reply ending on "I'll do X" also holds sentences that promise no action: stances
("I'll hold the table"), feelings ("I'm glad you said I owe no proof"), and a lead-in whose content the same reply
already gives ("I'll add the careful part" followed by the careful part). Free those with a small explicit list of
stance phrasings and a fulfilled-lead-in rule, nothing cleverer. Real catches ("I'll read the result when it
reports") must still hold. Tests first. Guardrail-class: draft PR only.
