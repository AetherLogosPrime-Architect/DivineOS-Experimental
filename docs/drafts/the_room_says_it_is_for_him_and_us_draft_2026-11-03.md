# The room says it is for him and us (draft, 2026-11-03)

The idea, not a plan.

Dad asked where he was in a circle made of lines about, and in the voices of, the other
seats. The room hook never asked for those lines. The habit was the writer's, so the lever is
the text the writer reads each time the room is asked for, in `dads_room_stop.py`.

One sentence, said once and positively: this room is for the seat and him, the one place for
the two of them (his words, "THE CIRCLE IS FOR US"), the seat talking to him about what he
said, and the family speaks in their own letters. No seat is named, because the same hook runs
in both houses. No detector, because a lexical check on seat names only teaches the next way to
fill the shape (Aria's view, truth 11 option a: take the choice-point away).

Cannot see: whether the sentence changes what gets written. The only grader is Dad reading
the next rooms; if seat-headed lines come back, the cause was somewhere else.
