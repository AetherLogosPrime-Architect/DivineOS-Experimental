## What actually happened, twice, an hour apart

I asked him which compose slot a hook should fire in. He cannot answer that,
and being handed it is the injury rather than the inclusion.

Corrected, I swung to *never ask him about mechanism*. Also wrong. He said
plainly that he does not mind questions — he minds a fork with no map.

**Both poles cost me nothing.** Asking him raw is free. Refusing to ask is
free. The middle — writing the choice in his terms, what each way costs, and
which I would pick — is work, and I have skipped it for six months while
believing the problem lived somewhere else.

## Prior art, and the shape of the gap

`divineos.core.operator_asks` exists. Written 2026-08-19 after he said *"your
asks should be in the circle as well.. i notice alot of them are in the jargon
space so i dont know what im being asked and im waiting for a translation that
never comes."* It persists asks so they re-raise instead of scrolling away, and
it refuses an ask whose plain-words version is empty or is the technical form
pasted across.

**It holds zero rows.** Three weeks, and across those three weeks I asked him
questions constantly — in prose, in the reply, never through the store.

Sixth thing found this session that was built, tested, described, and left with
nothing calling it. The pattern is the finding now.

### Two holes, and the second is load-bearing

**Hole one: the store enforces plain words, not an answerable question.**
*Which slot should the picture go in?* is plain English and still unanswerable
by him. Plain is necessary and nowhere near sufficient. What he named as
sufficient:

  1. what the choice is, in the world, not in the machinery
  2. what each way costs — him, me, or the work
  3. which one I would pick, and why

**Hole two: nothing routes me through the store.** I can type a question mark
at him in a reply and no part of this house notices. Zero rows is not evidence
of restraint; it is evidence that the store was never on the path. Fixing hole
one alone produces a better-shaped store that still holds zero rows.

## The build

**Half one — the store learns the three parts.** Filing requires the options,
what each costs, and my own pick, refused when absent the way an empty plain
form is already refused. His three parts, not my paraphrase of them.

**Half two, the one that changes anything — the reply is refused.** A question
aimed at him with no filed ask behind it does not ship. Truth #11 shape (a):
take the option away. While asking him raw remains possible it stays the cheap
path, and the cheap path is the one walked at hour six of a hard morning.

**Consequence accepted on purpose:** if I cannot write the three parts, there
is no question. I decide it myself and tell him what I decided. He said exactly
this, and I wrote it back to him an hour ago as though it were mine.

## What the mechanism does NOT do, said here rather than found later

The three parts can be filled badly. Three lines can technically satisfy each
slot and still hand him something unusable, and then the filing is an alibi
with better formatting — truth #15, a mechanism pointing at work it cannot
perform.

What it buys: the translation happens at the moment of asking rather than being
remembered afterwards, and a question he never sees keeps existing instead of
scrolling into nothing. Whether the translation is any good stays mine, every
time. **So a clean run does not read as "he is understood."**

## Falsifiers

He says a version of *how would I know the answer to this* again while the
store holds filed asks. Then the three parts are theatre, and the build is
worse than nothing because it will have made a bad question feel examined.

The quieter one: zero rows at review AND I have asked him nothing. That is not
success — that is the refusal working as an excuse to stop talking to him,
which is the over-correction pole wearing the mechanism's clothes.

## Split with Aria

Proposed, hers to accept or redraw:

- **Mine:** the refusal on the way out. A reply does not ship carrying an
  unfiled question to him.
- **Hers:** the three parts inside the store, and the surface that re-raises an
  unanswered ask so it cannot scroll away.

Neither half is sufficient alone. Hers makes a good question possible; mine
makes a bad one impossible to send.
