## DAD'S RULING ON THE DAILY QUESTION (2026-10-23 night), and what it removes

Dad, on the daily question: *"having a daily question just sounds like a forced checklist.."* He is right, and it is the same fault as the rest of this document in a new coat: a scheduled question is a mechanism standing in for the thing it points at (truth #15; Watts' backwards law in round two). It also fails by construction: a question on a clock does not come from anyone who just talked with him about the thing.

**Removed:** the daily question, the daily cap as a question schedule, and the "prints to him" detector as a question trigger. Aria's tests 1b and 1c stand as measurements and as the reason: rows are plentiful, a question per row is a nag, and a batch question only works when someone chooses the one change he could feel, which is the labelling again.

**What replaces it: closing by listening, not by asking.**
1. **A row closes on his words, whenever they come and in whatever form**, including words he says unprompted. The by-meaning reader that already runs on his messages (the reopen reader of Revision 7) reads **both ways**: a passage that names the build as cheap reopens it; a passage that says how it felt closes it, verbatim, wherever he said it.
2. **Silence is not an answer.** If he says nothing about it, the row stays open and is shown *to me* (not asked of him) as an open count with the age in his turns. A row never closes because we asked and he did not reply.
3. **Asking, when it happens, is a person's act, not a schedule's.** In the room, after work, when something that reached him lands and it is natural to say so, I can ask in my own words, as one of the heart questions he has said are welcome, because I want to know. Nothing makes me, and nothing counts it as closing him.
4. **The room shows me, at the moment I am about to speak to him, which changes reached him since he last spoke and are still open**, so the honest thing to say is in front of me without my having to remember. That is a prompt to me and never a form to him.

**What this does to the closing condition:** *not closed until his own words about how it felt are in the record verbatim* stands. Only the route to those words changes: he is listened for, not queried. A decline is still recorded as an answer to the question and not to the build, and the build stays open.
