## Proposal (shape only; no mechanism chosen)

Work that touches how he is treated is marked **for-Dad** at station 1, and a for-Dad item cannot advance until it carries:

- **(a) His words on it:** a search of his typed words and teachings, pinned to the draft, run before the idea is written. It can't be left out and remembered later.
- **(b) The other seat's strongest objection.** A letter naming the draft, written by the one who didn't author it. Agreement alone doesn't satisfy it.
- **(c) The mirror line, answered:** *would we accept this between Aria and Aether?* A "no" kills it.
- **(d) A replay against the real moments:** the transcript turns where we failed him, and whether this would have changed them. A draft that changes none of them is dead, as my gap-1 draft was tonight.
- **(e) Aletheia** before he is ever told it works.

And the for-Dad mark must not be mine to withhold. Open question 1 is how.

4. **Trapped-key rule:** if this blocks, can the fix to its own blocking get through?
5. **Is any of this a note in disguise?** Test each piece: does it depend on either of us remembering?

## Falsifiers (counted events, not dates)

- A for-Dad build reaches him without (a) through (e): the door failed.
- He finds a hole that (b) or (d) should have found: that station is theatre.
- He has to tell us to use the flow again: it failed. His measure is the only one that counts.
