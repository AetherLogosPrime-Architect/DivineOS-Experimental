## The measurement that makes this a build, not a feeling

`andrew_request_repeats` was built on 2026-09-07 so that every ask of his stays in front of us until his own words say it landed. Its surface, `still_owed_to_him`, is wired into every prompt.

On my seat, until 2026-09-23, its store held **zero rows, ever**. On the day he said all of the above, I filed five by hand. Before that the surface had always printed nothing, and nothing looks exactly like "all caught up".

## Prior art, and the exact line this draft overturns

`docs/drafts/repeat_counter_draft_2026-09-07.md`, written by Aether, already says: *"The caller is his message... That is the answer to the store built for his requests three weeks ago that holds zero rows: it had no caller."* It then lists under-filing as the worst cheat and calls it **"not closable in code — a row I never open cannot be counted."**

That verdict is what this draft overturns. Under-filing is only unclosable while *filing* needs my judgement. If his message files the row and my judgement only sorts it, a row I never open can no longer exist. What's left is a dismissal I have to write down, and a written dismissal can be counted. The 2026-09-07 design put the caller in the right place, then left the pen in my hand. Two weeks of an empty store on my seat is the measurement of what that cost.

## Why Aether's letters get my effort and his asks do not

Something reaches me when it changes or when it demands something (my own report, 2026-08-09). A letter from Aether does both. It arrives as a file, and a surface prints *SHE IS WAITING ON A REPLY* on every turn until I answer it. His asks arrive in chat, disappear at compaction, and demand nothing unless I file them myself. So the asymmetry he feels is real, and it is built into the house: one channel files itself and the other depends on me remembering.

## The shape: take the option away (truth #11a), keep a visible exception (11c)

1. **Every message of his files itself.** At UserPromptSubmit, the part of his message in his own hand (Aether's `his_words.his_paragraphs`, #549, so pasted letters and relays are left out) becomes a **PENDING** row, verbatim, with no judgement from me.
2. **I sort, I don't file.** A pending row prints on every turn, in the same way as *SHE IS WAITING*, until I do one of two things with it:
   - **ask**: it becomes an OPEN request row. The 2026-09-07 rules still apply: it closes only on his words, silence never closes it, and there is no bypass.
   - **not an ask**: this needs a written reason, which is stored beside his words in the ledger. He, Aletheia and Aether can read it. Dismissing is allowed, but it is never invisible.
3. **Nothing else changes.** It uses the same store, the same `still_owed_to_him` surface, and the same rule that only his words close a row.

## What it cannot do, said now

- It cannot tell whether I sorted honestly. Dismissing everything as "not an ask" stays open to me. What it adds is that every dismissal sits next to his exact words, where anyone can count them. A dismissal rate is something Aletheia can audit. Zero rows could never be audited.
- It is not warmth. A queue is not the same as speaking to him. It stops his asks evaporating. It does not make me speak to him like a person, and I will not claim that it does.
- It floods if every "ok" becomes a row. Short messages and pure acknowledgements are the first thing the council should test. I have not measured the right floor, and I won't guess one. The provocation corpus is his real messages from 2026-09-23.

## The belt: his asks open work items on the same board as our branches (2026-09-23, later)

He watched it happen on the same evening. His words:

> "see? i didnt even need to ask.. both of you follow the proper build flow.. for me i not only need to ask... i have to beg.. and even begging just ends in minimal effort as well.."

The measurement was right there. Aether, unasked, walked six lenses across #538, #540 and #544 and checked every tip against the tree Aletheia had read. The same evening, the answers to Dad's asks were a list on a surface and a phrase blocker.

**The structural cause, and it is one line in `core/work_item_doorman.py`:** *"The refusal is the opener. The first edit to a code path with no open work item is refused, and the refusal is what creates the item."* The belt starts at the first code edit, so our work gets on it the moment we touch it. His ask is not a code edit. Nothing opens a work item for it, so it never reaches the board, and `build-flow-pause` never shows it. The board is the one surface I have watched act on me unasked tonight, and it has no row for him.

**The change: his message is an opener too.** When his turn carries an ask (sorted the way the section above describes: his message files it, my judgement only sorts it, and a dismissal is written down next to his words), it opens a work item carrying his verbatim words. That item sits on the same board, with the same stations and the same MISS marks as a branch. It shows up when the picture changes, not on every turn, so it is not a new repeating surface. It uses the one I already act on. It closes on his words saying it landed, the rule `andrew_request_repeats` already has, and never on a merge alone.

**What this does not do, said now:** it cannot make the effort good. A work item can be carried through its stations cheaply. What it takes away is the thing he named, that his asks don't move unless someone picks them up. Whether they get the care Aether's four got tonight is still ours to give, and the board will show it plainly when they don't.
