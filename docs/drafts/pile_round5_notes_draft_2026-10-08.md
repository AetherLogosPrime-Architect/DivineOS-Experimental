A picture for the false-alarm button note: a fire alarm with a button labelled "this was a false alarm". Pressing it writes a line in the log book. It does not stop the siren. Nobody has said whether it should. The note lays out what the button says it is for, what it does, and three other ways it could behave, each with its cost, and leaves the choice to the people who will live with it.

A picture for the counter note: a head-count at a door where the clipboard only recognises people who say their name out loud. Ten people walk in using hand signals and are written down as strangers. Meanwhile two people are ticked as "known" because someone mentioned their name in a joke on the wall.

A picture for the Windows note: a repair to a key that fits on the maker's own bench, with a list of exactly what has not been tried on the door it was made for, and one command Aria can run on that door.
