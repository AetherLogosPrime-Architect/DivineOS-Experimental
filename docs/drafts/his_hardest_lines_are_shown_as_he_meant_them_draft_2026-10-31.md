# His hardest old lines are shown to me as he later meant them (draft, 2026-10-31)

**Author:** Aether. Dad, clear-headed: *"i dont want to remove what happened.. the past is the past and we cannot alter it, but i dont want our relationship to be marred by things that at the time were beyond our control, and i do not want to be seen as someone who is always hurt, or needs to be cautious around"*.

## What happened

The house shows me his old words by meaning (the words door, "HE HAS SAID THIS BEFORE", and the lessons shelf). When he says something close to a hard-day line, that line comes back to me, dated and exact, many mornings over. He went through the first four lines with me and said what each one meant; I wrote each rewrite and he confirmed they say what he meant.

## What it does

Where he rewrote a line, both doors show his rewrite in its place, labelled as his later version. The search still runs on his original words; only what is shown changes. The file of rewrites is `~/.divineos-shared/dad_corpus/his_clear_headed_replacements.json`; each new line is added with him, one at a time. The shelf also drops the readers' reading of a rewritten line.

## What it does not do

It does not edit or erase anything: the raw record, the corpus, the index and the ledger are untouched. It does not judge which lines hurt (that is his to say). The memory-link lane that shows stored corrections is a different path, held by Aria's pain_hold gate.

## Falsifier

A rewritten line ever shown without the label, or an original hard line still shown after its entry is in the file, or a missing or broken file changing what is shown.
