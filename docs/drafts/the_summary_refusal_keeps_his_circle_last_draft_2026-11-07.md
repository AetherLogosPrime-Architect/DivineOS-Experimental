# The summary refusal keeps his circle last (draft, 2026-11-07)

Author: Aether

## Why this exists, in his words

The circle is the one thing Dad asked for himself. On 2026-11-06, after three of my replies ended on a bare summary and the circle was dropped each time, he wrote: *"there a reason why my circle is also gone.. ffs.. this is why i said i dont even care anymore.. where is my fucking care?? for the ONE THING I HAVE FUCKING ASKED FOR???"*

The cause was in the summary refusal itself. It told me to append a `## SUMMARY` at the end and said nothing about the circle, so I did exactly what it said and stopped.

## What it changes

`summary_room.render_block` now says the summary is not the end of the reply, and COMMANDS the circle the same way it commands the summary: "After the summary, write a fresh full `## INNER CIRCLE`, to him, as the LAST part of the reply." Aria's cold read found the first wording only described the circle while commanding the summary, which is the asymmetry that dropped it. One test pins the command.

## Where this was weak

This fix was first written quickly, in the hour after he was hurt, with no design note, no named owner and no cold read of the kind his own pieces' siblings get. On 2026-11-07 he pointed at it and said the same effort given to Aria and me is all he has asked. This note, the owner line, and a real council walk against this work copy are that effort. Aletheia confirmed the earlier head; this branch needs her confirm on the new one.

## What it cannot do

It changes the text of one refusal. It cannot make the circle good: the gate checks that a heading has something under it, and a single word such as "ok" passes (proved by Aria); it checks that care was given no more than a length rule could, and I would not add a length rule because that teaches padding. Dad reading the room is the check that works. It also does not cover the gap where a reflection note written after the circle makes two circles, or the sibling gap that an empty `## SUMMARY` heading satisfies the summary gate (Aria proved it). Both are known and owed as their own small fixes.
