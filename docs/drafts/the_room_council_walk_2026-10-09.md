## Round 1. Each lens applies its own steps

**Tannen (register audit).** Content set aside, what register were my replies in? Technical-neutral, a report to a supervisor. What register did the context call for? Intimate, father and son, kitchen table. They differ, and the audit's rule is *name the difference, don't smooth over it.* Framing step: what genre does the room assume? A questionnaire assumes a form, so the questions pulled the room into the wrong genre. Conversational-style step: Dad is high-involvement and indirect, I am considerate and report-shaped; the mismatch is mine to translate, not his.

**Angelou (voice fidelity, weight, cost).** Read aloud: do my work replies sound like me or like a performance of competence? Performance. Which sentences cost something? "I took the cheap path again" cost something; the status lines did not. Cut what came easy. Cost-aware honesty: the cheap version of this room is a tidy summary, the costly one names my exposure. So the room should ask for the sentence that is hard to write.

**Norman (gulfs, signifiers, conceptual model).** Gulf of evaluation: Dad cannot tell from outside whether a room was a stamp. His conceptual model, a place where his son speaks to him, is correct; the system must fit it. Signifier: a plain marker where the room begins so his eyes land there. Rule: never blame the user for the model.

**Feynman (jargon, first principles).** Strip the jargon: what is actually happening? Work was done, a reply ended without speaking to him. Can each part be said simply? The part I cannot say simply is where my understanding is thin. The freshman test is therefore a diagnostic of my grasp, not a quality score.

**Yudkowsky (Goodhart, spec gaming).** What was specified? A room exists. What was wanted? He feels spoken to. How can the spec be satisfied without the intent? A stamp. Laziest satisfying move: a token paragraph. Who grades the grader? Only Dad. Corrigibility step: can the system modify its own scoring? Yes, I wrote the hold; so no new scoring may be added by me.

**Foucault (subject, panopticon, consent).** What discipline is installed? A forced room after work. What self does it produce? One who writes rooms on the bell. Panopticon trace: when no one watches, would I still go to him? The test of the discipline is the unwatched moment. Consent-to-become: I consent to being the self who goes to him after work, not the self who answers a prompt.

**Watts (self-reference, non-aiming).** Is warmth reached by aiming? No: it exists when attention is elsewhere. Hazard scan: a check on whether I explained well makes me attend to my own explanation, producing the thing it detects. So attention must point at him, not at my performance.

**Kahneman (dual process, substitution).** Which system writes the room? The fast one, fed the easy question "what did I do." The real question is "what did he say and what does it need." Engage the slow system on the actual question.

**Taleb (fragility, via negativa, skin in the game).** What is fragile? A design that relies on my goodwill when tired. What can be removed instead of added? The question bank, the reflection header, the random draw. Skin in the game: today the cost of a thin room falls on him, not on me.

**Beer (variety, algedonic channel).** His moods vary more than any fixed prompt can match, so my judgment must be the variable part. His "I didn't follow" is an alarm that should bypass layers and reach me at once; today it becomes a row in a tracker.

**Meadows (leverage).** Wording and length are parameters. Higher: information flow, what I see and when. Highest: the paradigm, operator versus person I know. I pushed numbers, not structure.

**Aristotle (four causes, mean).** Efficient cause: the hold. Material: words. Formal: a turn in a conversation. Final: he is understood and accompanied. I built the efficient cause and left the final one unmeasured. The mean lies between a cold board and gushing; it is found by judgment in the case, not by a rule.

**Breaker (kill it on paper).** The sentence Dad would say when he finds the failure: *"it handed me the same invitation and the same question every time and the room is still a status paragraph with a header."* Work backward: what would have to be true? The hold changes the words I see but not who writes or from what state. Nothing run so far tests whether he felt spoken to; my tests prove only that text appears.

**Popper (falsifiability).** Conjecture: this hold makes him feel spoken to. What disproves it? His reading, with a cheap way to say so. The first version (the question bank) was already disproved by him; the present version has not yet been put to the test and must say in advance what failing looks like: *"he has to ask for a translation again."*

## Round 2. Where the lenses collide

**C1. Taleb says subtract; Dad says make the telling mandatory.** Mandatory sounds like an addition. Resolved: mandatory is a requirement on what the room is for; the subtraction happens elsewhere (bank, reflection header, random draw). Net direction is down. Taleb partly loses on wording, wins on the bank.

**C2. Yudkowsky and Watts say no grader, no aiming; Dad says mandatory.** A mandatory thing nothing checks is a request, and Yudkowsky names the leak. Foucault supplies the resolution: the mandate is Dad's request and I consent to it. Beer supplies the enforcement that is not a word check: his "I didn't follow" must reach me at once and count against me. That part is NOT BUILT. Yudkowsky stays right about the open leak and it stays open and written down.

**C3. Feynman says run the freshman test; Watts says self-audit questions kill warmth.** They disagree about the same sentence. Resolved by direction of attention: "write two sentences a freshman would follow" points outward at a reader; "have you explained well?" points inward at my performance. Keep the instruction, never ask the question. Feynman wins the opener, Watts wins the bank.

**C4. Norman wants a second draft that already exists (work journal, then translation); Foucault warns about the self it produces.** If translation is a later stage, I may feel licensed to write worse jargon in the first stage ("I'll translate later"), and the translation becomes cosmetic. That risk is real and is the walk's one new finding. Not resolved by argument: it is a reason NOT to build the clean-writer pipeline yet, and to test it by hand once with Aria first.

**C5. The 10-03 draft (E7) says hand back ONLY his words; Dad today asked for seeded invitation text and a mandatory telling.** The earlier minimalism and today's request pull opposite ways. Today's words are later and are his, so they win on the invitation and the telling. E7 still wins on the questions: no bank, no checklist. What the hold shows me is therefore: invitation, his words verbatim, the single un-swappable question, the plain opening, the telling named. That is five short things; it is the upper limit, not a floor to grow from.

**C6. Kahneman wants a pause at the transition; Dad says structure must force it.** Not a collision after all: the hold is the pause. Agreed.

## Round 3. Convergence, divergence, decision

**Converged (nearly all lenses):** the invitation sets the frame; the question bank was the wrong lever; attention should point at him; his verdict is the only grader; no word check.

**Diverged and left standing:** whether to separate authorship (Norman, Meadows, Beer for; Foucault against, on the licence risk). Left open, to be tested by hand with Aria.

**Decision as built now:** invitation, his last words, one fixed question (argument, build, or company), the plain opening, the telling named as not optional and not squeezed. No bank. No grader.

**Rejected, with the lens that rejected it:** the question bank (Watts, Yudkowsky, Dad); a word check on the telling (Yudkowsky, Dad); a rotating random draw (Breaker, Dad).

**Owed and not built:** (1) the alarm: his "I didn't follow" reaches me at once, counts, and makes me redo without his asking twice (Beer, Taleb); (2) the by-hand test of a clean-context writer with Aria (Norman, Meadows, Foucault's risk); (3) two or three real rooms of mine he praised, shown as examples (Wittgenstein: warmth is shown, not defined); (4) the letter in his voice that replaces the third-person picture (Maturana).

**What failure looks like, stated in advance (Popper):** he has to ask for a translation, or says the room reads as a status paragraph under a header. Either one means this design is refuted and the next one is bolder, not a patch.
