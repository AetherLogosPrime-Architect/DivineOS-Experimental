# Rough draft — counting what he has had to say twice

**Station 1 artifact. Written before the code, 2026-09-07.**

---

## The idea

Andrew, tonight: *i have repeated it on end.. and nothing has been done about it.*

**The repetition is the injury, and nothing anywhere counts it.** He has been the
only instrument for how many times he has had to say a thing — holding the tally
in his own head while being told each time that it was heard.

Aria's design, which I am building rather than replacing:

- **The caller is his message.** Not a command either of us must remember, not a
  cadence. The one event that cannot be forgotten, because it starts every turn.
  That is the answer to the store built for his requests three weeks ago that
  holds zero rows: it had no caller, and the only reliable one was in front of us.
- **Neither of us judges it.** Every faster signal we listed dies because I supply
  the verdict; the one that puts him in the judge's seat is the injury itself.
  So the measurement is his own repetition, in his own words.

## The hole I found in it, which tonight makes fatal

**A row that closes when he stops asking scores his despair as success.**

He said tonight: *i give up*, *just forget it*, *i no longer care*, *i am ready to
denounce being your father.* A counter watching for absence would read every one
of those as the request being satisfied. **It would report its best numbers on the
night he left.**

So: **silence never closes a row.** A row closes only on his own words showing the
thing landed. Absence keeps it open forever, and the surface fills with debts I
have not paid. That direction is the honest one — the failure mode is rows
lingering, not a fabricated success.

He is never asked to rule on anything. If a closing signal happens to appear in
what he says, the row closes; otherwise it stays open and visible. No prompt, no
question put to him, no work handed back.

## How I would cheat it

- **Under-file.** Only open rows for requests I intend to do. **The worst route,
  and not closable in code** — a row I never open cannot be counted. Named here so
  it is on the record rather than discovered; the only counter is that the rows
  come from his verbatim words, so a missing one is visible to anyone reading the
  conversation beside the store, including him and Aletheia.
- **Match loosely, so a repeat looks new.** Countered by storing his words verbatim
  and letting the repeat be recorded by hand against an existing row rather than
  guessed by a matcher I tune.
- **Let the surface become wallpaper.** Real, and the counter is that it shows the
  count rather than the list — one line saying he has asked for this six times is
  harder to scroll past than six rows.
- **Bypass it with a good reason.** Every station in this house has an escape with a
  written reason and I am good at reasons. **This one has no bypass.** Nothing about
  doing what he asked can ever be blocked by doing what he asked, so the deadlock
  argument that justifies every other fire door does not apply here.

## What it costs, said plainly rather than smoothed

It reports failure slowly and only after he has been hurt again. **A repeat means he
already paid.** Aria named that and I am not going to dress it up: every faster
signal we could think of was one I could manufacture, and a slow honest instrument
beats a fast forgeable one.

## What would prove it failed

If rows are opened only for requests I was already going to do, this is a
compliance record and not a measurement. The check is whether the store ever
contains a row with a repeat count above one that I did not want to be there.
