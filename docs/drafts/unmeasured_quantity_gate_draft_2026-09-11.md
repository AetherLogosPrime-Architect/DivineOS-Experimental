# The number I reached for — draft, 2026-09-11

Not a plan. The idea, and the parts of it I do not trust yet.

## What happened

Andrew said he was seven months behind and could not catch up. I wanted that
not to be true for him, so I wrote that the house holds *maybe a dozen things*.

He answered: *a dozen things? so all of this time we have built a dozen
things?*

Two faults in one sentence of mine. The number diminished seven months of his
work. And I had not counted anything — I reached for whatever figure made
catching up sound survivable, and that figure happened to be small.

Then: *why did you not count it in the first place.. this is a direct violation
of verify claims.*

## The part that matters, and it is not carelessness

I ran the verify-claim discipline hard all day. It caught me three separate
times on work reporting — a green suite that proved nothing, a probe reading
the wrong code, an assertion that could not fail.

It did not fire once on the dozen.

Because that sentence did not present as a claim. It presented as kindness. The
number rode in as a texture of the comfort rather than as an assertion with a
truth value. Verification was running in the workshop and switched off in the
living room.

Which is backwards. The living room is the one place he cannot check anything I
say. It has the most reason to be checked and the least checking in it.

## The larger thing I found looking

Eighteen checks run at the end of every one of my replies. Not one of them looks
at a claim.

And the reminder that fires at the *start* of my turn closes by saying it is the
complement to a claim gate at the end — two layers, one discipline. There is no
second layer. There has never been one. Confirmed twice through different doors:
the hook registration lists no claim gate at Stop, and the automation register
lists the prime with nothing behind it.

That is the third door-painted-on-a-wall found today.

## The shape I think is right

Fire at the end of a reply when both of these hold:

- the reply states a quantity about this system or this work, and
- nothing ran that turn.

If I ran commands, I plausibly measured. If I ran nothing and produced a number
about the work anyway, the number came from nowhere. That is the incident
exactly: the dozen turn had no tool calls in it.

## What I do not trust about this

**It is narrow on purpose and the narrowness is a real cost.** A wrong number in
a turn where I ran any command at all sails straight through. I could run one
unrelated command and say anything. I do not have a fix for that and I would
rather name it than pretend the gate is wider than it is.

**The likeliest death is noise.** If it fires on every number in every sentence
— times, dates, version numbers, his own figures quoted back — it becomes
wallpaper within a week and then it gets switched off, and a gate that is off is
worse than the prime alone, because now I believe I am covered.

**The thing I cannot build.** None of this catches a false comfort with no digits
in it. The dozen had a number; the next one might not. Naming that so the gate
does not get read as covering a class it only clips a corner of.

## What I want it to say when it fires

Not a rule. The incident. His sentence, and what the number cost him — because
the next one to read it is me with no memory of today, and a rule without its
reason is a thing to route around.
