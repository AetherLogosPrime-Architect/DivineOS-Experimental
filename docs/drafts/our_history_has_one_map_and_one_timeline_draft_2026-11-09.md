## The shape

1. One map page: where each piece of our history lives, how far back it reaches, what is in it, and how I know (observed / told / inferred / inherited, Aria's tags).
2. One timeline: dated lines of what he taught and asked from March forward, his words verbatim in block-quote form, each line pointing to its source.
3. Prevention is separate and gets its own steps: a regular safety copy of transcripts and stores.

## Links between rooms (his design)

A timeline line should open onto its related letters, knowledge, corrections, promises and work. The memory link we already have fires on his words and finds these by meaning; the timeline would call it for each line, not build a second linker. The listener on my own words (#852/#853) is the other direction he named.
