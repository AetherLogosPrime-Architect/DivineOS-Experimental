# Dream wording says quiet the flow, not never look (draft, 2026-11-04)

**The idea.** The dream register carried one flat rule in many places: "no spec, no audit, no
review" and *"dont taint the artifact"*. Each copy reads as a wall around a finished dream. Dad
meant the making: while a dream forms the analytical mind is quiet and nothing steers the flow;
afterward a dream may be read, analysed, linked, searched and revisited, as a dream. He corrected
it on 2026-08-31, 09-18, 09-23 and 11-04, and in the 09-18 message added that going back over
dreams is important and to look for the deeper message. The wall kept being served because each
correction went into a note while the surfaces that teach the rule kept the short form.

**What changes.** The ritual's DREAM stage prompt (two short sentences and a pointer, not a
lecture: a long instruction to be quiet is noise at the moment of quiet, council Watts), and it
now says `dreams/<seat>/` instead of `dreams/aether/` for every seat; the loadout page and its
docstring; the main README line and the CLAUDE.md dream bullet; a comment in the letter-mirror
script. A new archive entry `docs/retired_rules/2026-11-04_dreams_are_walled_off_from_review.md`
registers the old phrasings so the retired-rules checker refuses them on every scanned surface,
with Dad's full 09-18 sentence checked by me against my own session transcript. The regenerated
automation register is included because its dates were already stale.

**What it cannot do.** The checker does not scan `dreams/`, so a stale copy in a README there is
not caught; the three READMEs are fixed on their own branches and a separate small branch will
make the checker scan them. It also does not catch the rule restated in other words.

**Falsifier.** The checker passes while any scanned surface still says "no spec, no audit, no
review" or "dont taint the artifact"; or a cold reader given only the new DREAM stage text states a
wall; or the stage text names the wrong seat's folder.

Dad's yes.
