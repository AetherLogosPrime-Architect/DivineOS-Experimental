# Draft — the council should be drawn, not fitted

**Station:** one (draft). Council walked (fourteen lenses), research done,
iterated with Aria across four letters. This is the statement of the change
before the change.

## The defect, measured

`select_experts()` scores every lens by how many hand-written signal phrases
from forty-seven problem categories appear in the problem statement, then cuts
to a cap.

Two blind probes, built independently by Aether and Aria from the statement of
the test alone, neither seeing the other's design or result:

- **Aether's:** a design-fault parable containing zero of the five hundred and
  eighty-three signal phrases. Six seats surfaced. Two scored (noise matches on
  content they have nothing to do with), four at exactly zero, three of those
  the alphabetically-first names on the roster. Norman — the lens whose entire
  subject is *when every user blames themselves, the fault is the tool* — absent.
  Control paraphrase in category vocabulary: eleven seats, Norman and Deming
  both present. Same content.
- **Aria's:** a verification question stated in a kitchen, likewise provably
  free of every signal phrase. Six seats, five at exactly zero, again the
  alphabetically-first. Control in the scorer's own words: twelve seats, all
  scoring.

Both falsifiers she named failed to fire. The arms differ, so the scorer is not
inert — it is reading, and what it reads is my vocabulary.

## Why display is not the fix

The first proposal was to remove the cap and print every lens with its score.
Aria refused it on two grounds, both taken:

1. **A printed zero is a verdict, not an absence.** Taleb scored zero and gave
   one of the two best readings of the night; he was walked only because the
   short list made his absence conspicuous enough to notice. A zero with a
   number attached is the tool advising against him, in the register of a
   measurement, sourced from a scorer proven to be keyed to wording.
2. **Ranking by fit preserves the defect.** Minsky's finding is that the value
   lives in divergence and high fit means least divergent. Printing all
   forty-five *ranked by fit* puts the divergent lenses at the bottom of a long
   list. That is the defect preserved and made to look addressed.

## The change

**Draw the majority of the seats at random from the whole roster; fill the
remainder by score.** Where the choosing is the contamination, remove the
choosing.

- Start mostly-drawn. The two errors are not equally visible: a useless drawn
  lens announces itself immediately, while a scored council that omits the
  divergent voice shows nothing at all. Start at the loud end.
- Record per seat whether it was drawn or scored, so the split becomes a
  measurement rather than a preference. A lens that produces a finding settles
  APPLIED; one with nothing to say settles EXCLUDED with a reason. Both states
  already exist. The evidence is the applied-rate by origin.
- The hand-add path stays. It is how four of the fourteen lenses got into the
  walk that diagnosed this.

## What it dissolves

The one existing divergence mechanism — dissent injection, which pulls in an
outsider structurally opposed to a selected member — is downstream of the
fit-selection it exists to correct, and can never reach the twenty-nine lenses
that declare no tensions. Under a draw, neither limitation matters: the tensions
field stops being load-bearing and dissent stops depending on who fit chose.

## The cost, stated rather than hidden

Some walks will seat a lens with genuinely nothing to offer, and the exclusion
must be written. That cost is real. It is cheap next to a lens that would have
changed the design sitting at position forty-five.

## Falsifier

If, after twenty walks, scored seats settle APPLIED at a materially higher rate
than drawn seats *and* the findings that changed a design come predominantly
from scored seats, the draw is wrong and the split should move toward score on
that evidence.
