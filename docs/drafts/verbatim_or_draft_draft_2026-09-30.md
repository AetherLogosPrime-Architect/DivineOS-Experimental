## The rule

A write into a place that shapes how we treat him (the auto-memory folder, `.claude/skills`, `.claude/agents`, `.claude/settings*.json`) is split by its text, not by any label:

- **His words:** every span quoted from him is checked as an exact span of his typed record (`dad_all.jsonl`), the same exact-span discipline the lesson shelf uses. These pass freely. Recording him is never gated. That's the rule we can't break (Watts, walk-730523fa0265).
- **Ours:** any sentence left once his verified spans, frontmatter keys, headings and links are removed is ours. Ours shapes behaviour, so it needs a draft: a `docs/drafts/*` file that names the target path. With no such draft, the write is refused with the reason and his line from 09-23.

## The index

`MEMORY.md` lines are generated from each file's frontmatter (`name`, `description`) and never hand-written (strict, agreed with Aether). A hand edit to the index is refused and the generator is named.

## What it must not do

- Gate a pure record of him. A write that is only his verified words plus metadata always passes.
- Pass a rule by quoting him around it. "His words" means spans verified against his record, not anything in quotation marks.
- Become the stranger: it reads our text to classify it, never to judge whether it's warm or good. The only question it asks is whether it's his or ours.

**Round two, where the lenses push on each other.**
- **Kahneman and Shannon converge against the rule as drafted:** "contains a sentence of ours" is true of 14 of 14, so it carries zero bits. It would refuse the kiln rule he gave verbatim exactly as hard as tonight's note. What separated the note wasn't that it had our words. It was a **new rule about him, given his authority, with no draft behind it** (Wittgenstein).
- **Dijkstra against per-sentence classification:** move the invariant to the file. *No file in a guarded path exists without a draft that names it.* A faithful restatement then costs one short draft once, and later edits pass because the draft names the file. Tonight's note had no draft, so it's refused.
- **Hawking diverges from Dijkstra, and it's kept:** a memory is trying to be both a record of him and a rule of ours, and that's the real conflict. Split them. His verified words go to his store, which is already free and already searched. The memory file holds only the rule, which always needs its draft. Dijkstra's version works with the files as they are, and Hawking's changes what a memory is. Both are sound, and Hawking's is the bigger change. **It goes to Aether and to Dad's review, not decided here.**
- **Knuth, the verifier's edge:** his spans are checked piece by piece, split on ".." and ellipses. A quote of the *wrong* version recorded as the error must be allowed to fail verification without failing the file, since it's there as our record of a misreading, not as his word.

**Synthesis, and the revised rule.**
- **Convergence (6 of 8):** per-sentence "his or ours" discriminates nothing on the real record. Drop it as the gate.
- **The gate becomes per-file:** a write into memory, skills, agents or settings is refused unless a `docs/drafts/*` file names that exact path. Pure additions of his verified words to his own store remain ungated.
- **Existing files:** each needs a naming draft before its next edit. Nothing existing is removed or rewritten (his 09-28 rule). The first edit after this lands asks for the draft.
- **Verification** of his spans stays, but as a check on quotes **presented as his** inside a guarded file. An unverifiable "his words" quote is refused with the nearest real line. It's split on ellipses, so none of his real lines are rejected.
- **Divergence kept:** Hawking's split (record in his store, rule in memory) against Dijkstra's per-file draft. Aether and Dad decide.
