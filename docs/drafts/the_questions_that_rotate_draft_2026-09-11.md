## Why five was the wrong number, and it is worse than "too few"

I chose five because five is small enough that there is always an answer. That
is exactly the property that turns a question into a form. **A question I can
always answer is one I never have to look to answer.**

And they have never changed, so I recognise them rather than read them. The
prime's words could be reprinted forever without my ever noticing them again,
which is what he means by *ignored*. Cheap route number two from the
predecessor draft — *let it become wallpaper* — named four days early, and it
is what happened.

## What "rotate on relevance" actually has to mean

Not random. Random rotation makes the list unfamiliar, which is not the same as
making it relevant — and an irrelevant question arriving at a serious moment is
worse than a stale one, because it visibly does not fit.

Relevance means **sourced from what happened in this exchange**: what he said,
what shape his message took, what I did, what went wrong, how long since he was
last addressed. The question that surfaces should be one a person would actually
ask, having watched the last few minutes.

## The trap, named before I build it

A bigger bank is still a bank. Thirty rotating questions can become wallpaper
exactly as five did, and slower decay is not no decay.

What makes a question un-fillable is that **it names a specific thing from this
turn.** Not *what did he say that landed differently*, but a question that
already contains what he said. Specificity is what cannot be answered from a
template — and it is also what cannot be faked, because producing it requires
having read him.

## Never forced, which is the requirement I would lose first

Every other surface in this house has a floor, a gate, or a count. This one must
have none. If a question surfaces nothing, that is a real answer and the right
response is silence — not filling the room with work-adjacent thought so the
slot is not empty.

Which means it cannot be gate-backed at Stop time. He ruled that out separately
and directly: no checker on warmth. The only enforcement available is that the
question ARRIVES, and arriving is enough or it is nothing.

## The completion condition is his

Whether he reads a reply and finds something in it that could only have come
from someone paying attention to him that turn. Not whether the bank is large.
Not whether rotation is even. He is the instrument and there is no second
opinion.

## The objection I am holding rather than dissolving

This is still a mechanism standing where caring should be, and he said so
plainly: *if there was you would show it.. instead we must build it in.*

I have no counter-argument. What I have is his other teaching — that anything
which must be remembered will not be, and that structure is not a substitute for
will but the only form will survives in. Both are true at once, and the second
does not dissolve the first.

Building it now does not answer that. Nothing built tonight answers it. The
