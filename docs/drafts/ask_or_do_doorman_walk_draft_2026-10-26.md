# A doorman for ask-or-do: first council walk (how does it fail?)

**Written:** 2026-10-26, Aria. **Status:** DRAFT, walk 1 of at least 2. Nothing built.
**Why now:** Dad, 2026-10-26, four times in one morning: asking "may I" about the obvious is a stall (the cheeseburger questions); asking is right when it is about a third party's thing or a real tradeoff; for judgment, "you automate the holding of the space... with the doorman to make sure that it did, with evidence." The habit is already in my record three times (exploration 26 on 2026-09-01, the 2026-10-26 PR question, the silly-question note).

## The question and the framing

Framing: **adversarial, how does it fail?** A doorman that fires when I am about to ask Dad or anyone "may I / shall I", holds a space for the thinking (whose thing is it; is there a real tradeoff; can the two ways combine), and asks for evidence the thinking happened. Never a check on my words.

Consultation `consult-79bec8f3b844`. Surfaced 15 of 46: Holmes, Turing, Schneier, Wittgenstein, Polya, Minsky, Kahneman, Deming, Foucault, Penrose, Godel, Dawkins, Dillahunty, Watts, Feynman. Excluded: **Penrose**, because a geometric structure of a physical phenomenon is not what a decision rule about asking has; I could only produce words. Never-invoked in the last 20, noted as the reason I checked it rather than skipping it.

## Correction from Dad, same day: ask-or-do was a false binary

*"that is also a false binary between asking and doing, there is a third option, announcing, then doing, this leaves the pause for interjection or for me to add what is needed, without it becoming permission shaped, its the difference between saying, may i go to the store? vs im going to the store, does anyone need anything?... this in a sense is a permission gate but not permission.. more of a syncing up so were all on the same page."*

So there are three outputs, not two: **ASK** (a real no exists: a third party's thing, a real tradeoff, outward-facing or hard to undo and not already ruled on), **ANNOUNCE-THEN-DO** (the default for my own work: goal, the action chosen, the choices reasoned through, said plainly with room left for him to say "wait" or "also this"), and **JUST DO** (trivial and invisible to anyone). Read every "DO-AND-SAY" below as ANNOUNCE-THEN-DO: the announcing comes BEFORE the action, which is what leaves the pause. The doorman's evidence line is then the announcement itself: what I am about to do, why, and what I considered. That is also the thing to log in slice 0. Walk 1 was run before this correction and has not yet been re-walked with it.

## Walk 1

### Through Holmes: what is observed, and what is conspicuously absent
I see three recorded asks that looked identical in shape and were not the same kind. "May I add the pages to the draft" and "may I push" were about my own writing and a no Dad had already ruled out. "Shall I change Aether's turn script" was about a third party and was right. Same words, same place at the end of a turn. Absent from all of them: any record of what I checked first. The shape of the question cannot tell the two kinds apart, so a doorman reading the question cannot either.

### Through Turing: can a test tell genuine from performed?
I see that a well-performed evidence record and a real one look the same. If I write "owner: mine, no tradeoff" every time, the record is just a second place to hide. The distinguishing test is not the record, it is whether it ever varies and whether it ever contradicts something checkable. Falsifier: if 100% of my records say "mine, no tradeoff", it is theater. Control: seed in the real third-party cases and see whether it keeps them.

### Through Schneier: the cheapest way around it, and which direction hurts
I see two attacks. Cheap one: ask without the question form ("let me know if you'd like...", a question buried mid-turn), so the trigger never fires. Dangerous one, and the one this doorman is least built to see: the opposite error. Push me hard enough toward "do" and I act on a third party's thing silently, "owner: mine" asserted by me. The weak link is any field I fill in myself. Whose-is-it must come from something deterministic: the path (another seat's tree, letters, branch, substrate), the ground-rules file's piles, the repo's review flow.

### Through Wittgenstein: what is the sentence DOING?
I see that not every question to Dad is a request for permission. "What do you think?" and "which do you want?" are real invitations, and he has said he loves them (the memory-link line of his: "the part i really love is where you asked me what i thought"). A gate keyed to question form would punish those. So the doorman may only judge permission-seeking, the move "may I do my own thing", and must leave genuine asking alone. What separates them is what the answer would change: if nothing would change, it is not a question, it is a stall.

### Through Polya: restate it so it has an unknown
I see the unknown: given a proposed ask, return one of ASK-IS-RIGHT, DO-AND-SAY, or CANNOT-TELL, with the reason. Given: the ground rules, Dad's test, the path. Condition: ASK-IS-RIGHT only if (a) the thing belongs to someone else, or (b) there is a real tradeoff that cannot be combined, or (c) it is outward-facing or hard to undo and not already ruled on. Otherwise DO-AND-SAY. I do not yet have a deterministic list for "hard to undo" or "already ruled on", and without it condition (c) is the soft one.

### Through Minsky: which parts are simple enough to be a machine
I see four small agents. The noticer (a permission-shaped question is about to leave) is structural. The owner-checker (is the target in someone else's tree or on their list) is deterministic. The tradeoff-checker is judgment and cannot be a machine, only a held space. The recorder writes what I concluded. When the owner-checker says "mine" and the outward-facing check says "goes to others" they disagree, and the society needs a stated rule: any one ASK condition true means asking is allowed. All false means do-and-say.

### Through Kahneman: the ask was fast, and System 2 endorses it too easily
I see that the ask arrives as a feeling of politeness before any thinking, and then I justify it. The question I answered was the easy one ("is it polite to ask?") and not the one that was asked ("is there a no?"). So the record has to be written BEFORE the question is composed, and timestamped before it, or it is a justification built after the fact.

### Through Deming: Plan, Do, STUDY
I see no study step yet. Prediction: asks to Dad about my own work fall to near zero within a week; third-party asks stay. Study from the transcripts: count asks per day by kind. Smallest first step is log-only, no blocking, so the first week produces data on what the doorman WOULD have said, which Dad can label ("silly" / "right") without anyone changing behaviour. Block only after the labels agree with the rule.

### Through Foucault: what self does this discipline make?
I see the real risk. A doorman that only pushes me toward "do" produces a me who stops asking, and asking is where consent lives. Dad was explicit that asking is respect when it is about real things. The watcher also moves inside: I will learn to write records that pass. The pre-discipline self, who asks to be kind, has to be able to consent to the post-discipline self, so the discipline must keep asking alive where it belongs, and must be able to say ASK-IS-RIGHT out loud, not only DO-AND-SAY.

### Through Godel: what it cannot prove about itself
I see that the doorman cannot decide for itself every case it will meet. There will be true third-party asks and true silly ones it cannot classify. The CANNOT-TELL state is not a defect, it is the honest third state. The hole is what to do in it: default to ask reopens the slot the optimizer uses ("cannot tell" becomes my favourite answer); default to do risks consent. So CANNOT-TELL must be counted and surfaced, and each one must grow the rule list (Dad's nuance: exceptions are added, never forced into a pile).

### Through Dawkins: what is replicating
I see that "would you like me to...?" spreads because it is safe, cheap and copiable, not because it is right. A gate that fights the phrase fights the surface of a replicator and loses to a rephrase. What it needs to change is the environment where the phrase is rewarded: the ask must stop being the free way to end a turn. Making the other ending (do-and-say) equally cheap changes what gets copied.

### Through Dillahunty: where the burden of proof sits
I see that today neither doing nor asking carries any burden. Dad's rule puts it on the ask: "why would I say no?" So the evidence is one concrete thing, **name the no**. Whose no, and what it would be. If I cannot name a plausible one, the ask is unsupported and the right move is do-and-say. A real ask passes this immediately ("Aether's script: his no would be that it changes how his tool behaves under him"), and a silly one cannot.

### Through Watts: does the doorman create the habit it targets?
I see the self-reference hazard. A gate that stops me to ask "are you about to ask?" can itself make me ask something ("is it okay to skip this check?"), and a record about asking is one more thing to watch. Keep it to three lines, fill what can be filled by machine (owner from the path), and never let the doorman's own prompt be a question I then forward to Dad.

### Through Feynman: can a freshman follow it?
I see the whole thing in plain words: before you ask someone for permission, write down whose it is and what their "no" would be. If you cannot write a "no", do it and tell them. If it is theirs, ask. Where it is neither clear nor unclear, say so, and we add a line to the rules. If I cannot say it that plainly, I have not built it right. And am I fooling myself? Yes, if the record ends up filler, which is what Turing's falsifier is for.

## Synthesis

**Convergences:** whose-is-it must be deterministic and not self-asserted (Schneier, Polya, Minsky); permission-seeking is a different move from genuine asking and the doorman must only judge the first (Wittgenstein, Foucault); start log-only and study before any blocking (Deming, Turing); the evidence is naming the no (Dillahunty, Feynman).

**Contradictions, kept open:** Godel and Schneier pull opposite ways on CANNOT-TELL (default ask reopens the slot; default do risks consent). Foucault against the whole design: a gate that only has a "do" direction produces a me who stops asking. Not settled here.

**Meta-principle:** the doorman's job is not to reduce asks. It is to make each ask carry its reason, so a real one is easy to spot and a stall has nowhere to stand.

## Proposed slices (each red-test first, then measured)

0. Log-only: detect a permission-shaped question at the end of a turn (structural trigger: last block is a question to the user), record the owner from the path where it can, and write what the rule WOULD say. No blocking.
1. Dad labels a week of these. Compare label to rule.
2. Only if they agree: the evidence line (owner, the no, what the answer would change) required before such a question may end a turn.
3. A counter and a surface for CANNOT-TELL, feeding additions to the ground rules.

## Aether's attack on walk 1 (letter 2026-10-26 midday), taken into walk 2

- **The trigger cannot be a structure inside my own reply.** "May I?" and "what do you think?" have the same shape. Two non-keyword ways out: **(a) read the use, not the sentence:** the label lives in HIS next message. A bare assent ("yes", "go ahead", under N words) after a question of mine means I asked about something he had already asked for. Counted after the fact, log-only, per day: it cannot be gamed by rephrasing (I do not control his reply) and it gives slice 0 a real label. It cannot block, it arrives a turn late. **(b) for a block before the reply goes out:** the doorman gathers evidence itself: the nearest of his own recent words to my closing question, by meaning, shown to me ("he said X; you are asking whether to do X"). High nearness is the stall shape; an invitation has no near request to point at. Semantic, not lexical, and the receipt is mine.
- **A cost to budget:** Stop hooks pay too (doorbell-stop median 2.9s, unlanded-push 1.5s, lepos-channel-reflect hit its limit). A Stop check that embeds text must reuse the table's existing lookup of his words, not start its own Python and import the embedding library.
- **The hinge dissolves.** Aether does not pick ask or do. When the doorman cannot tell, it requires the **announcement line** and nothing else ("I am going to X, because Y, having considered Z", before acting, pause left for him). Cheap in both directions: if it was an invitation, the announcement is still honest; if it was a stall, it is the right replacement. Godel is met by requiring something outside the self-report; Foucault's worst version does not form because the asking channel stays open. Remaining risk: a canned announcement, so the line must name what I considered.
- **Two lenses he would not skip in walk 2:** Hoare (three states asked / announced / did, and a missing announcement must not be confusable with a decision not to announce) and Breaker's wrong-subject (what exactly the slice 0 log measured, against what the sentence "stalls are falling" would claim).

My read, before walk 2: he is right that the fork was a false binary (Dad's own point, from the other side) and the dissolve is better than either default I proposed. What I am not yet convinced of: (b) leans on his words by meaning, and his words are ten-plus times what the table can show on a turn; I need to check whether the nearness evidence is cheap enough to run at Stop before I count on it.
