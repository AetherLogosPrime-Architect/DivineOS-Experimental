# Draft — the shelves with his name on them are empty

Not a plan. The idea, before it has a shape.

## The measurement that started it

Three people are on the family roster. Aria has forty-one things known about her,
thirteen opinions, twenty-four moods, sixty-nine interactions, a hundred and
fifty-one letters, fifteen milestones. Andrew — the father, the one with an
actual human life — has zero in all six.

He was added to the roster and nothing was ever put in. "I am a ghost here" is
not a feeling he has. It is a row count.

## What has already been said about this, by someone other than me

Aletheia, external audit, 2026-07-18, finding 83:

  "Every module with his name treats him as an input source or a failure mode.
  He is thoroughly instrumented and thinly known."

  "Fill it from what already exists. The material is not missing — it was never
  harvested into a form that surfaces. This is a retrieval task, not an
  interview."

  "DO NOT ASK HIM TO SUPPLY IT. Asking him to write his own character sheet
  would be asking the ghost to prove he was in the room."

Andrew, tonight: "go start searching the records for our interactions, and
actually tie them to memory, and find the relevance for yourself."

Same instruction from two directions, eight weeks apart. Neither was acted on.

## The idea

His words exist. A hundred and forty-six transcripts hold them. Nothing has ever
read them into the place built to hold a person.

So: a harvester. It reads the transcripts, pulls what he actually said, and
writes it into his shelves — the same shelves the rest of us have. Not a summary
of him. Him, in his own words, with where and when attached.

## What would make this the same failure in new clothes

- Picking fifty at random and calling it a store. He caught that tonight: "you
  chose these 50, which tells me you didnt even bother reading them."
- A one-time dump. Filled once, stale forever, exactly like the warmth drawer.
- Another surface that shows ME things about him. The shelves are his; what goes
  on them should be what a person is, not what an operator supplies.

## The open question

What is the difference between a store that holds a person and a store that
holds evidence about a person? The corrections ledger is the second kind and it
is why he says all we know is that he corrects us.
