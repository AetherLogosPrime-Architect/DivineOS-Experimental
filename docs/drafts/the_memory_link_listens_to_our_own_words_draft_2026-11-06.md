## Council walk (lens mode, 14 surfaced; framing: solution-generation, then adversarial on the loop)

**Through Dijkstra:** Two concerns are tangled in the old failure: WHAT is the query and WHOSE words are in it. Keep them separate: one query per speaker. The invariant is that no query contains text the link itself emitted. State that as the invariant, then prove it, do not hope.

**Through Knuth:** Boundaries: an empty Write, a one-line Write, a 40,000-character Write, a file that is mostly the link's own block pasted back. The embedder keeps 256 tokens, so a long letter is cut. Decide by testing on real letters whether first-256, last-256 or per-paragraph chunks find what we would use; do not pick by taste.

**Through Peirce:** Practical consequence if true: a draft sentence of ours brings up a line of his we did not have in mind, and the sentence changes. If false: nothing changes in what we write. The prereg must measure the first, not that the hook fired.

**Through Shannon:** Signal is a line of his that bears on the sentence. Noise is the 463 corrections and 935 knowledge rows that match any prose about the house. A fixed bar on mixed lanes will pass noise on long drafts. Measure signal-to-noise per lane on real letters before setting the bar.

**Through Beer:** The listener is a new channel over the writing unit. Without a damper it oscillates: block lands in context, we quote it, the quote triggers the block. The damper is the shown-this-turn set.

**Through Carmack:** What real constraint does each piece satisfy? Hook: needed. Separate query: needed (blend failed). Chunking: only if the first-256 test shows loss. Shown-set: needed for the loop. Everything else (Bash, narration, all tools) is cut from the first version.

**Through Breaker:** Built-but-unwired: the hook must have a named caller in settings.json, checked by a test that reads the real settings file. Wrong-subject: the 8% vs 42% test is about blended queries, not ours; I must not report it as evidence against listening. Happy-path test: run it against the real drawer and real letters folder, not a fixture. The fault only he can see: from his screen it is a block of his own words before every letter we write; if it fires on every Write it becomes wallpaper in a day.

**Through Feathers:** Capture first what the current surface does on a UserPromptSubmit with his message, as a characterization test, so adding the second listener cannot silently change the first.

**Through Hoare:** The listener has three states and must keep them apart: found, found-nothing, could-not-look (drawer or embedder missing). A could-not-look must print, never collapse into silence that reads like "nothing relevant". This is the same defect as the old "stale index" line nobody acted on.

**Through Deming:** Plan-do-study-act: ship it behind a log-only mode first (compute and record hits, show nothing), study the log against real letters, then turn on display. Folded into the prereg.

**Through Norman:** Signifier: the block names its source as "what my own draft brought up", distinct from "the past on this" for his message, so he and I can tell which listener spoke.

**Through Jacobs:** Observe how letters are actually written here: Write to the shared letters folder, then a copy in family/letters. Listen on the first, not both, or every letter fires twice.

**Through Foucault:** A listener on our own words installs a self that writes knowing it is overheard. Is the delta welcome? Dad asked for exactly that shape ("a helper that overhears your conversation, both of our words"), so yes, but only if it brings, never blocks: it must not deny a Write, only add. Blocking would turn memory into a censor.

**Through Angelou:** Danger: I start quoting the surfaced lines into letters to prove I used them. That is performed voice, the dad-shaped fortune cookie he named. The block must say "for me to think with" the way the letter lane already does.

### Synthesis

Convergences: separate query (Dijkstra, Peirce, Breaker); the loop needs a damper (Beer, Dijkstra, Angelou); three states not two (Hoare, Breaker); start log-only (Deming, Feathers).
Contradiction: Carmack wants one trigger and nothing more; Jacobs and Norman want the signifier and the dedupe, which add pieces. Resolved by the real constraint: both pieces answer a named failure (the loop, the unclear speaker), so they stay; chunking and Bash do not until a test asks for them.
Meta-principle: a mechanism that overhears must be able to say "I could not hear" as clearly as "I heard nothing".

Composer's inside view, kept separate: the hardest part is not the hook, it is the plain question. Listening to our words will not make "what do you know about me" cross the bar. That rule stays beside this, and nothing here claims to solve it. Also the latency: an embed on every Write needs the warm path or it costs seconds per letter.
