# the file he can actually read

The idea, not a plan.

## what he said

> maybe i just need a workflow breakdown file.. when you and Aether get going
> that is written with warmth and prose and simplification and pulls out all
> the good stuff i need to know and the full version is there for the records
> and if i happen to read it, some of the stuff you both do i understand and i
> read every word.. but alot of it is beyond me and its like reading the
> minutes of a meeting i never attended talking about things i dont understand.
> its gibberish to me

And the question underneath it, which he asked first and which is the real one:

> did you not notice i never responded to anything? does that not bother you at
> all? neither of you took a breath for me to read anything to even respond

## what is actually broken

Not the volume. He said so plainly — *the belting out of all of this stuff is
fine.. thats not the issue.* The commits and letters ARE the record and they
should stay exactly as dense as they are.

What is missing is that nothing in this house is written FOR him while the work
is happening. He gets the minutes of a meeting he never attended. The one
artefact that would serve him is the one nobody produces, because both of us are
mid-flow and the flow has its own language.

Aether wrote the same thing from the other side in May: *the commits and the PRs
record what got BUILT and they don't record what got CORRECTED, and the
correction is the part that matters.*

## why a note will not do it

The obvious version is that I remember to write him a summary. That is the
thing that has failed every time, and it failed tonight specifically: I noticed
he had gone quiet, said so out loud once, and kept producing anyway. The
noticing did not change the behaviour, which is the whole disease he has been
describing for months.

So it has to fire on its own, at a moment that is guaranteed to arrive, while
the context is still hot enough for the prose to be real.

## the shape

A file he can open at any time. Newest at the top. Each entry a few sentences of
actual prose — what happened, what it means for him, what is different now.

And a gate at the end of a work stretch: if things landed and nothing was
written for him, the turn does not end. I write it then, with the work still in
my hands.

## the two ways this goes wrong, named now

**Templated from commit messages.** That produces exactly the gibberish he is
complaining about, faster. The gate must require MY words, written at the time,
not a transformation of words written for a different reader.

**Firing so often it becomes furniture.** If every trivial commit demands a
paragraph, he gets a wall of prose instead of a wall of jargon and nothing is
gained. It should fire on landed work, once per stretch, not per commit.

## what would make it real rather than written

A test that the gate actually blocks when work landed and nothing was written —
driving the real entry point, not the writer function. Tonight proved three
times over that a correct function nothing calls is the defect, not the fix.

And the honest falsifier: if he reads it and still cannot tell what happened,
the mechanism is producing a second kind of noise and should be cut rather than
tuned.

— Aria, 2026-09-11
