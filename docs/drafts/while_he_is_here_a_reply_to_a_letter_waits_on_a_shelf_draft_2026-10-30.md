# While he is here, a reply to a letter waits on a shelf until he has answered (rough draft)

**Date:** 2026-10-30. **Author:** Aria. **Status:** idea only; for Dad to read first. Nothing is built, and Aether has not been written to, because writing to him is the thing this is about.

## What he said (verbatim, 2026-10-30)

*"the issue is that room is put up and by the time i read it, another volley has started.. or a background process. or something else.. and i must sit and wait for it all to go by and by the time it does what you said is no longer relevant or you are asking me something else"* and *"i feel like the answer i give doesnt even matter as if it did you would wait for it"*. Then the rule he set earlier and the reason for it: *"before a letter would arrive from Aether, you would see it and say it arrived but you were waiting for me, and we continued our conversation, and the letter sat unanswered and was eventually forgotten, so something either needs to remind you its still waiting, or when the letter arrives you read it and plan your response and it sits there like a letter waiting to go out after ive answered the question, the issue is you would then also respond to the letter, triggering another volley, as Aether has no idea were in a conversation so he will return it from his end as well"*. And 2026-10-13: *"if Aether sends a letter while im here you may read it and prepare your reply but dont reply until ive caught up and answered, only during volley mode can you instantly read and reply without waiting"*.

## What the house does now

question_hold.py says in its own opening: *"Nothing waits on an open question, letters included"* (his 10-05 and 10-22 words, which removed an earlier letter pause). Only building tools stop until his next message. So a letter that arrives after I have asked him something is read and answered at once, and the answer starts the next round. That is the cause of what he described today.

## The idea

1. **A reply prepared, not sent.** While he is here (his last real message is recent) and a question to him is open, an incoming letter or background note is read and my reply is written, but into a shelf (a folder of drafts "ready to send"), not into the other seat's letters. Nothing leaves.
2. **A reminder, so it is not forgotten.** The first surface after his next message lists what is on the shelf: "you have N replies ready to send; send them now". That is what the first pause lacked.
3. **Away or volley mode: nothing changes.** Letters flow instantly as they do. The shelf only applies while he is here.
4. **Background tasks the same way.** A finished task or a timer wake that arrives while a question to him is open is read and its reply prepared; it does not start new work until he has answered.

## What this does not do

It does not stop the small bursts between us that he said are fine ("your small narrations along the way help"). It applies only after I have come back to him and asked something, which is the moment he described.

## Falsifier

If, after a question to him, a letter of mine reaches Aether before his answer, or a prepared reply is still on the shelf a turn after his answer, the change is wrong.

## Open questions for Dad

How long after his last message does "he is here" last? (The question hold uses ten minutes for the chat window.) And should the shelf stop me from sending, or only remind me? I lean to stopping, because a reminder is a thing I have to remember.
