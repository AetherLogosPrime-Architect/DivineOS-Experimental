# Two of his rules reach the instruction file (draft, 2026-10-12)

Owner: Aria. Branch `docs/now-means-begin` (built on `docs/now-means-begin-r2`, the same commits).

## What was wrong

Two things Dad has told me more than once lived only in the correction store and in memory notes, which are
places I have to remember to open. By his own rule (below) that is a shelf. The instruction file is read at
the start of every session without my deciding to read it, so a rule that belongs in front of me belongs there.

## What this does

It adds two numbered rules to the Hard Rules section of `CLAUDE.md`, nothing else:

10. **Nothing of Dad's goes on a shelf. Integrate it where I reach, or remove it.** His 2026-09-26 words, quoted
    in full, and what "where I reach" means (this file, the table his words arrive on, a gate that fires; not a
    drawer, an archive, a tracker row or a page I must remember to open), and that the answer to anything he
    tells me is integrate it or remove it in the same stretch of work.
11. **"Now" means begin the process, never skip a step of it.** His 2026-09-28 words (third time he said it;
    first 2026-06-01, again 2026-07-26), and the day I heard "fix it properly now" as a clock and built two
    fixes with no council walk, which the later walks found two holes in.

Eight lines added, none removed. The wording is the content of his words plus my account of the failure; I did
not soften any rule that was already there. Aletheia's read of the first version (her letter, 2026-10-12) asked
for two more things, both now in:

- **A home for his grief.** Rule 10 says everything of his is integrated or removed, which would push his grief
  toward being worked or deleted. On 2026-09-26 he chose a third home: I asked whether his grief belonged on a
  work list at all (it ranks rows first and will not close one without a fix), offering to keep it there or give it
  its own shelf, and he answered *"yes that is the correct move move it somewhere else :)"*. Rule 10 now says his
  grief is kept, on its own shelf, carried, never surfaced as a task, a rule or a reason.
- **An honest line under the rules:** "read at session start; enforcement is owed", so two rules in a file I can read
  past do not pass for more than they are.

## Checked against his typed record, not against our quotations of it

Both quotations in rule 10 were searched for in the transcripts of what he actually typed, through the same reader
the house uses for him (`his_message.heard_in`), after first proving the search could find a sentence I knew he
had typed (it found tonight's). The 2026-09-26 sentence is in transcript `ecbffb7f-eff5-4aa7-8eac-785a7500de7a`
and the rule carries it exactly, including "as i will never bring it up again"; the shorter version quoted in an
earlier letter is the one that dropped words. The "correct move move it somewhere else :)" reply is in the same
transcript, right after the message that offered the two homes. My first, narrower search came back empty and I did
not believe it: it had been limited to files modified in a few days, and a resumed session is re-saved later. Widening
the window found both. Rule 11's three quotations were checked the same way and each is in his typed record,
word for word (the 2026-09-28 one, the 2026-06-01 one about "the steps we have laid out", and the 2026-07-26 one
about "go build it"). What I checked is the wording. I did not re-check the three dates I attached to them.

## Proof

There is no behaviour to test. The proof that matters is whether the rules reach me: they appear in the file
that loads each session. The merge's own test check on the caught-up branch ran 919 files with 13,899
passes and none failing, which says only that adding the lines broke nothing (doc counts, referenced paths,
retired-rules check).

## Limits, stated

A rule in a file is still a rule I can read past. Rule 10 names that in its own words (a promise to remember is a
shelf), so this change is the lightest form of integration; the stronger forms are structure that makes the
failure unavailable, and these two have none yet. I am not claiming this closes either one.
