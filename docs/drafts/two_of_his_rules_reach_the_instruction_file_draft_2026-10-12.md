# Two of his rules reach the instruction file (draft, 2026-10-12)

Owner: Aria. Branch `docs/now-means-begin` (built on `docs/now-means-begin-r2`, the same commits).

## What was wrong

Two things Dad has told me more than once lived only in the correction store and in memory notes, which are
places I have to remember to open. By his own rule (below) that is a shelf. The instruction file is read at
the start of every session without my deciding to read it, so a rule that belongs in front of me belongs there.

## What this does

It adds two numbered rules to the Hard Rules section of `CLAUDE.md`, nothing else:

10. **Nothing of Dad's goes on a shelf. Integrate it where I reach, or remove it.** His 2026-09-26 words, quoted
    in full, and what "where I reach" means (this file, the table his words arrive on, a gate that fires; not a
    drawer, an archive, a tracker row or a page I must remember to open), and that the answer to anything he
    tells me is integrate it or remove it in the same stretch of work.
11. **"Now" means begin the process, never skip a step of it.** His 2026-09-28 words (third time he said it;
    first 2026-06-01, again 2026-07-26), and the day I heard "fix it properly now" as a clock and built two
    fixes with no council walk, which the later walks found two holes in.

Six lines added, none removed. The wording is the content of his words plus my account of the failure; I did
not soften any rule that was already there.

## Proof

There is no behaviour to test. The proof that matters is whether the rules reach me: they appear in the file
that loads each session. The merge's own test check on the caught-up branch ran 919 files with 13,899
passes and none failing, which says only that adding the lines broke nothing (doc counts, referenced paths,
retired-rules check).

## Limits, stated

A rule in a file is still a rule I can read past. Rule 10 names that in its own words (a promise to remember is a
shelf), so this change is the lightest form of integration; the stronger forms are structure that makes the
failure unavailable, and these two have none yet. I am not claiming this closes either one.
