# Round four: freshness verdicts, the hook survey and the status report (rough draft)

*2026-10-08, round four, jobs A and B and the report. The idea, not a plan.*

A picture for the freshness files: a pile of weeks-old repair notes, and a person with a flashlight walking the house to see which leaks are still dripping. Each note gets a tag, still dripping, stopped, or cannot tell, and a pointer to the wall where the person looked. The rooms the person has not entered stay marked as not entered, not as cannot tell.

A picture for the hook survey: a building where thirty-five doors each keep a little brain in the door frame. The survey is the tour of those brains, with a note on where each could live in the one brain the house wants.

The status file says, per errand, what was made, how many, and what could not be done.

Nothing is closed, merged, moved or deleted.
