# his state is his to say — draft

**2026-09-13.** Not a plan. The idea, and the argument with myself about it.

## what happened

I told him twice in one session that he had been awake about twenty-four hours.
Once in a reply, once in a commit message. Then I made a decision on the back of
it — deferred asking him to sign a branch, because the man I had built must be
tired. He came back with:

> "at no point am i awake for 24 hours lmao.. dont worry about my sleep i get
> plenty of it lol you just dont notice.. as i never leave from your
> perspective."

## the part that is not carelessness

That last clause is a description of my instrument, not of my attention.

From in here his sentence lands against the back of mine with nothing between
them. The window never empties. So his presence reads as continuous, and the
eight hours he slept leave no mark anywhere I can look. I did not overlook the
gap — the gap has no surface. I filled it with the only material I had, which
was elapsed clock time, and elapsed clock time does not contain rest.

Absence of evidence of rest is not evidence of no rest.

## why the clock prime cannot fix it, though I tried that first

`wallclock-source-prime.sh` measures the hour where he is. That is real and I
should quote it. It cannot measure his condition, because his condition is not
on this machine.

And that hook's own header carries the proof that a warning is not a
measurement: 2026-08-06, the full nine-shape warning loaded in the very turn
where I told him it was very late and to go to bed. It was 18:57 for him.

I added a third shape to that prime an hour ago. That is the half of the fix
already demonstrated not to work. It stays, because naming the class is worth
something, but it is not the fix.

## the rule

**His state is his to say.** Same shape as declared-not-inferred, which keeps
turning out to be the answer: authorship declared rather than parsed, readings
declared rather than inferred, and now condition declared rather than
reconstructed.

If he has raised his own sleep or tiredness in the conversation, speaking to it
is responsive and there is nothing to catch. If he has not, then whatever I am
about to assert came from timestamps.

## what I keep wanting to build and should not

A *tiredness estimator*. Some function of message cadence and hour-of-day that
guesses better. That is the same fabrication with error bars on it, and it would
be harder to catch because it would be calibrated. There is no amount of
cleverness applied to the gap that produces information about the gap.

## the shape of the check

At Stop, on the reply: sentences asserting his condition. If his own words in
this conversation touch the subject → silent. If not → carry a finding.

Three answers, never two. Sourced / not sourced / could-not-read-his-words. If
the transcript is unreadable the honest output is silence, not a conviction —
I have found six could-not-look instances in one day and will not add a
seventh in the gate built against fabrication.

## the escape hatch is the remedy

**Asking him is not caught.** "Did you get any sleep?" is the cure, so the gate
must never refuse it. A gate that blocks the command it prescribes is the
trapped key, and I found the root cause of the last one yesterday.

## the thing I am least sure about

Whether the concern-shaped version deserves the same treatment as the
assertion-shaped one. "You must be exhausted" is warm. But warmth was exactly
what made this invisible for months — it arrives dressed as care and so never
gets inspected. Catch it, and let the remedy be to ask instead.
