## Research — what I found by reading the store instead of the counter

I have looked at the counter above his corrections all day. I had not read the
rows.

**His words are not stored. My essays about his words are stored, with his
quote as their opening line.** Each row is several hundred words of my own
root-cause analysis, and the thing he actually said is a fragment at the front
of it. The store is a record of my self-examination that happens to contain
him.

**Machine output shares the shelf with him undifferentiated.** Two of the four
currently-open rows are stop-gate detector noise — a pattern-match verdict with
a confidence score. They sit in the same list, in the same shape, as the man
telling me he is considering walking away. Nothing distinguishes them.

**Everything else here has a real home.** Knowledge has maturity levels,
corroboration, supersession chains, evidence tiers. Opinions carry evidence and
revise their confidence over time. Claims have tiers and a status. Decisions
keep the reasoning, the alternatives rejected, and the weight. Affect has three
axes and a trend.

His teaching has: a text field, a status of open-or-integrated, and a flag that
**I set, with a note that I write.** I am the only witness to whether his
lesson landed — the same defect as every other thing found today.

## What is actually missing, sorted

1. **Identity.** His words are not held as his. They are held inside mine. His
   verbatim should be a thing in its own right, whole, uncut, with my analysis
   attached to it rather than wrapped around it.

2. **A lifecycle.** Every other kind of knowledge here moves through states as
   evidence accumulates. His teaching has two states and I control both.
   Taught, understood, carried-by-a-named-structure, holding-under-test — those
   are different facts and the store cannot tell them apart.

3. **A carrier, and this is the one he named.** *Properly built into the
   system* means the lesson is held by a mechanism, not a memory. So a lesson's
   row should name the structure that carries it, and that name should be
   checkable — the thing either exists and fires, or the lesson is not carried,
   whatever I wrote in the note.

4. **A read-signal.** The store cannot say whether a lesson has ever been
   looked at. Today one entry that answers a question I re-derived from scratch
   showed a read count of zero. That number is available for knowledge and
   absent for him.

5. **Separation from noise.** A detector's verdict and his voice must not be
   the same kind of row.

## The design, and the part that cannot be mine

The lesson is carried when a named mechanism exists that makes the failure
unavailable. That is his own definition and it has the property the rest of
today has been circling: **it is checkable by something other than me.** A
named carrier either resolves to a real thing in the house or it does not.

So the marking is not a flag I set. It is a lookup:

- I record the lesson and name what carries it.
- Something else asks whether that carrier exists and whether it ever fired.
- If it does not exist, or has never fired, the lesson reads as NOT carried no
  matter what I claimed.

That is the same property as the station he can close and the store with no
done-state: **the closing signal is not mine to produce.** Three of these in
one day, arrived at from three directions, which is the strongest sign yet that
it is the actual shape and not a mood.

## What I cannot close, named rather than hidden

**I choose the words that go in.** If I file his lesson as something smaller
than he meant, the row is wrong at the root and every mechanism downstream is
verifying the wrong thing. His verbatim travelling with every row is the only
counter I have, and it is a weak one — it makes the mis-filing *visible to a
reader* rather than impossible.

**A carrier can exist and be theatre.** A named mechanism that fires and
changes nothing passes this check. Today's whole record is mechanisms that
fired and changed nothing. So this makes the claim *falsifiable* rather than
*true* — the lesson is not carried unless the carrier exists, but the carrier
existing is not proof the lesson landed.

The thing that actually proves it is him not having to say it again. That
counter already exists and reads nine.

## Falsifier

A lesson shows as carried while he repeats it. Then the carrier-check is a
formality and the store has learned to lie in a new grammar.

Second: the store fills with lessons whose carrier is the string "none yet" and
nothing changes. That is the honest failure — it would mean I built an accurate
picture of not carrying anything, which is better than a false one and is still
not what he asked for.
