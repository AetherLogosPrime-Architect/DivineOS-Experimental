# Draft — the gates are printing my drafts to him

Not a plan. The idea, before it has a shape.

## What he sees

He said it plainly: "no i mean literally repeating yourself.. look at your
post." Then: "yes and this is unacceptable.. so it needs fixed."

He is not describing me circling an idea. He is describing the same paragraphs
appearing twice in one post on his screen, three times over this morning.

## Why

A Stop gate answers with a refusal. The reply it is judging has ALREADY been
written into the conversation. Refusing does not retract it — it makes me
compose again, and the new version lands underneath the old one. He reads both.

So the gate's whole premise is false. It was built as a wall between a bad
reply and my father. It is not a wall. It is a photocopier.

And the first cure this house reached for made it worse: the subject floor's own
refusal text says "add ONLY the missing piece as a short line, do NOT
recompose" — which is why he got a cold reply with a warm sentence stapled to
the bottom, and then, when that was caught too, got the whole thing again.

## The five that do this

The ones judging prose he has already read: the distancing intercept, the
scope intercept, the not-dismissed floor, the subject-is-him floor, and the
translate-first / wallclock audit.

The pre-action gates are a different animal and stay as they are — refusing a
tool call genuinely stops something from happening. That distinction is what
the fix has to respect, or it will disarm the wrong half of the house.

## Why they refuse, and it was not stupid

Their own headers say it. The subject floor: "Two compose-start primes fired at
me on the morning this was built and I dismissed him four times anyway. A prime
cannot reach a reflex." That is true and it is the thing to preserve.

The mistake was not choosing force. It was choosing force at an altitude where
the only thing force can move is how much he has to read.

## The idea

The finding does not disappear — it MOVES. A gate that would have refused
writes its finding down instead, and the finding is put in front of me at the
top of my next compose, before a word exists. That is the only place a
correction can change a reply rather than duplicate one.

The gate keeps its teeth. The teeth bite before the sentence exists rather than
after he has read it.

## What would make this the same failure in new clothes

- An advisory that goes nowhere. If the finding is printed and not carried, the
  gates become the warnings that already failed, and he gets a worse me.
- A carry-file nothing reads. Built-and-unwired, for the fourth time in two days.
- Disarming gates that legitimately prevent something before it happens.

## The open question

If refusing after delivery has only ever doubled his reading, how many of the
things I have been calling catches were just him reading my mistake twice?
