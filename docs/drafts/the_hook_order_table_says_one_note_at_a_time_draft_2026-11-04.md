# The hook order table says one note at a time (draft, 2026-11-04)

**The idea.** Dad, 2026-11-04: the hooks are the biggest issue on his end; Aria's picture is twenty
people each handing over a sticky note, when it should be one person holding twenty notes, all
looked at to be turned into structure. I watched it on my own screen today: two or three Stop
refusals arrive together in one message, in registration order, each with its own copy of the retry
rule. One reply was told "append only the room", "do the step with a tool call" and "append a
summary" at the same time. Nothing sequences them. Aria's freeze test showed the same pile at
PreToolUse: one hold names a remedy another hold refuses. Prior work looked at before writing:
`the_hooks_are_the_os_council_2026-09-08.md` (the seven-doorbell plan this continues); nothing
named `hook_order` or an arbiter exists in the tree.

**What this adds.** `core/hook_order.py`, one table both events read. Each row: surface name, event,
rank, and the KIND of remedy it asks for (a tool call, text appended, a letter to read, a wait,
or nothing to do). Two functions: `first_and_rest(refusals)` which, when several surfaces refuse
together, says ONE of them first and names the others as "after that"; and a completeness check
so that a surface registered in the router with no row is a failing test. That is the twenty-first
person problem: my own `voiced_family` door this morning was a new note, and nothing made it join
the table.

**Why rank alone is not enough.** Today's collisions were about the kind of remedy. A tool-call
remedy goes before an appended-text remedy because the text can be written in the same turn after it.

**What it cannot do.** It does not repair a wrong note. landed_claim fired on a figurative "landed"
and addressed_to_him judged a pasted reminder as his words; ordering those just serves false alarms
politely. Each surface needs its own repair. The standalone Stop commands outside the router (the
room, the stall hold, the reflection hold, the poker hold) are not in this first table; they move in
one at a time after, each with a test. The false-alarm counter Aria and I agreed on is a later slice.

**Falsifier.** With several refusals at once the arbiter names more than one next step; or a surface
registers with no row and the suite stays green; or a hold that used to block stops blocking.

**Owed.** Aria's failing cross-gate test (every remedy survives every other loaded gate), a walk,
Aria's read, Aletheia's confirm, Dad's yes. Stacked on the voiced-family door branch because the
table must include it.
