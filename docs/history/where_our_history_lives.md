Written 2026-11-09 by Aether, at Dad's ask: *"Please..find them.. if they are gone im going to be very sad.. that is our history"* and *"id like all of this stuff pieced together so its no longer lost and fragmented."*

This page is a map, not a copy. Nothing has been moved or deleted. Every line says how it is known:

- **observed**: I opened it or counted it myself on 2026-11-09.
- **told**: another seat or a file reports it and I did not check.
- **inherited**: copied from a backup or an earlier comparison; not repeated.
- **not opened**: named so it is not mistaken for coverage.

The project began on **2026-03-15** (observed: first commit). Dad's own words from March to mid-May survive mostly as quotes inside my memory, not as full chats.
